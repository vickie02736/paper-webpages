## Teaching review


Each page has a source-specific prerequisite map, method-purpose flow, paired evidence explanation and a self-check. These additions address the before-audit, preserve experimental conditions and keep limits visible. They are not a generic abstract rewrite. Controls reveal meaningful method/research layers; original scientific controls and data remain.

- mof-stability-three-axes: Check: why does a TGA plateau not establish intact pores? Answer: TGA measures net mass change. Rearrangement or pore collapse can occur without a corresponding large loss of matter. Diffraction and pore measurements, with heating conditions, are still needed.

- mof-kinetic-stability: Check: does higher static ammonia uptake guarantee a better protective filter? Answer: No. A filter must delay outlet breakthrough at the target concentration, humidity and flow rate; degradation and use conditions also matter. Pure-gas equilibrium capacity cannot replace that breakthrough test.

- water-stable-mof-design: Check: do retained diffraction peaks after adding a water-repelling layer complete optimization? Answer: No. Peaks support retained order; pore access, capacity and selectivity in the target feed, and regeneration still need checking. Structural protection and capture performance require separate evidence.

- hydrolytic-stability-benchmark: Check: does nearly 100% corrected mass mean every pore remains usable? Answer: No. Matter can remain while connections or pore shapes change, or entrances become blocked. Gas sorption and diffraction are separate evidence; missing CO₂ data must not be filled with zero.

- water-dual-impact: Check: if all levels shift upward by 10 kJ/mol, does the local barrier change? Answer: No. This is a hypothetical change of common reference: 12.8 − (−15.2) is still 28.0. The start-to-peak difference is unchanged. A level becoming positive or negative alone does not establish a barrierless reaction.

Human learning outcomes have not been tested. The review establishes explanatory coverage and evidence traceability, not comprehension.
