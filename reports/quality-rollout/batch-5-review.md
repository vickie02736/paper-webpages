## Teaching review


Each page has a source-specific prerequisite map, method-purpose flow, paired evidence explanation and a self-check. These additions address the before-audit, preserve experimental conditions and keep limits visible. They are not a generic abstract rewrite. Controls reveal meaningful method/research layers; original scientific controls and data remain.

- dga-mil101-europium: Check: why is β not 1.8 when removal is 90% versus 50%? Answer: Because D also divides by each residual liquid fraction. Removing 90% leaves 10%, while removing 50% leaves 50%; calculate 9 and 1 first, then divide to obtain 9. Conditions must match, and these values are teaching assumptions.

- mil101-sba15-lu-y: Check: does a larger Sips Qₘ with high R² prove the sorbent holds more? Answer: No. It is a model estimate of a limit requiring near-saturation data and model checking. Agreement within the observed range does not independently validate a distant asymptote.

- dtpa-mil101-ammonium: Check: does a small ammonium-nitrogen concentration change prove no ammonium on the solid? Answer: No. The percentage must be read with the large background, measurement uncertainty and measurements of both phases. Site occupancy or product composition needs relevant compositional analysis; a small concentration difference is insufficient.

- hkust1-lutetium: Check: can high trace removal validate a capacity of 512.82 mg/g? Answer: No. This experiment supplies at most 0.4 mg/g and never approaches that capacity scale. The 512.82 mg/g value is a Langmuir parameter from another concentration series and must be judged using its data and model.

- boroxine-mof-ree: Check: do Yb₂O₃ diffraction peaks establish purity and total recovery? Answer: No. Peaks support that crystalline phase, but do not quantify every trace impurity or close the feed-to-product Yb balance. Product elemental analysis, yield and Yb in mother liquor and washes are needed.

Human learning outcomes have not been tested. The review establishes explanatory coverage and evidence traceability, not comprehension.
