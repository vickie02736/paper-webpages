## Teaching review


Each page has a source-specific prerequisite map, method-purpose flow, paired evidence explanation and a self-check. These additions address the before-audit, preserve experimental conditions and keep limits visible. They are not a generic abstract rewrite. Controls reveal meaningful method/research layers; original scientific controls and data remain.

- uio66-dangling-linkers: Check: does recovery after drying prove an unchanged wet structure for a year? Answer: No. SI Fig. S3 measures a sample redried after a year of hydrated storage. It supports recovery after drying, not unchanged wet-state connections, leaching or adsorption performance throughout storage.

- ree-mof-critical-review: Check: does a good pseudo-second-order fit alone prove chemical coordination? Answer: No. It describes a macroscopic time curve, and different processes can fit similarly. Relevant spectroscopy, composition and controls must constrain the mechanism and test precipitation or material change.

- ree-mof-targeted-review: Check: does strong REE/iron separation establish high-purity neodymium? Answer: No. It may only enrich REEs as a group. Nd separation from coexisting REEs, subsequent release and product composition still need testing, with target losses tracked at each step.

- ree-porous-framework-review: Check: does covalent bonding make every COF resistant to strong acid and base? Answer: No. Linkage chemistry changes response to water, acid and base; boron–oxygen linkages can hydrolyze, for example. Check the actual linkage, treatment, duration and post-treatment structure and function.

- ree-mof-protocol: Check: why do four high-removal loadings not prove four successful regenerations? Answer: Because the adsorbed REEs were not removed between loadings. This does not test desorption, regenerant damage, post-regeneration selectivity or final product purity.

Human learning outcomes have not been tested. The review establishes explanatory coverage and evidence traceability, not comprehension.
