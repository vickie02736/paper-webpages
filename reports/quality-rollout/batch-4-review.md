## Teaching review


Each page has a source-specific prerequisite map, method-purpose flow, paired evidence explanation and a self-check. These additions address the before-audit, preserve experimental conditions and keep limits visible. They are not a generic abstract rewrite. Controls reveal meaningful method/research layers; original scientific controls and data remain.

- functionalized-cr-mil101: Check: does the highest Gd uptake make PMIDA best for every tested REE? Answer: No. Table 2 gives higher DETA screening uptake for La, Ce and Nd, while PMIDA is higher for Sm and Gd. Read the actual ion and experiment rather than extending the abstract’s general ordering to every column.

- acrylic-mil101-scandium: Check: can Sc/La Sel = 2.31 mean 2.31-fold preference or 2.31% recovery? Answer: Neither. It is the logarithm of a distribution-ratio ratio, not a direct ratio or percentage. Its positive sign favors Sc under those conditions, but the unspecified base prevents an exact fold value and it does not establish product purity.

- dual-functional-uio66-gd: Check: does a 4.94-fold fitted capacity increase imply 4.94-fold faster uptake? Answer: No. Capacity compares endpoint amounts; kinetics compares progress toward them. The article’s text reports slower equilibration for the ED material, with the kinetic-figure labeling issue retained.

- uio66-zif8-ree: Check: does exceeding U6N establish superiority over both parents? Answer: No. The SI single-ion Langmuir tables give ZIF-8 higher capacity parameters for all four REEs than the selected core–shell material. Acid response, capacity and reuse require separate comparisons with both baselines.

- organophosphorus-mil101: Check: why measure phosphorus groups and solution P when diffraction peaks remain? Answer: Diffraction mainly probes the ordered solid support. Functional molecules can be lost without erasing all support peaks. Spectra, liquid composition and next-run uptake add evidence about functional retention.

Human learning outcomes have not been tested. The review establishes explanatory coverage and evidence traceability, not comprehension.
