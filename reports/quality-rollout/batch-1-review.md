## Teaching review


Each page has a source-specific prerequisite map, method-purpose flow, paired evidence explanation and a self-check. These additions address the before-audit, preserve experimental conditions and keep limits visible. They are not a generic abstract rewrite. Controls reveal meaningful method/research layers; original scientific controls and data remain.

- hydrolytic-stability-grading: Check: why is PXRD alone insufficient for 6A? Answer: 6 only identifies boiling-water exposure. A additionally requires retained order and porosity; without the pore measurement the letter lacks evidence. Duration must still be reported.

- mof-hydrolysis-atomistic: Check: does reaction at 450 ps in a biased trajectory mean failure after 450 ps? Answer: No. Bias changes event timing, the event is local, and bulk failure has not been defined. Unbiased rates, propagation and functional loss require additional evidence.

- mil100-water-matrices: Check: are retained PXRD peaks, lower BET area and falling pH contradictory? Answer: No. They examine ordered regions, probe-accessible surface and solution chemistry. Ordered material may remain while pore accessibility and liquid composition change.

- cmpo-mil101-ree: Check: why can 12.46 mg/g and 3.2 not directly rank the same performance? Answer: The first is a fitted single-ion capacity limit; the second is a partitioning preference in a specific mixture. Their objects, units and conditions differ; decide whether capacity or separation is the target.

- defect-phosphate-uio66-yb: Check: do lower area and larger reported pore width prove more total porosity? Answer: No. Area, pore width and total pore volume are different quantities; total volume also decreases here. Reported width depends on measurement/model choices and is not an exact entry window for hydrated Yb.

Human learning outcomes have not been tested. The review establishes explanatory coverage and evidence traceability, not comprehension.
