To explore the maps visually, open a file in [FreeMind](https://freemind.sourceforge.io/) or Freeplane. The homepage presents each paper once under its primary theme. Existing map references now carry `paperId` and `primaryThemeId` attributes so a paper can be read in several contexts without becoming several catalogue records.


The AI/ML theme is organised by research topic, not conference prestige or acceptance status. Conference names, publication years and preprint labels remain paper-level metadata. FrontierCS 2 belongs under Evaluation & Reliability → Research & Engineering Agent Benchmarks; a benchmark for agents is distinct from a method for building agents. The historical roadmap filename remains unchanged to preserve existing links.

## MOF + AI reading routes

The homepage adds three bilingual routes in `researchRoutes`: high-throughput MOF screening and inverse design; MOF machine-learning potentials and simulation; and automated MOF experiments. Each stage references existing paper IDs. These curated routes complement the 34 preserved conceptual routes in these maps.

MOF has explicit Stability and Rare-Earth Adsorption & Separation subcategories. Non-MOF comparisons such as LanM remain available in the MOF reading collection while their primary catalogue home is general chemistry/materials. Existing filenames, source URLs and explanatory map nodes remain intact.
