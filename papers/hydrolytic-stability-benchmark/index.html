MOF 的连接点和连接体围出孔道。规则排列可以留下衍射峰，但气体未必还能进入全部孔；回收的固体也可能夹带水。本文的多指标设计正是为了避免一种读数替所有性质作答。MOF junctions and linkers enclose pores. Ordered packing can retain diffraction peaks even when not all pores remain accessible; recovered solids may also contain water. Multiple measurements prevent one reading from standing in for all these properties.

有序排列Ordered packing

PXRD 即粉末 X 射线衍射：测散射强度随角度的变化。本研究比较峰的积分强度；它不是直接称出“完整晶体”的质量比例。PXRD, powder X-ray diffraction, measures scattered intensity versus angle. This study compares integrated peak intensity; that does not directly weigh the fraction of intact crystals.

可达孔道Accessible pores

气体吸附测气体能进入并留住多少。BET 分析给出每克样品可达表面积；CO₂ 吸附量则直接针对特定气体和条件。Gas adsorption measures entry and retention. BET analysis estimates accessible surface area per gram; CO₂ uptake addresses a specific gas and set of conditions.

校正质量Corrected mass

TGA 即热重分析：加热并称重，以规定平台估计除去溶剂后的质量分数。原样和回收样品都要校正，才有同类分母。TGA, thermogravimetric analysis, heats and weighs the sample. A specified plateau estimates its solvent-free mass fraction. Both pristine and recovered samples need correction for a like-for-like denominator.

读完要能解释为什么 47.4 mg 回收固体可以对应约 98% 的校正质量保留，而不是框架“生长了 58%”。You should be able to explain why 47.4 mg recovered solid can correspond to about 98% corrected mass retention, rather than 58% framework growth.

01 · 先看处理历史Start with treatment history

一次测试，包含几种应力One test includes several stresses

这条流程测到了哪些共同历史？What combined history does this workflow test?

  1. 设定相同水暴露Set a common water exposure

    30 mg 样品加入 5 mL 去离子水，常温 24 h。这样统一液固用量与时间，但并不统一各样品的缺陷、粒径或合成历史。Expose 30 mg to 5 mL deionized water for 24 h at ambient temperature. This aligns liquid-to-solid amount and time, not defects, particle size or synthesis history.

  2. 回收与干燥Recover and dry

    过滤并弃去滤液，空气干燥 72 h 后称重。滤液被弃去意味着这套数据没有直接清点所有已进入水中的组分。Filter, discard the filtrate, air-dry for 72 h and weigh. Discarding the filtrate means this dataset does not directly count every component that entered the water.

  3. 按测量要求前处理Prepare for each measurement

    气体吸附前另有 120 °C 真空 15 h 的活化。孔隙结果因此包含浸水、干燥与活化的影响，不能全归给单一断键反应。Gas sorption includes activation at 120 °C under vacuum for 15 h. Pore results therefore include immersion, drying and activation, not solely one bond-cleavage reaction.

这是一套统一处理后的比较，不是实时追踪浸水中的每一步。三批 UiO-66 的重复性也不是所有材料的误差条。This is comparison after a common treatment, not real-time tracking of every immersion step. Repeatability from three UiO-66 batches is not an error bar for every material.

Their results separate three questions: is crystalline order retained, are pores accessible, and how much solid remains? Water stability requires all three lines of evidence.

为什么干燥方式会改变结论？Why can drying change the conclusion?

水解会改变金属–配体连接；溶剂离开孔道时产生的毛细应力也可能使框架坍塌。这套测试把两者的最终影响一起测了出来，没有在浸水阶段原位分开它们。作者在 §3.2 特别讨论 UiO-67 的溶剂置换和再生研究，因此不能把干燥后的失孔一律当成已被单独证明的水解机制。Hydrolysis changes metal–linker connectivity, while capillary stress during solvent removal can also collapse a framework. This workflow measures their combined final effects without separating them in situ during immersion. Article §3.2 discusses solvent exchange and regeneration of UiO-67; a loss of porosity after drying therefore does not uniquely establish hydrolysis.

02 · 把“稳定”拆开Separate the meanings of stability

三个指标不能互相替代Three measures that cannot replace one another

PXRD · 长程有序Long-range order

扣除背景后，比较衍射峰总积分强度。必须同时查看峰位和相变；新相也可能给出更强的峰。Compare integrated peak intensity after background subtraction. Peak positions and phase changes still matter: a new phase may diffract more strongly.

RXRD = Iafter / Ibefore × 100%

这是相对强度指标，不是绝对结晶相质量分数。A relative intensity measure, not an absolute crystalline mass fraction.

气体吸附 · 可用孔道Gas sorption · Accessible pores

N₂ 等温线给出 BET 比表面积。有些超微孔材料对低温 N₂ 的可及性很低，再用 CO₂ 检查气体容量。N₂ isotherms yield BET surface area. Some ultramicroporous materials have little accessibility to cryogenic N₂, so CO₂ provides an additional uptake probe.

RBET = Aafter / Abefore × 100%

CO₂ 容量保留率与 BET 面积保留率分别展示，不混成同一指标。CO₂ uptake retention and BET area retention are displayed separately.

TGA · 校正后的固体质量Corrected solid mass

吸水、溶剂交换会改变称量结果。用 TGA 的无溶剂平台质量分数 w 校正回收质量 m，再与初始样品比较。Water uptake and solvent exchange change the weighed mass. Correct recovered mass m using the solvent-free plateau fraction w from TGA, then compare with the pristine sample.

Rmass = (mw)after / (mw)before × 100%

留下固体不代表原框架或原始化学组成全部保留。Recovered solid does not establish complete preservation of the original framework or composition.

公式为 SI S4–S5 原定义的简写；w 在计算中用 0–1 的质量分数。数值大于 100% 不截断，也不自动解释为“变得更稳定”。Equations abbreviate the definitions in SI S4–S5; w is a mass fraction between 0 and 1. Values above 100% are retained and do not automatically mean improved stability.

04 · 一个容易误读的称量An easy mass-balance trap

回收 47.4 mg，不等于框架增重 58%Recovering 47.4 mg does not mean 58% more framework

先算两边的无溶剂等效质量，再相除First calculate solvent-free equivalent mass on both sides

原样：30 mg 的 82%Pristine: 82% of 30 mg

30 × 0.82 = 24.60 mg。分母是原样按 TGA 平台换算的无溶剂等效质量，不是直接使用 30 mg。30 × 0.82 = 24.60 mg. The denominator is the pristine solvent-free equivalent mass derived using the TGA plateau, not simply 30 mg.

回收：47.40 mg 的 51%Recovered: 51% of 47.40 mg

47.40 × 0.51 = 24.174 mg。回收总质量较大，但其中用于校正的比例较小。两者必须一起读。47.40 × 0.51 = 24.174 mg. The recovered total mass is larger but its correction fraction is smaller. Both quantities must be read together.

Rₘ = (m后 × w后) ÷ (m前 × w前) × 100% ≈ 98.27%Rₘ = (m_after × w_after) ÷ (m_before × w_before) × 100% ≈ 98.27%

m 是称量质量（mg），w 是 TGA 无溶剂平台质量分数（用 0–1 小数）。本式是按 SI 数值复算；mg 相消。它依赖平台能代表所定义无溶剂质量的假设。m is weighed mass in mg; w is the TGA solvent-free plateau fraction expressed from 0 to 1. This recalculates SI values; mg cancels. It relies on the plateau representing the defined solvent-free mass.

98.27% 是本方法的校正质量保留率，既不是有序晶体比例，也不是所有金属和连接体的完整物料衡算。滤液未被这条流程定量。98.27% is corrected mass retention under this method, not the ordered-crystal fraction or a complete metal-and-linker mass balance. This workflow did not quantify the filtrate.

出处：SI 表 S3 的 MIL-101(Cr) 行（30、47.40、82、51、98.27）；正文质量评价定义。Source: MIL-101(Cr) row in SI Table S3 (30, 47.40, 82, 51, 98.27); main mass-retention definition.

自测：校正质量接近 100%，能说明孔道全部可用吗？Check: does nearly 100% corrected mass mean every pore remains usable?

不能。质量可以留下，但连接或孔形改变、孔口堵塞。需要另看气体吸附与衍射；缺失的 CO₂ 数据也不能填成零。No. Matter can remain while connections or pore shapes change, or entrances become blocked. Gas sorption and diffraction are separate evidence; missing CO₂ data must not be filled with zero.

MIL-101(Cr) 的原始称量是 30 mg，浸水后回收 47.40 mg。但 TGA 无溶剂平台质量分数由 82% 变为 51%。把两者一起算，质量保留率约为 98.27%。MIL-101(Cr) starts at 30 mg and yields 47.40 mg after treatment. Its TGA solvent-free plateau fraction changes from 82% to 51%. Combining both measurements gives about 98.27% mass retention.

质量校正没有测定滤液中的金属或配体，也不能辨认留下的每种化学物种。超过 100% 的质量结果仍需组成证据解释。The correction does not measure metal or linker in the filtrate and cannot identify every retained chemical species. Mass values above 100% still need compositional evidence.

同一系列：UiO-66 → 67 → 68Within a series: UiO-66 → 67 → 68

相对结晶度为 64%、8%、14%；BET 保留率为 90.7%、2.2%、3.6%。作者联系到更长配体、柔性与干燥毛细应力。它支持在本流程下比较这些批次，不证明配体长度是唯一变量。Relative crystallinity is 64%, 8% and 14%; BET retention is 90.7%, 2.2% and 3.6%. The authors invoke a hydrophobic environment, coordination protection and interpenetration. These are mechanistic interpretations rather than a causal isolation of each variable.

论文没有测量 REE 吸附。用于稀土回收的研究启发是：在实际 pH、盐、缓冲液和再生液中，同时检查骨架变化、溶出与竞争吸附。这里的去离子水结果不能直接代替那些验证。This paper does not measure REE adsorption. Its implication for rare-earth recovery is to check framework changes, leaching and competitive adsorption under the actual pH, salts, buffers and regenerants. Results in deionized water cannot replace those tests.

研究者阅读：接下来最需要补什么？Research reading: what would strengthen the evidence?

以下是讲解者分析：测量滤液中的金属和配体可完善质量闭合；浸水过程中的原位结构表征可区分水解与后续干燥损伤；多批次、多时间点、湿干循环以及应用液体对照可测试结果的稳健性。对于极低的初始 BET 值，应同时报告绝对值和适当气体探针，而不是只比较百分比。这些不是本论文已经完成的额外实验。Explainer analysis: measuring dissolved metals and linkers would improve mass closure; in-situ structure measurements during immersion would distinguish hydrolysis from later drying damage. Multiple batches, exposure times, wet–dry cycles and application-specific liquids would test robustness.
