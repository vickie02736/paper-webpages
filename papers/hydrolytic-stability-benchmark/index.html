01 · 先看处理历史Start with treatment history

一次测试，包含几种应力One test includes several stresses

  1. 取样Weigh每种材料 30 mg；同时表征原始样品。Use 30 mg per material and characterize a pristine sample.
  2. 浸水Immerse加入 5 mL 去离子水，常温放置 24 h。Add 5 mL deionized water and expose for 24 h at ambient temperature.
  3. 回收并干燥Recover and dry过滤，弃去滤液，空气中干燥 72 h 后称重。Filter, discard the filtrate, air-dry for 72 h and weigh.
  4. 交叉表征Cross-characterizePXRD、TGA、FTIR；气体吸附前在 120 °C 真空活化 15 h。Measure PXRD, TGA and FTIR; activate at 120 °C under vacuum for 15 h before gas sorption.

出处：正文 §2.1–2.5；SI S4–S5。气体探针为 77 K N₂，以及部分材料的 298 K CO₂（保留率按 1 bar 容量）。Source: article §§2.1–2.5; SI S4–S5. Gas probes are N₂ at 77 K and, for selected materials, CO₂ at 298 K (retention of uptake at 1 bar).

为什么干燥方式会改变结论？Why can drying change the conclusion?

水解会改变金属–配体连接；溶剂离开孔道时产生的毛细应力也可能使框架坍塌。这套测试把两者的最终影响一起测了出来，没有在浸水阶段原位分开它们。作者在 §3.2 特别讨论 UiO-67 的溶剂置换和再生研究，因此不能把干燥后的失孔一律当成已被单独证明的水解机制。Hydrolysis changes metal–linker connectivity, while capillary stress during solvent removal can also collapse a framework. This workflow measures their combined final effects without separating them in situ during immersion. Article §3.2 discusses solvent exchange and regeneration of UiO-67; a loss of porosity after drying therefore does not uniquely establish hydrolysis.

02 · 把“稳定”拆开Separate the meanings of stability

三个指标不能互相替代Three measures that cannot replace one another

PXRD · 长程有序Long-range order

扣除背景后，比较衍射峰总积分强度。必须同时查看峰位和相变；新相也可能给出更强的峰。Compare integrated peak intensity after background subtraction. Peak positions and phase changes still matter: a new phase may diffract more strongly.

RXRD = Iafter / Ibefore × 100%

这是相对强度指标，不是绝对结晶相质量分数。A relative intensity measure, not an absolute crystalline mass fraction.

气体吸附 · 可用孔道Gas sorption · Accessible pores

N₂ 等温线给出 BET 比表面积。有些超微孔材料对低温 N₂ 的可及性很低，再用 CO₂ 检查气体容量。N₂ isotherms yield BET surface area. Some ultramicroporous materials have little accessibility to cryogenic N₂, so CO₂ provides an additional uptake probe.

RBET = Aafter / Abefore × 100%

CO₂ 容量保留率与 BET 面积保留率分别展示，不混成同一指标。CO₂ uptake retention and BET area retention are displayed separately.

TGA · 校正后的固体质量Corrected solid mass

吸水、溶剂交换会改变称量结果。用 TGA 的无溶剂平台质量分数 w 校正回收质量 m，再与初始样品比较。Water uptake and solvent exchange change the weighed mass. Correct recovered mass m using the solvent-free plateau fraction w from TGA, then compare with the pristine sample.

Rmass = (mw)after / (mw)before × 100%

留下固体不代表原框架或原始化学组成全部保留。Recovered solid does not establish complete preservation of the original framework or composition.

公式为 SI S4–S5 原定义的简写；w 在计算中用 0–1 的质量分数。数值大于 100% 不截断，也不自动解释为“变得更稳定”。Equations abbreviate the definitions in SI S4–S5; w is a mass fraction between 0 and 1. Values above 100% are retained and do not automatically mean improved stability.

04 · 一个容易误读的称量An easy mass-balance trap

回收 47.4 mg，不等于框架增重 58%Recovering 47.4 mg does not mean 58% more framework

MIL-101(Cr) 的原始称量是 30 mg，浸水后回收 47.40 mg。但 TGA 无溶剂平台质量分数由 82% 变为 51%。把两者一起算，质量保留率约为 98.27%。MIL-101(Cr) starts at 30 mg and yields 47.40 mg after treatment. Its TGA solvent-free plateau fraction changes from 82% to 51%. Combining both measurements gives about 98.27% mass retention.

质量校正没有测定滤液中的金属或配体，也不能辨认留下的每种化学物种。超过 100% 的质量结果仍需组成证据解释。The correction does not measure metal or linker in the filtrate and cannot identify every retained chemical species. Mass values above 100% still need compositional evidence.

05 · 从案例走向设计From cases to design

设计经验有用，实验中的反例更有用Design rules help; counterexamples test them

同一系列：UiO-66 → 67 → 68Within a series: UiO-66 → 67 → 68

相对结晶度为 64%、8%、14%；BET 保留率为 90.7%、2.2%、3.6%。作者联系到更长配体、柔性与干燥毛细应力。它支持在本流程下比较这些批次，不证明配体长度是唯一变量。Relative crystallinity is 64%, 8% and 14%; BET retention is 90.7%, 2.2% and 3.6%. The authors discuss longer linkers, flexibility and drying stress. This supports a comparison of these batches under this workflow, without proving linker length is the only variable.

跨系列：Al 与 Zr 不遵循一条直线Across series: Al and Zr do not follow one line

大芳香配体并未使 Al-PyrMOF 失去孔隙：BET 保留 98.6%，校正质量保留 99.85%。但 CAU-10-H 在结晶度 104% 时只保留 62.2% BET 面积。不要把“多数 Al 材料表现好”推广为所有 Al-MOF 都稳定。A large aromatic linker did not eliminate porosity in Al-PyrMOF: BET and corrected mass retention are 98.6% and 99.85%. Yet CAU-10-H retains 104% crystallinity with only 62.2% BET area. Good performance in many tested Al materials is not a guarantee for every Al-MOF.

开放金属位不等于必然失效Open metal sites are not a certain failure

mCBMOF-1 的结晶度为 102%，BET 面积由 591 升至 635 m²/g。作者用疏水环境、配位保护和互穿解释这种耐受性。这些是机制解释，不是逐一隔离变量的因果证明。mCBMOF-1 retains 102% crystallinity, with BET area rising from 591 to 635 m²/g. The authors invoke a hydrophobic environment, coordination protection and interpenetration. These are mechanistic interpretations rather than a causal isolation of each variable.

论文没有测量 REE 吸附。用于稀土回收的研究启发是：在实际 pH、盐、缓冲液和再生液中，同时检查骨架变化、溶出与竞争吸附。这里的去离子水结果不能直接代替那些验证。This paper does not measure REE adsorption. Its implication for rare-earth recovery is to check framework changes, leaching and competitive adsorption under the actual pH, salts, buffers and regenerants. Results in deionized water cannot replace those tests.
06 · 读数值也要读边界Read the limits alongside the numbers

哪些结论仍不能从这里推出？What does this study leave unresolved?

三批 UiO-66，不是所有材料的误差条Three UiO-66 batches are not error bars for every material

SI S105–S106 明确写三批独立合成样品按同一流程测试。结晶度分别为 64.0799%、72.5481%、54.5361%；SI 报告的标准差为 7.3577 个百分点，BET 与质量分别为 2.1926、2.5011 个百分点。不能将它们视为全部 51 种材料的置信区间，也不能把三个批次当作连续三次循环。SI S105–S106 describes three independently synthesized batches tested by the same protocol. Crystallinity is 64.0799%, 72.5481% and 54.5361%; the SI reports a standard deviation of 7.3577 percentage points, with 2.1926 and 2.5011 points for BET and mass. These are not confidence intervals for all 51 materials, and three batches are not three sequential cycles.

研究者阅读：接下来最需要补什么？Research reading: what would strengthen the evidence?

以下是讲解者分析：测量滤液中的金属和配体可完善质量闭合；浸水过程中的原位结构表征可区分水解与后续干燥损伤；多批次、多时间点、湿干循环以及应用液体对照可测试结果的稳健性。对于极低的初始 BET 值，应同时报告绝对值和适当气体探针，而不是只比较百分比。这些不是本论文已经完成的额外实验。Explainer analysis: measuring dissolved metals and linkers would improve mass closure; in-situ structure measurements during immersion would distinguish hydrolysis from later drying damage. Multiple batches, exposure times, wet–dry cycles and application-specific liquids would test robustness. Very low initial BET areas require absolute values and an appropriate gas probe alongside percentages. These are proposed controls, not additional experiments completed in this paper.
