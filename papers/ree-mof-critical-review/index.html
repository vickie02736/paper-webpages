高容量之后，还缺什么？What is missing beyond high capacity?

这篇批判性综述把问题从“哪个 MOF 吸得多”推进到“凭什么选择、如何证明、怎样回收成品”。它质疑仅靠面积、功能团名称和几轮循环能否支撑实际稀土分离。This critical review moves from which MOF loads more to why it selects, how evidence supports that claim and how usable products are recovered. It questions whether area, group labels and a few cycles establish practical REE separation.

核心主张不是选出通用冠军，而是联合考察位点、节点、溶剂化、竞争基质、耐久与后处理。Its central argument is to jointly evaluate sites, nodes, solvation, competing matrices, durability and downstream treatment rather than identify a universal winner.

功能团、节点和孔道一起工作Groups, nodes and pores act together

§3.1–3.2 对比原始 MOF、功能化与复合材料。含氧、含氮和含磷基团可参与结合，但可及密度、空间预组织、质子化及水合竞争会改变有效亲和性。Sections 3.1–3.2 compare pristine, functionalized and composite systems. O/N/P groups can participate in binding, while accessible density, spatial organization, protonation and hydration competition change effective affinity.

节点不是被动支架：§3.2.3 引用 XAFS 研究，指出部分 Cr／Zr MOF 的稀土结合涉及金属氧簇。缺失连接体会改变这类位点的可及性；不能把所有提升归给新增有机基团。Nodes are active participants. Section 3.2.3 cites XAFS studies involving metal-oxo clusters in some Cr/Zr MOFs. Missing linkers change their accessibility, so improvements cannot all be assigned to added organic groups.

BET 气体面积衡量可达孔隙的一种特征，不等于水中活性位点总数，更不是直接的结合自由能。Gas-derived BET area characterizes accessible porosity; it is neither the total aqueous active-site count nor a direct binding free energy.

SF 很大，仍可能共捕集很多杂质High SF can coexist with substantial impurity capture

以下是恒定线性分配的教学例子，不是任何论文的测量或预测。假设 Kd,A = 10000、Kd,B = 10 mL/g，V/m = 1000 mL/g，故 SF = 1000。用 f = Kd/(Kd + V/m) 计算单次捕集比例，保持参数不变，只改变进料 B/A 的质量比。This is a constant linear-partition teaching example, not a paper measurement or prediction. Assume Kd,A = 10000, Kd,B = 10 mL/g and V/m = 1000 mL/g, giving SF = 1000. Compute the captured fraction as f = Kd/(Kd + V/m), varying only feed mass ratio B/A.

显示的是理想两组分捕集相的 A 质量分数；真实竞争、位点饱和与溶液物种会改变 Kd。洗脱或氧化物产品还可能具有不同组成。The output is A mass fraction in an ideal two-component captured phase. Actual competition, site saturation and speciation alter Kd, and eluate/oxide composition can differ.

真实基质会改变哪些条件？What changes in real matrices?

§3.3 同时考察稀土彼此竞争、Fe／Al 等共存金属，以及高铵背景。对一种背景离子耐受，不能推广为对所有实际废水耐受；原研究可能先中和或调配进料。Section 3.3 considers inter-REE competition, coexisting metals such as Fe/Al and ammonium-rich backgrounds. Tolerance to one ion does not establish tolerance to all wastewater; original studies may neutralize or adjust feeds.

综述报告 BNMG-1 在实际磁体／灯废料中的 Nd／Y 回收约 57%／27%，明显低于部分模拟液表现。这里只作为综述所述案例，不能推广为任何新废料的固定效率。The review reports approximately 57% Nd and 27% Y recovery by BNMG-1 in actual magnet/lamp wastes, below some synthetic-feed performance. These remain review-reported examples rather than fixed efficiencies for other wastes.

循环容量保留，不等于骨架完整Capacity retention does not establish structural persistence

§4.3–4.5 要求结合循环吸附、PXRD、形貌、孔隙与 ICP 浸出。残留晶相可保留衍射峰，同时一部分骨架已溶解或孔道减少；短期容量也可能由新生成相维持。Sections 4.3–4.5 call for cycling, PXRD, morphology, porosity and ICP release measurements together. Remaining crystallites can retain peaks despite partial dissolution or pore loss, while new phases may sustain short-term capture.

酸的种类、浓度、时间、温度与背景配体共同决定再生损伤。五到十轮测试是有限的操作证据，不能直接外推长期固定床寿命。Acid identity, concentration, time, temperature and background ligands jointly control regeneration damage. Five to ten cycles give bounded operating evidence rather than a long-term fixed-bed lifetime.
