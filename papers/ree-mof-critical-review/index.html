从一杯混合溶液，到一个合格产品From a mixed solution to a usable product

稀土元素（REE）是一组性质相近的元素；在这里通常以带电离子存在于水中。MOF 的孔内结合位置可以留下这些离子，但也可能留下杂质。吸附后的固体还需处理，才能取得目标产品。Rare-earth elements (REEs) have similar chemical properties; here they typically occur as charged ions in water. Binding sites in MOF pores can retain them but may also retain impurities. The loaded solid still needs processing to yield a target product.

容量 qCapacity q

单位质量吸附剂留下多少目标物，常用 mg/g。它回答“装了多少”，分母是吸附剂质量，不能直接读成产品含稀土百分数。Target mass retained per sorbent mass, often mg/g. It answers how much is loaded; the denominator is sorbent mass, not product mass for a purity percentage.

分离因子 SFSeparation factor SF

比较目标与竞争离子在固液两相间分配的偏好。它是比值，还需要知道进料组成和液固比才能讨论共捕集多少。A ratio comparing target and competitor partitioning between solid and liquid. Feed composition and liquid-to-solid ratio are also needed to assess co-captured amounts.

回收率与纯度Recovery and purity

回收率以进料中的目标总量为分母；纯度以最终产品总量为分母。损失少不保证杂质少，杂质少也不保证目标都找回。Recovery divides target obtained by target initially fed. Purity divides target in the final product by total product amount. Low loss does not guarantee low contamination, or vice versa.

功能团、节点和孔道一起工作Groups, nodes and pores act together

BET 气体面积衡量可达孔隙的一种特征，不等于水中活性位点总数，更不是直接的结合自由能。Gas-derived BET area characterizes accessible porosity; it is neither the total aqueous active-site count nor a direct binding free energy.

为什么拟合、光谱和对照要一起用？Why combine fits, spectra and controls?

  1. 先描述整体规律Describe the overall trend

    动力学拟合描述吸附量随时间变化，等温线拟合描述平衡量随浓度变化。曲线贴合说明数学模型能描述这些数据，不直接识别原子结合方式。Kinetic fits describe uptake versus time; isotherm fits describe equilibrium loading versus concentration. A good fit describes these data mathematically but does not identify atomic binding by itself.

  2. 看局部环境是否变化Look for local changes

    红外光谱（FT-IR）看分子振动，X 射线光电子能谱（XPS）看表面电子环境；变化可支持基团参与，却通常不能唯一确定配位数与几何。Infrared spectroscopy (FT-IR) probes molecular vibrations; X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) probes surface electronic environments. Changes can support group involvement but usually do not uniquely determine coordination number and geometry.

  3. 排除替代解释Test alternative explanations

    比较无功能团、不同节点与无材料空白等对照，并结合 X 射线吸收谱（XAS）的局部距离等信息。需要问：沉淀、背景离子或框架变化是否也能解释结果？Compare controls such as no functional group, different nodes and no sorbent, alongside local information from X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS).
