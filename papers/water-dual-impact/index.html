水与 MOF：
破坏，也可能保护Water and MOFs:
damage and protection

水能靠近材料，并不代表它一定会把连接拆开。本文比较四种 MOF 的计算模型，追问水先停在哪里、怎样聚在一起，以及能否走向局部水解。Water approaching a material does not necessarily break its connections. Calculations for four MOFs ask where water first binds, how it gathers and whether it can proceed toward local hydrolysis.

Fatemeh Keshavarz
Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics 28, 6089–6098 (2026)

吸水很强，为什么仍可能不耐水？Why can strong water uptake coexist with instability?

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

先分清吸附、聚集与断键Separate adsorption, clustering and bond cleavage

MOF 的金属连接点与有机连接体围出孔道。水进入孔后，可以被某个位点吸引；更多水还可以彼此连接。只有进一步改变原有连接，才涉及这里研究的初始水解。Metal junctions and organic linkers enclose MOF pores. Water can enter and be attracted to a site; additional water can also associate with other water molecules. Initial hydrolysis involves a further change to the original framework connections.

氢键Hydrogen bond

水的氢可与附近含氧或含氮部位产生有方向的吸引，形成水网络。它与框架的金属–连接体连接不是同一种键。A water hydrogen can interact directionally with nearby oxygen- or nitrogen-containing sites, building a water network. This differs from a framework metal–linker bond.

DFT 计算DFT calculation

密度泛函理论用电子分布估算给定原子排列的能量和作用力。改变排列并比较能量，可研究局部过程；它不是直接拍摄材料。Density functional theory uses electron density to estimate energies and forces for an atomic arrangement. Comparing arrangements probes local processes; it is not direct imaging of a material.

自由能路径Free-energy path

在指定温度和压力下，给一串候选结构计算相对自由能。中间稳定结构称中间体，连接步骤中的高点称过渡态；路径不是实际经过时间。At specified temperature and pressure, assign relative free energies to candidate structures. Local stable structures are intermediates; a connecting high point is a transition state. The path is not elapsed time.

读完应能解释：为什么“吸水强”与“耐水”并不矛盾，以及为什么模型路径上的 +2.8 不能直接当成从已吸附水状态出发的能垒。You should explain why strong water attraction can coexist with water resistance, and why +2.8 on a path is not directly the barrier measured from the water-bound state.

不能仅凭“亲水/疏水”判断耐水性。关键还在于水结合在哪里、网络怎样形成，以及局部断键能否发生。Hydrophilicity alone does not determine water stability. Where water binds, how networks form and which local bonds can cleave all matter.

水进入孔道之后，既可能参与金属–连接体键的断裂，也可能与极性连接体组成氢键网络。论文用四种 MOF 的周期与截断簇 DFT 计算，区分吸附、聚集与初始水解，解释这两种方向如何出现。After entering a pore, water can facilitate metal–linker cleavage or form a hydrogen-bond network with polar linkers. Periodic and truncated-cluster DFT calculations for four MOFs separate adsorption, clustering and initial hydrolysis to explain these different outcomes.

  1. 吸附：第一批水在哪里？Adsorption: where does water first bind?

    开放金属位点和连接体上的 O/N/H 位点，提供不同的初始位置。Open metals and O/N/H sites on linkers offer different initial binding environments.

  2. 聚集：后来的水怎样组织？Clustering: how does more water organize?

    水–水、水–连接体氢键与配体运动共同改变局部环境。Water–water and water–linker hydrogen bonding interact with ligand motion.

  3. 水解：什么键、什么路径？Hydrolysis: which bond and pathway?

    局部过渡态搜索检验初始断键路径，而不是直接测量材料寿命。Local transition-state searches examine initial cleavage pathways rather than measure material lifetime.

这是计算机理研究。四种材料的“耐水/不耐水”背景分类来自既有文献，本研究没有重新测量吸附容量、PXRD 保持率或 REE 分离性能。This is a computational mechanism study. The background water-stability classes come from prior literature; this work does not newly measure uptake capacities, PXRD retention or REE separation.

周期晶体与截断簇，各自回答什么？What do periodic crystals and truncated clusters answer?

机制 · 这些步骤为什么不能省略Mechanism · Why these steps matter

两种模型保留了什么，又省去了什么？What does each model retain or leave out?

  1. 周期晶体Periodic crystal

    把晶胞在空间中重复，保留框架网络的连接约束，再计算加入水后的排列。这里部分含水计算收敛困难，作者主要据此讨论定性机制。Repeat a unit cell in space to retain network constraints, then calculate arrangements with water. Some hydrated calculations have convergence difficulties, so the authors mainly use them for qualitative mechanisms.

  2. 截断簇Truncated cluster

    取出局部节点与连接体片段，用氢处理切断的边界。计算量较小，便于寻找反应步骤，但边缘与柔性会不同于完整晶体。Extract a node-and-linker fragment and cap cut boundaries with hydrogen. This reduces cost and facilitates reaction searches, but edges and flexibility differ from the full crystal.

  3. 只在明确参照内比较Compare within a defined reference

    先确认模型、方法、含水数和自旋设置，再比较同一路径的结构。不同模型的绝对能量不能直接相减来排材料寿命。Identify model, method, water count and spin treatment before comparing structures along a path. Absolute energies from different models cannot simply be subtracted to rank material lifetimes.

周期模型：保留晶格约束Periodic models: retain lattice constraints

VASP、SCAN + D3、450 eV、Γ 点，允许原子和晶胞松弛。它提供完整连接网络中的局部吸水环境；部分含水体系收敛困难，因此作者主要用它判断定性机制。VASP calculations use SCAN + D3, 450 eV and Γ-point sampling with atomic and cell relaxation. 截断簇：看局部化学与路径Truncated clusters: local chemistry and paths

从节点与连接体提取片段，切断处用 H 封端。SI 指定后续计算采用 PBE0/LanL2DZ + GD3；频率与 IRC 检查用于区分极小值和确认部分过渡路径。Fragments contain a node and linkers, with H-capped cuts. The SI specifies PBE0/LanL2DZ + GD3 for subsequent calculations; frequencies and IRC checks distinguish minima and confirm some reaction paths.

截断后自由端可以旋转、堆叠，完整无缺陷晶体的连接体却受到邻近节点约束。因此，簇中的“孔口闭合”可启发理解欠配位或部分水解后的局部运动，不能直接等同于完整晶体已经塌陷。Free ends in a truncated cluster can rotate and stack, whereas neighbouring nodes constrain linkers in an intact crystal. Cluster pore closure can inform local motion after undercoordination or partial hydrolysis; it does not directly demonstrate collapse of an intact crystal.

MOF-303 的亲水位点可能形成保护网络Hydrophilic sites may support a protective network in MOF-303

作者的过渡态搜索没有在 MOF-303 的所试模型中确认水解过渡态；候选结构倾向于把水稳定在连接体或桥联位点附近。SI 表 S5 中 Al 的 |f⁺| ≤ 0.01，也支持模型内反应敏感位置与其他材料不同。No hydrolysis transition state was confirmed in the tested MOF-303 models. 据此，作者提出水–连接体氢键网络一方面限制水直接接近 Al–O 键，另一方面抑制自由配体的堆叠。这是基于所试构型的保护机制解释；没有找到过渡态，不等于证明所有路径不存在，也不能推出无限能垒或无限寿命。The proposed explanation is that water–linker hydrogen bonding limits direct access to Al–O bonds and suppresses stacking of free ligands. Fukui 指数测量的是什么？What does the Fukui index describe?

本文通过加入一个电子前后的 Hirshfeld 原子电荷差，定位对亲核进攻较敏感的位置。它是电子结构描述符，受所选簇、封端与电荷分配方法影响；本身不是实验水解速率常数。Here, differences in Hirshfeld atomic charges before and after adding an electron locate sites sensitive to nucleophilic attack. This electronic descriptor depends on the cluster, caps and charge-partitioning method; it is not an experimental hydrolysis rate constant.

对 REE 吸附材料的启发：同时看水与位点REE connection: consider both water and binding sites

以下是从机理研究延伸的分析：为捕获 REE 引入极性位点时，应同时检验这些位点如何组织水、是否保持连接、以及酸洗和竞争阴离子下的行为。更亲水或更疏水，不能单独替代工作条件下的稳定性测量。The following is an analysis extension: when adding polar REE-binding sites, also test how they organize water, whether attachments persist, and how acid stripping and competing anions affect them. Hydrophilicity or hydrophobicity alone cannot replace stability measurements under working conditions.
