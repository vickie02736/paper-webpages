提高容量，是否也提高分离选择性？Does greater capacity also improve separation selectivity?

论文先给 UiO-66-COOH 引入磷酸基，再以酒石酸钠处理调节缺陷，得到 UiO-66-COOH-OP-ST2。Yb 吸附量提高，但与轻稀土的分离偏好并非同步增强。The paper adds phosphate groups to UiO-66-COOH, then uses sodium tartrate to modify defects, yielding UiO-66-COOH-OP-ST2. Yb loading increases, while preference over lighter REEs does not improve uniformly.

在 pH 6 的比较中，OP → ST2：83.11 → 132.76 mg/g。In the pH 6 comparison, OP → ST2: 83.11 → 132.76 mg/g.

选择性Selectivity

混合液 Yb/La：4.650 → 2.518；Yb/Nd 却稍有上升。Mixed-feed Yb/La: 4.650 → 2.518, while Yb/Nd rises slightly.

权衡Trade-off

BET 面积与热稳定性降低；五轮后仍有容量但有衰减。BET area and thermal stability decrease; capacity persists after five cycles with some decline.

这是模拟稀土溶液中的材料研究；没有真实矿山废水或磁体废料验证。容量、分配比选择性和循环容量保留率使用不同分母。This material study uses simulated REE solutions, without actual mine-water or magnet-waste validation. Capacity, partition-based selectivity and cycle-capacity retention use different denominators.

功能化之后，再调节缺陷Functionalize first, then modify defects

UiO-66-COOH 由 ZrCl₄、偏苯三甲酸在 DMF / HCl 中 180 °C、24 h 制得。随后用 O-磷酰乙醇胺和 DCC，在 DMF 中 160 °C、48 h 引入磷酸基，得到 OP。UiO-66-COOH is prepared from ZrCl₄ and trimellitic acid in DMF/HCl at 180 °C for 24 h. O-phosphorylethanolamine and DCC then introduce phosphate groups in DMF at 160 °C for 48 h, giving OP.

100 mg OP 分别与 50 mL、0.016 / 0.048 / 0.064 mol/L 酒石酸钠水溶液在 60 °C 反应 10 h，得到 ST1 / ST2 / ST3。ST2 是这组三种处理条件中容量最高的材料，不是越强蚀刻越好。A 100 mg OP sample contacts 50 mL sodium tartrate at 0.016/0.048/0.064 mol/L and 60 °C for 10 h to give ST1/ST2/ST3. ST2 has the highest capacity within these treatments; stronger etching does not keep improving performance.

ATR-IR 支持功能基团引入；SEM 显示 ST2 表面变粗糙并出现孔洞。作者用缺失连接体、更多暴露含氧位点解释性能变化。没有为每种处理独立测得所有原子级缺陷构型，本页不重绘 Scheme 1 的原子连接。ATR-IR supports functional-group incorporation; SEM shows rougher ST2 surfaces and holes. The authors interpret the changes through missing linkers and more exposed oxygen-containing sites. Atomic defect configurations are not independently resolved for every treatment, so Scheme 1 connectivity is not redrawn.

中等处理条件的 Yb 捕集最高Intermediate treatment gives the greatest Yb capture

正文 §3.2 与 SI 图 S2 的报告值；统一 0–150 mg/g。主要比较在 Yb 约 1000 mg/L、pH 6、30 °C、8 h、固体 1 mg/mL 进行。这里是该条件下吸附量，不是 Langmuir 拟合 Qm。Reported in main §3.2 and SI Figure S2, on a common 0–150 mg/g scale. The principal comparison uses approximately 1000 mg/L Yb, pH 6, 30 °C, 8 h and 1 mg/mL sorbent. These are condition-specific loadings rather than fitted Langmuir Qm values.

ST3 的容量降低，作者提出过度调节可能损伤骨架和功能基团。它是合理的工作解释；没有把未测得的 ST1 / ST3 缺陷数补成精确数列。For lower ST3 capacity, the authors propose excessive treatment may damage the framework and groups. This is a working interpretation; unmeasured ST1/ST3 defect counts are not filled in as an exact series.

面积下降，孔宽上升Area decreases while reported pore width increases

SI 表 S1；实际吸附气体为 Ar、87 K，尽管正文引言另称 N₂。由 340.56 降至 86.85 m²/g 的面积，与 2.32 升至 6.69 nm 的报告孔宽是不同指标；总孔体积也下降。本页不把孔宽扩大写成总孔隙度增加。SI Table S1 uses Ar at 87 K, despite an introductory mention of N₂. Area declining from 340.56 to 86.85 m²/g and reported width increasing from 2.32 to 6.69 nm are different metrics; total pore volume also falls. Greater width is not presented as greater total porosity.

原始分布、模型、粗糙表面与剩余骨架共同影响这些指标。气体测得的孔宽不是一个直接用于预测水合 Yb 迁移速率的分子筛窗口。The measured distribution, model, rough surfaces and remaining framework jointly affect these metrics. Gas-derived pore width is not a molecular-sieve window that directly predicts hydrated Yb transport.

“缺陷数”来自模型与表征Defect counts depend on models and characterization

改变理论失重假设，会怎样？What if the theoretical mass-loss assumption changes?

教学敏感性：固定 ST2 报告失重为 20.71%，只改变理论完整材料失重 L。沿用 SI 的 n = 6(1 − 20.71/L)。这不是新测得的缺陷数，也不代表真实合成一定产生该构型。Teaching sensitivity: fix reported ST2 loss at 20.71% and vary theoretical intact-material loss L. Apply the SI expression n = 6(1 − 20.71/L). This is not a newly measured defect count or a claim that synthesis necessarily yields that configuration.

由此模型推算的缺失连接体数 / Zr₆Missing linkers per Zr₆ inferred under this model

默认 L = 56.30% 时约为 3.79，复现 SI 的算法；改变组成、功能化程度、残渣或归一化假设，会改变推算结果。At default L = 56.30%, the estimate is about 3.79, reproducing the SI arithmetic. Composition, grafting, residue and normalization assumptions affect the inferred value.

低 pH 抑制捕集，也帮助再生Low pH suppresses capture and supports regeneration

pH 2–6 中，Yb 捕集随 pH 上升。作者用质子竞争、功能基团质子化与静电作用解释该趋势。ST2 在所测区间的 ζ 电位均为负；OP 在 pH 2 为 +13.3 mV，其余所测点为负。Yb capture increases over pH 2–6. The authors invoke proton competition, functional-group protonation and electrostatics. ST2 has negative ζ potential throughout the tested range; OP is +13.3 mV at pH 2 and negative at the other measured points.

负电表面有利于阳离子接近，但不能独立证明唯一络合位点。初始 pH、最终 pH 与水解/沉淀控制也需区分；没有用这一趋势保证所有 pH 6、高浓度或不同背景离子条件下均无并行沉淀。A negative surface can favor cation approach without resolving one binding site. Initial/final pH and hydrolysis/precipitation controls remain distinct; this trend does not guarantee zero parallel precipitation in every pH 6, high-concentration or different-background feed.

拟合得好，不等于唯一机理A good fit does not identify a unique mechanism

图 4 / 正文约 240 min 接近平衡，实测平台 OP / ST2 为 82.27 / 131.27 mg/g；SI 表 S3 的 PSO 拟合 qe 却为 43.39 / 68.10 mg/g，约低一半。它们不能视作相互验证的一套 qe。本页明确分开，不修补成新数据。Figure 4/main approaches equilibrium around 240 min, reporting OP/ST2 plateau values of 82.27/131.27 mg/g. SI Table S3 instead gives PSO fitted qe = 43.39/68.10 mg/g, roughly half as large. They do not mutually validate one qe dataset. They remain separate rather than being repaired into new data.

PSO 的 R² 为 0.9991 / 0.9873，优于对应 PFO，但不能独立证明化学反应是唯一限速步骤。粒内扩散的分段直线也是多过程线索，不等于完整传质机理已解析。PSO R² values are 0.9991/0.9873, higher than corresponding PFO fits, without proving one chemical reaction is the sole limiting step. Segmented intraparticle-diffusion lines suggest multiple stages rather than a fully resolved transport mechanism.

更多 Yb 负载，与更强 Yb/La 分离不同Greater Yb loading differs from stronger Yb/La separation

正文式 12–13：Kd = (C₀ − Ce)V/(Cem)，SYb/M = Kd,Yb / Kd,M；这里是普通比值，不是对数。图 7b 混合 La / Ce / Nd / Yb 各约 250 mg/L，15 mg / 15 mL，pH 6、30 °C、8 h。统一 0–5 的无单位量尺，1 表示等分配偏好。Main Equations 12–13: Kd = (C₀ − Ce)V/(Cem), SYb/M = Kd,Yb/Kd,M. This is a direct ratio rather than a logarithm. Figure 7b uses mixed La/Ce/Nd/Yb at approximately 250 mg/L each, 15 mg/15 mL, pH 6, 30 °C for 8 h. One dimensionless 0–5 scale is used, with 1 denoting equal partition preference.

ST2 的 Yb/La 与 Yb/Ce 低于 OP，Yb/Nd 高于 OP；因此既不能概括为所有选择性改善，也不能概括为每对都下降。单离子图 7a 的 La / Yb 容量差本身不是这个竞争系数。ST2 has lower Yb/La and Yb/Ce than OP, but greater Yb/Nd. Neither uniformly improved nor uniformly reduced selectivity describes every pair. La/Yb capacity differences in single-ion Figure 7a are not these competitive coefficients.

温度促进捕集，标准态仍需说明Warmer conditions favor uptake, with standard-state conventions explicit

20 / 40 / 60 °C 的等温线显示捕集随温度提高；60 °C 正文报告 OP / ST2 达到 109.04 / 145.44 mg/g。SI 表 S4 列出 Langmuir KL 与 R²，却没有给 Qm，因此本页不从不完整参数生成预测等温线。Isotherms at 20/40/60 °C show greater uptake at higher temperature, with main-text values of 109.04/145.44 mg/g for OP/ST2 at 60 °C. SI Table S4 lists Langmuir KL and R² without Qm, so incomplete parameters are not used to generate predicted isotherms.

SI 表 S5 的原报告。正文用 Ke = 1000KLM，将 KL 转为摩尔浓度基准并写 Ke 单位 L/mol；取对数与解释“标准”自由能还需要标准态约定。正焓与升温促进吸附一致，负 ΔG 不是唯一化学结合结构的证明。Original reports from SI Table S5. The main uses Ke = 1000KLM to convert KL to a molar-concentration basis and labels Ke in L/mol; taking its logarithm and interpreting standard free energy requires a standard-state convention. Positive enthalpy agrees with enhanced warm uptake; negative ΔG does not identify a unique chemical binding structure.

五轮后保留了多少容量？How much capacity remains after five cycles?

再生为 0.5 mol/L HNO₃、2 h；图 8a 与正文报告五轮结果。这里的百分比是相对初始容量的保留率，不能写成从工作液去除同等百分比的 Yb。正文式 11 印成初始 qe / 再生后 qe，与 <100% 保留率叙述相反；本页采用明确标注的报告保留率，不重复反向公式。Regeneration uses 0.5 mol/L HNO₃ for 2 h; Figure 8a/main reports five-cycle results. Percentages describe retention relative to initial capacity, not the same percentage of Yb removed from feed. Printed Equation 11 places initial qe over regenerated qe, opposite to the <100% retention narrative. The reported retention values are explicitly used without reproducing that inverted formula.

SI 图 S5–S6 的回收固体仍有衍射特征，但整体图谱较宽。它支持所测五轮中的结构特征保留，没有独立证明每个缺陷、孔道和功能基团完全不变。Recovered solids retain diffraction features in SI S5–S6, with broad overall patterns. This supports retained features over the tested five cycles without independently establishing every defect, pore and group is unchanged.

络合与静电作用：解释及证据Complexation and electrostatics: interpretation and evidence

EPR g = 2.003、TGA 推算、孔隙/ζ 电位变化、Yb 元素分布与吸附后 O 1s / P 2p 变化，共同支持作者提出的含氧位点络合与静电吸引。XPS 中 Yb 4d 与 Zr 3d 重叠，因此不能把总谱看成孤立清晰的 Yb 4d 峰。EPR at g = 2.003, TGA estimates, pore/ζ changes, Yb maps and post-uptake O 1s/P 2p changes support the authors’ proposed oxygen-site complexation and electrostatics. Yb 4d overlaps Zr 3d in XPS, so the survey is not treated as an isolated resolved Yb 4d peak.

正文报告吸附后溶液中 Zr 总量 OP / ST2 为 0.041 / 0.035 μg，支持该次测试中 Zr 释放很少。它不是浓度，也不是所有循环、全部组分都不浸出或绝对排除任何离子交换的证明。The main reports total Zr in post-uptake solution of 0.041/0.035 μg for OP/ST2, supporting small Zr release in that test. These are amounts rather than concentrations, and do not establish zero release of every component across all cycles or absolutely exclude any ion exchange.

本页不重绘图 10 的原子连接，也不将氧空位直接等同于逐个计数的水中裸露配位位点。实际水合、质子化、缺陷封端和物种分布仍会影响结合。Figure 10 atomic connectivity is not redrawn, and oxygen vacancies are not equated to individually counted bare coordination sites in water. Hydration, protonation, defect termination and solution speciation still affect binding.

下一步需要完整工作液与长期验证The next step needs complete feeds and long-term validation

该研究揭示功能化与缺陷调节可以共同提高条件容量，并出现容量—选择性—耐久权衡。它尚未测遍相邻重稀土、实际酸浸液、连续柱穿透、产品纯度、颗粒成型或完整酸/组分物料衡算。The study shows functionalization and defect treatment can raise conditional loading while revealing capacity/selectivity/durability trade-offs. It does not cover every adjacent heavy REE, actual acid leachate, continuous-column breakthrough, product purity, shaping or full acid/component balances.

在同一进料条件下比较容量与选择性，并追踪循环前后面积、孔体积、配体/磷/Zr 释放，能帮助判断暴露位点的收益是否值得结构代价。Comparing capacity and selectivity at matched feeds, while tracking cycle-wise area, pore volume and ligand/P/Zr release, would help assess whether exposed-site benefits justify structural costs.
