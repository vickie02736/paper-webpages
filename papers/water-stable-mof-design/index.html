让框架不被水破坏，只完成了第一步。用于捕集二氧化碳时，还要让气体进得去、能被留下，并能在再生时放出来。Preventing water damage is only the first step. For carbon dioxide capture, gas must also enter, be retained and be released during regeneration.

耐水材料，要同时回答两个问题Two questions for a water-resistant adsorbent

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

稳定的“容器”，还要有可用的入口A durable container also needs usable entrances

MOF 是由金属连接点和有机连接体搭成的多孔固体。“吸附”指分子在材料表面或孔内被留下；“再生”指用适当条件把它们释放，使材料能重复使用。本综述把耐水设计与 CO₂ 捕集联系起来。A MOF is a porous solid made of metal junctions and organic linkers. Adsorption retains molecules at surfaces or within pores. Regeneration releases them under suitable conditions so the material can be used again. This review connects water-resistant design with CO₂ capture.

连接是否耐受Do the connections persist?

水可能竞争金属附近的位置或改变连接体。加强连接、增加连接数等策略针对这一问题，但需在具体介质中验证。Water can compete near a metal or alter a linker. Stronger or more numerous connections address this problem, but need validation in the actual medium.

入口是否可达Are entrances accessible?

疏水表示较不容易与水接近或润湿。入口的疏水修饰可能减少水接触，也可能影响气体进入；不能只看拒水效果。Hydrophobic means less favorable contact or wetting by water. Hydrophobic entrance modifications may reduce water access but also affect gas entry; water exclusion is not the only target.

亲和力与选择性Affinity and selectivity

亲和力描述某种分子被吸引的倾向；选择性比较混合物中不同分子的偏好。单独一种气体的高吸附量不是混合气分离结果。Affinity describes attraction toward a species; selectivity compares preferences among mixture components. High uptake of one pure gas is not a mixture-separation result.

读完应能把设计分成“保住材料”和“完成捕集”两项检验，并指出需要在哪种含水气流下复测。You should separate preserving the material from accomplishing capture, and identify the humid feed under which retesting is needed.

从配位匹配、孔工程和疏水保护，走到湿气中的 CO₂ 捕集；每一步都把设计、测试条件和证据连起来。Connect coordination chemistry, pore engineering and hydrophobic protection with CO₂ capture in humid streams—while keeping design, test conditions and evidence together.

这篇综述整理水稳定 MOF 的构筑策略，并以 CO₂ 捕集说明“结构能留下来”与“功能还能工作”之间的联系。前者涉及水解、溶出和孔隙变化，后者还涉及竞争吸附、可用位点与再生。This review surveys strategies for constructing water-stable MOFs, then uses CO₂ capture to connect structural survival with retained function. The first concerns hydrolysis, dissolution and porosity; the second also involves competition, accessible sites and regeneration.

结构关Structural question

水接触后，连接方式、结晶性、固体质量和孔隙是否改变？必须把水、温度、时间、酸碱度及测量方法写清楚。After water exposure, do connectivity, crystallinity, solid mass or porosity change? Specify the medium, temperature, duration, acidity and measurements.

功能关Functional question

在实际进料、湿度和循环条件下，目标物还能被分离吗？耐水和高单组分吸附量，均不能单独证明工艺可用。Can the target still be separated under the actual feed, humidity and cycling conditions? Water resistance and high pure-component uptake alone do not establish process performance.

ΔG：产物是否有利？ΔG: are products favourable?

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS 描述指定反应与条件下的自由能变化。金属与配体的匹配、稳定配位单元及连接方式，会改变反应物与产物之间的能量关系。ΔG = ΔH − TΔS describes the free-energy change for a specified reaction and set of conditions. Metal–ligand matching, stable coordination units and connectivity influence the relationship between reactants and products.

能垒和可接近性：能走多快？Barriers and access: how fast can it proceed?

即使水解产物更有利，较高反应能垒、金属附近的位阻或较慢的水进入，都可能延缓降解。疏水表面主要改变接触和进入，不能直接证明水解 ΔG 已变为正。Even if hydrolysis products are favourable, reaction barriers, steric shielding or slower water access can delay degradation. A hydrophobic surface changes contact and entry; it does not establish a positive hydrolysis ΔG.

这是概念框架，不是本文测得的各材料自由能图。强键、动力学惰性、刚性和疏水性可能共同起作用，四种设计策略并非互斥分类。This is a conceptual framework, rather than measured free-energy profiles for every material. Bond strength, substitution kinetics, rigidity and hydrophobicity can act together, so the design strategies overlap.

从改哪里，到测什么From the location of a change to the test it requires

  1. 指明设计作用处Locate the design intervention

    改变金属–连接体结合、网络连接数或孔口环境，作用对象不同。先确定要阻止连接断裂，还是减少水接近。Changing metal–linker bonding, network connectivity or a pore entrance affects different objects. First decide whether the aim is to prevent bond loss or reduce water access.

  2. 检查处理后的材料Examine the treated solid

    衍射看有序排列；气体吸附看孔是否可达；溶出分析看哪些组分离开。几类证据一起约束“耐水”的含义。Diffraction probes ordered packing; gas adsorption probes pore accessibility; leaching analysis tracks departing components. Together these constrain the meaning of water resistance.

  3. 回到实际捕集条件Return to capture conditions

    在目标 CO₂ 浓度、温度、湿度和竞争气体下测捕集与释放，并查看循环后材料。结构保留不自动保证工作容量。Measure capture and release at the target CO₂ concentration, temperature, humidity and competing gases, then examine the cycled solid. Structural retention does not guarantee working capacity.

混合气里更偏爱谁Which component is preferred in a mixture?

表中的 IAST 选择性来自“理想吸附溶液理论”的模型估算，用单组分等温线预测混合物偏好。它与真正让湿混合气流过材料的突破实验不是同一证据。IAST selectivity is an estimate from ideal adsorbed solution theory, using single-component isotherms to predict mixture preference. It differs from breakthrough evidence obtained by passing a humid mixture through the solid.

水有时竞争位点，有时参与胺与 CO₂ 的反应；方向取决于材料和反应条件。综述里的不同例子不能合成“加水总能提高捕集”的规律。再生后能释放多少，还决定可循环使用的容量。Water can compete for sites or participate in amine–CO₂ chemistry; the direction depends on material and conditions. Different reviewed examples do not establish that adding water always improves capture. Release during regeneration also determines reusable capacity.

物理吸附Physisorption

开放金属位点、极性孔环境、氢键和孔径匹配可以稳定孔内 CO₂。它们可共同作用；原位衍射、光谱和计算帮助定位，而不是仅凭吸附热大小给机制下定义。Open metal sites, polar pore environments, hydrogen bonds and pore-size matching can stabilize CO₂. These interactions may coexist. In situ diffraction, spectroscopy and calculations help identify sites; adsorption heat alone does not define the mechanism.

化学吸附Chemisorption

某些胺或碱性含氧位点与 CO₂ 形成新的化学物种，如氨基甲酸盐、氨基甲酸或碳酸氢盐。产物取决于胺、金属和水环境；合适的再生条件下可以逆转。Some amines or basic oxygen sites form new species with CO₂, including carbamates, carbamic acid or bicarbonate. Products depend on the amine, metal and water environment; suitable regeneration conditions can reverse the reaction.

综述图 29 的例子尤其说明水的作用：IRMOF-74-III-(CH₂NH₂)₂ 在干态与含水条件下给出不同含碳产物。不能把“水竞争位点”当成所有材料的唯一情形；另一些材料中水会促进捕集化学。Review Fig. 29 illustrates the role of water: IRMOF-74-III-(CH₂NH₂)₂ yields different carbon-containing products under dry and humid conditions. Water competition is therefore one possibility; water can also promote capture chemistry.

物理吸附与化学吸附可并存。高吸附热可能有利于低分压亲和力，却不能单独给出完整再生能耗；后者还受水、工作容量与操作条件影响。Physisorption and chemisorption can coexist. High adsorption heat may favour low-pressure affinity, but total regeneration energy also depends on water, working capacity and operating conditions.

疏水保护的代价The cost of hydrophobic protection

转向 REE 水相吸附：继承设计思路，重做验证For aqueous REE capture, transfer the ideas and repeat the tests

以下是讲解者延伸，本文没有给出 REE 吸附容量或选择性。强配位骨架和位点保护提供设计起点，但液态水中的水合离子、竞争配体、酸性解吸剂及金属交换会引入不同过程。Explainer extension: this review reports no REE uptake or selectivity. Robust coordination units and site protection offer starting ideas, but hydrated ions, competing ligands, acidic stripping solutions and metal exchange create different processes in liquid water.

  1. 在真正的进料与解吸液中测试，而不是用纯水结论代替。Test the actual feed and stripping solutions.
  2. 同步测量 REE 去除、节点金属和连接体释放，核查吸附、沉淀与交换的贡献。Measure REE removal together with node-metal/linker release to assess adsorption, precipitation and exchange.
  3. 检查疏水层是否妨碍水合 REE 到达配体；以混合离子选择性、可回收量和循环物料衡算评价。Check whether hydrophobic layers obstruct hydrated REE access; evaluate mixed-ion selectivity, recoverable amount and cycle mass balances.
