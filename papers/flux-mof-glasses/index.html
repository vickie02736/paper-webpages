先认识三件事：连接、排列、流动Three starting points: bonding, order and flow

MOF 是什么？What is a MOF?

金属有机框架（MOF）是金属离子与有机分子通过配位键连接形成的延展结构。配位键涉及有机分子的供电子原子与金属结合。部分框架内部有气体可进入的孔。A metal–organic framework (MOF) is an extended structure of metal ions joined by organic molecules through coordination bonds. Electron-donating atoms of the organic molecules bind the metal. Some frameworks contain gas-accessible pores.

晶体与非晶如何区分？Crystal or amorphous?

晶体中的结构在较大距离上周期性重复；非晶材料没有这种长程重复，但近邻原子仍可保持明确的结合关系。失去周期性不等于所有化学键都断裂。Crystals repeat periodically over long distances. Amorphous materials lack that repetition, yet neighboring atoms can retain defined bonding. Loss of periodicity does not mean that all chemical bonds break.

玻璃为何能加工？Why can glass be processed?

玻璃是结构重排很慢的非晶固体。加热到玻璃化转变温度 Tg 附近，其结构运动在实验时间尺度上变得明显，材料逐渐软化。Tg 不等于晶体熔点，也不等于分解温度。Glass is an amorphous solid with very slow structural rearrangement. Near its glass-transition temperature Tg, structural motion becomes appreciable on the experimental timescale and the material softens. Tg is neither a crystal melting point nor a decomposition temperature.

这篇研究的问题：能否在材料明显分解之前使其流动，同时有控制地改变金属周围的结合关系？作者加入 phen，即 1,10-菲咯啉，一种含两个可与金属配位的氮原子的有机分子。The question is whether a material can flow before substantial decomposition, while its metal coordination is changed deliberately. The authors add phen (1,10-phenanthroline), an organic molecule with two nitrogen atoms able to bind a metal.

① 混合① Mix

将晶态 MOF 与 phen 按设定比例混合。ZIF 是以咪唑酸根类分子连接金属的一类 MOF；62、12 是结构名称编号。Mix a crystalline MOF with phen at a specified ratio. ZIFs are MOFs joined by imidazolate-type linkers; 62 and 12 are structure identifiers.

② 加热并反应② Heat and react

phen 帮助混合物在较低温度形成可流动的状态；在 Co 体系中还参与配体交换。配体交换指原先结合的分子被其他分子部分取代。phen helps the mixture become mobile at lower temperature. In Co systems it also participates in ligand exchange: partial replacement of originally bound molecules.

③ 冷却与验证③ Cool and test

冷却后重排变慢，得到玻璃或含少量晶体的产物。需检查热行为、周期性和局部化学，不能只看外观。Cooling slows rearrangement, producing glass or material retaining some crystals. Thermal behavior, periodicity and local chemistry must be checked; appearance alone is insufficient.

添加剂怎样参与配位反应The additive participates in coordination chemistry

传统 MOF 玻璃的难点是：很多 MOF 在熔化前就分解。本文让 phen 在熔融阶段与金属竞争配位，降低加工温度，同时把原有节点的配位数和连接度改掉。The bottleneck is that many MOFs decompose before melting. Here phen competes for metal coordination during melting, lowering the processing temperature while changing node coordination number and connectivity.

配位效应Coordination effect

4 → 3 / 2

热行为：加工温度与玻璃化温度分别说明什么Thermal behaviour opens the processing window

phen₀.₄₉|ZIF-62(Co) 和 phen₀.₅₁|ZIF-62(Zn) 先在约 90 °C 熔化吸水 phen·H₂O，随后从约 150 °C 开始失去 Bragg 峰；降温后 Co 样品完全非晶；Zn 样品仍有少量 ZIF-zni 晶相。第二次 DSC 的 Tg 分别为 206 °C（Co）和 184 °C（Zn），相比未改性的 312 °C 和 314 °C 明显下降。phen₀.₄₉|ZIF-62(Co) and phen₀.₅₁|ZIF-62(Zn) first melt phen·H₂O near 90 °C, then lose Bragg reflections from about 150 °C and form glass on cooling; the Zn sample retains a small crystalline ZIF-zni fraction. Second-scan Tg values are 206 °C (Co) and 184 °C (Zn), down from 312 °C and 314 °C for the unmodified materials.

ZIF-12 原本不成玻璃；加入 1.33 当量 phen 后，从 230 °C 熔融淬冷得到完全非晶、Tg 约 125 °C 的玻璃。原文将其列为 rho 拓扑 ZIF 玻璃化的扩展实例；rho 指一种特定的网络连接类型。ZIF-12 is not a glass former on its own. With 1.33 equivalents of phen, it gives a fully amorphous glass after quenching from 230 °C, with Tg around 125 °C. This extends vitrification to a rho-topology ZIF.

金属选择性决定 phen 会不会真正重构网络Metal selectivity decides whether the network is rebuilt

Co：真正的配位球工程Co: coordination-sphere engineering

phen 进入 Co²⁺ 配位球，增加配位数并使一个咪唑酸根变成端基。ZIF-62(Co) 平均从 4 连接降到 3 连接；ZIF-12 在高 phen 比下变成 2 连接链。phen enters the Co²⁺ coordination sphere, increasing coordination number and making one imidazolate terminal. ZIF-62(Co) moves from four- to three-connected; high phen loading makes ZIF-12 a two-connected chain.

Zn：降 Tg，但不换拓扑Zn: lower Tg without topology exchange

远红外、总散射、PDF 和热容都没有支持持久 phen–Zn²⁺ 配位。作者认为，Zn 在这些条件下偏好四面体咪唑酸根环境；phen 可能通过增加局部异质性和可访问构型稳定过冷液体。这是作者提出的机制解释。Far-IR, total scattering, PDF and heat capacity do not support persistent phen–Zn²⁺ coordination. The authors propose that Zn favors tetrahedral imidazolate coordination under these conditions; phen may stabilize the supercooled liquid through increased heterogeneity and more accessible configurations. This is a mechanistic interpretation.

磁性Magnetism

−25.19(8) K

phen 改性 Co 玻璃在 5 K 以上没有长程磁有序，但保留反铁磁相互作用；更低温下是否冻结仍未排除。The phen-modified Co glass has no long-range magnetic order above 5 K but retains antiferromagnetic interactions; lower-temperature freezing is not excluded.

因此“更容易成玻璃”不等于“更适合储气”。phen 占据空间并降低连接度，导致孔隙损失；它可能更适合可加工膜或金属中心性质调控，但这些应用仍需单独验证。“Easier to vitrify” does not mean “better for gas storage.” phen occupies space and lowers connectivity, reducing porosity. The material may instead suit processable membranes or metal-centred property control, which still require validation.

从 ZIF 扩展到羧酸盐：玻璃的身份也要重新命名Beyond ZIFs, even the identity of the glass changes

Cu(im)₂Cu(im)₂

phen₀.₅ 可在 240 °C 后得到非晶玻璃，Tg 约 39 °C；局部 Cu(im)₂ 相关仍在，phen 配位归属不够确定。phen₀.₅ gives an amorphous glass after 240 °C treatment with Tg around 39 °C. Local Cu(im)₂ correlations remain, while phen coordination is not assigned conclusively.

Ni(inic)₂Ni(inic)₂

phen₂|Ni(inic)₂ 在 230 °C 后完全非晶，Tg 127 °C；主要像 Ni(phen)₂(inic)₂ 分子配位络合物玻璃，作者明确称其为 MOF 衍生的配位络合物玻璃。phen₂|Ni(inic)₂ becomes fully amorphous after 230 °C, with Tg 127 °C. It is mainly consistent with molecular Ni(phen)₂(inic)₂-like units, so the authors call it an MOF-derived coordination-complex glass.

phen₆|Cu₃(btc)₂ 也可出现弱玻璃化转变（约 100 °C），但 PDF 显示 Cu···Cu 桨轮相关消失，羧酸根由双齿桥联转向以单齿为主，说明“玻璃化”伴随了显著的局部化学重构。phen₆|Cu₃(btc)₂ also shows a weak glass transition near 100 °C, but PDF loses the Cu···Cu paddle-wheel correlation and IR indicates predominantly monodentate carboxylates. Vitrification therefore comes with substantial local chemical restructuring.

最强的结论与需要保留的怀疑Strong conclusions and necessary reservations

强结论Strong conclusion

phen 可在熔融态进行金属选择性的配体交换，降低 Tg，并把 Co-ZIF 的网络连接度作为可调变量。phen can mediate metal-selective ligand exchange in the melt, lower Tg and make Co-ZIF network connectivity tunable.

证据边界Evidence boundary

玻璃的结构只能通过 IR、XAS、PDF、NMR、DSC 等互补证据间接重建；部分体系的 phen 配位仍是推断。Glass structure is reconstructed indirectly from complementary IR, XAS, PDF, NMR and DSC evidence; phen coordination remains inferential for some systems.

需要测试更小 modifier、较低负载、孔隙恢复、循环稳定性和膜分离选择性，才能判断工艺价值。Smaller modifiers, lower loading, pore recovery, cycling stability and membrane selectivity are needed to assess process value.
