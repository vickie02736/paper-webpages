改性后，偏好的元素变了Functionalization changes the preferred element

这篇论文将 DGA 衍生基团引入 MIL-101-NH₂(Cr)，在 La、Y、Lu、Eu 的比较中优先捕集 Eu(III)。核心问题不是“能否吸附稀土”，而是固体载体与功能基团组合后，能否改变元素间的分配。This paper introduces DGA-derived groups to MIL-101-NH₂(Cr) and preferentially captures Eu(III) in comparisons involving La, Y, Lu and Eu. The central question is whether combining a solid support and functional groups changes partitioning among elements.

作者称这种改变为“协同”。对比实验支持改性产生选择性差异；它尚未把每一种载体—配体相互作用或协同贡献独立定量。The authors call the change synergistic. Comparisons support altered selectivity after modification, while individual support–ligand interactions and their contributions are not independently quantified.

先制备载体，再引入功能基团Prepare the support, then introduce the functional group

载体由 Cr(NO₃)₃·9H₂O、NaOH 与 2-氨基对苯二甲酸按 4:1:4 摩尔比，在 75 mL 水中配制，150 °C 反应 24 h；洗涤后 120 °C 过夜干燥。后修饰使用二氯甲烷、25 °C、72 h，洗涤后 80 °C 真空干燥 24 h。The support uses Cr(NO₃)₃·9H₂O, NaOH and 2-aminoterephthalic acid at a 4:1:4 molar ratio in 75 mL water, heated at 150 °C for 24 h, washed and dried overnight at 120 °C. Post-functionalization uses dichloromethane at 25 °C for 72 h, followed by washing and vacuum drying at 80 °C for 24 h.

正文表 1 由元素分析报告接枝率 90.13%。C/N 质量比由 7.403 变为 8.433；它是作者的接枝估计，不等于独立测得 90.13% 的所有吸附位点都可用。Main Table 1 estimates 90.13% grafting from elemental analysis, with the C/N mass ratio changing from 7.403 to 8.433. This is the authors’ grafting estimate, not a measurement that 90.13% of every adsorption site is accessible.

合成描述中应保留的差异A discrepancy in the synthesis description

试剂与方法文字称 diglycolic acid，而图 1 与表 1 称 diglycolic anhydride，并画出酸酐开环形成酰胺酸的路线。这里沿用材料名称 MIL-101-DGA，不把两种起始试剂默认为同一个精确合成配方，也不重绘原子连接。The reagent/method text says diglycolic acid, whereas Figure 1 and Table 1 say diglycolic anhydride and illustrate anhydride opening to an amic-acid group. The material name MIL-101-DGA is retained without treating the two starting reagents as one unambiguous recipe or redrawing atomic connectivity.

孔隙数据能说明什么？What do the porosity data establish?

SI 表 S1：BET 面积由 841.236 增至 943.962 m²/g。图 S3 报告孔径特征约 0.567 与 0.590 nm。面积增加支持改性后仍可吸附氮气；它本身不能定位所有功能基团在颗粒外表面，也不能只用裸离子半径证明水合 Eu 的孔内输运或物理吸附机制。SI Table S1 gives BET areas of 841.236 and 943.962 m²/g; Figure S3 reports pore-size features around 0.567 and 0.590 nm. Increased area supports retained nitrogen accessibility. It alone neither locates all groups on the external surface nor establishes hydrated Eu transport or physisorption by comparison with a bare-ion radius.

为何不能用两个去除率直接相除？Why not divide the two removal percentages?

教学假设：同一批次、V = 10 mL、m = 20 mg，忽略沉淀和容器损失。分别假设 Eu 与竞争元素 j 的去除率 r，D = (V/m)·r/(1−r)，β = DEu/Dj。滑块不拟合论文数据。Teaching assumptions: one batch, V = 10 mL and m = 20 mg, ignoring precipitation and container losses. For assumed removal fraction r, D = (V/m)·r/(1−r) and β = DEu/Dj. The sliders do not fit the paper’s data.

当 Ce 趋近于零，D 对残余浓度和检出限极其敏感。100% 去除、竞争元素 D = 0 或两者均为零时，这个有限数值比值不成立；页面显示未定义，不把它写成已测得的无穷大选择性。As Ce approaches zero, D becomes highly sensitive to residual concentration and detection limits. With 100% removal, zero competitor D or both zero, this finite numerical ratio is undefined. The page does not turn it into measured infinite selectivity.

动力学与容量拟合回答不同问题Kinetic and capacity fits answer different questions

拟二级拟合较好不独立证明唯一的化学吸附控制步骤；Langmuir 拟合较好也不直接证明每个真实位点等能或只有一种配位结构。浓度、剂量与实验系列不同的 qe 不能直接比较。A better pseudo-second-order fit does not independently establish one chemisorption-controlled step. A better Langmuir fit does not directly prove identical real sites or one coordination geometry. Values of qe from different concentrations, doses and experimental series are not directly comparable.

哪些证据支持 O 供体参与？Which evidence supports oxygen-donor participation?

作者据这些结果提出羧基参与和约 1:1 的功能基团—Eu 结合解释。光谱位移与表观斜率并不能单独确定配位数、唯一内层结构或硝酸根完整计量；吸附平衡与溶液物种也可能影响斜率。The authors use these results to propose carboxyl involvement and approximately 1:1 functional-group-to-Eu binding. Spectral shifts and apparent slopes alone do not fix coordination number, a unique inner-sphere geometry or complete nitrate stoichiometry. Adsorption equilibria and solution speciation can also affect slopes.

作者借 HSAB 解释 Eu 偏好；本文列出的元素电负性排序并非直接测得水合三价离子的硬度。这个解释没有独立预测整个镧系、真实盐溶液或竞争配体下的分离。The authors invoke HSAB to explain Eu preference. The listed elemental-electronegativity order is not a direct measurement of hydrated trivalent-ion hardness. This interpretation does not independently predict separation across the lanthanide series, real salt solutions or competing ligands.

模拟荧光粉浸出液，还不是完整工业分离Simulated phosphor leachate is a step toward a full process

图 10 在 pH 3.97 的模拟体系中显示 Eu 高去除、Y 部分去除，而图中 Ca、Zn、Cu、Fe 很低。它支持在所测混合物中的 Eu 偏好；不等于已经报告最终产品纯度、全部金属质量守恒或连续床层突破曲线。Figure 10 at pH 3.97 shows high Eu removal, partial Y removal and small plotted uptake of Ca, Zn, Cu and Fe in a simulated mixture. It supports Eu preference in that tested mixture without reporting final product purity, complete metal mass balances or a continuous-bed breakthrough curve.

文中结论使用“continuous SPE”等表述，但所列实验主要是批次比较与重复吸附/解吸。放大前仍需真实浸出液、颗粒成型、酸/盐耐受、浸出和长期流动验证。Although the conclusion uses language including continuous SPE, the reported experiments are primarily batch comparisons and repeated adsorption/desorption. Scale-up still needs actual leachates, shaped particles, acid/salt tolerance, leaching measurements and long-term flow tests.
