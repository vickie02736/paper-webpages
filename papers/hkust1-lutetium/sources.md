## Claim, figure and interaction inventory

1. **Core idea and loading comparison:** abstract, §§2.4, 3.4, 3.6 and main Tables 1–2. HKUST-1 is a Cu/BTC framework; Lu denotes lutetium. The 512.82 mg/g value is Langmuir fitted qm at 303 K, not a measured point. Highest uptake explicitly reported in §3.4 is 485.87 mg/g at initial 500 mg/L. Trace water uses 50 µg/L × 0.4 L = 20 µg total Lu and 0.05 g sorbent: maximum loading 0.4 mg/g if all target is removed. These mass-balance values are teaching calculations, not an additional experiment. No claim of real clinical radioactive-waste validation, recovered product purity or service lifetime.
2. **Preparation and pre-use characterization:** §§2.1–3.1, Figures 1–2. 0.725 g copper nitrate in 20 mL water; 0.420 g BTC in 20 mL DMF; mix/stir 30 min; 120 °C 12 h, filter/wash with water, vacuum-dry 70 °C 24 h. BET 525.99 m²/g and pore volume 0.299 cm³/g. BJH 2.28 nm is reported without equating that result to an intrinsic crystallographic pore size. SEM micrometre scale retained; no unsupported nanoparticle geometry is drawn.
3. **Conditions and pH selector, five branches:** §2.4, §3.2, Figure 3a–b. General dose 5 mg/10 mL = 0.5 g/L, 160 rpm; general method 25 °C, temperature-specific kinetics/isotherms 303/313/323 K. Figure 3a explicitly annotates pH/uptake pairs: 3/113.35, 4/183.01, 5/178.38, 6/180.01, 6.5/182.33 mg/g. All five displayed values rechecked against the saved original figure. Only one point's text is shown at a time; no invented continuous pH curve or error bars. Acidic-condition explanation follows §3.2 (framework degradation/material loss), not an invented H+ mechanism. Subsequent pH 6 selection and precipitation concern are the authors' rationale, not a measured universal precipitation threshold. Source calls pH 6.5 optimal although pH 4 annotation is slightly larger; page does not rank these small differences as a robust optimum.
4. **Temperature selector, three branches and bar chart:** §3.4, main Table 1 and Figure 4c,d. qm at 303/313/323 K = 512.82/500.00/375.94 mg/g. Fixed 0–600 mg/g scale; measured 485.87 shown as a distinct purple bar only at 303 K. No other-temperature experimental maxima are digitized. Table 1 wins over prose's 375.93 at 323 K, with difference explicitly disclosed. qm values do not demonstrate a unique monolayer mechanism.
5. **Kinetic details:** §3.3, Table 1, Figure 4a,b and SI Figure S1. Reported equilibrium about 120 min. Displayed PFO R² = .806/.603/.318; PSO .991/.995/.998; Langmuir .989/.989/.985; Freundlich .793/.923/.926, columns ordered 303/313/323 K. PSO superiority does not independently prove chemisorption. SI S1 is a three-point Arrhenius plot, slope −2053.65 K and Ea 17.074 kJ/mol. No uncertainty meaning or extrapolated physical lifetime assigned. Source physical/chemical mechanistic descriptions conflict; neither category is resolved solely from fitting or a threshold.
6. **Trace-water slider and calculations:** §3.6 and Figure 6b. Fixed source C0 50 µg/L, V 0.4 L, m 0.05 g, pH 6; background Ca/Mg/Na/Cl/sulfate/iodide. Complete background concentrations are absent from main and this short SI. Slider Ce 0–50 µg/L in .5 steps is explicitly hypothetical. R = 100(C0−Ce)/C0, removed micrograms = (C0−Ce)V, qe = removed µg/1000/m. At Ce 5: 90%, 18 µg and .36 mg/g; at zero: 100%, 20 µg and .4 mg/g; at 50: zero removal/loading. Concentration loss assumed to be adsorption, no sampling/precipitation/container losses. Main prose below-5 claim is qualified: late figure scatter is approximately 3–6 µg/L, with some above 5. No point digitization, all-time compliance, column performance, clinical 177Lu test or saturation time inferred.
7. **Competition selector, two branches:** §3.5, Figure 5a and SI Figure S3. Seven-ion case Lu/Ca/Mg/Co/Zn/Na/K each 100 mg/L, pH 6: qualitative Lu preference only, no digitized competitor bars; approximately 96 mg/g is not 96% removal. Three-REE mixture each 500 mg/L at pH 6: Lu 239.35, Yb 153.17 and Gd 95.64 mg/g from main text, visually consistent with SI S3. Fixed 0–300 mg/g scale, exact reported values, no invented errors. SI error-bar statistics/replicate count not defined. Equal-mass feeds are not equimolar. Higher mass uptake does not directly establish molar preference, Kd ratios, separation factors or eluate purity.
8. **Reuse selector, two branches:** §§3.5–3.6, Figure 5b. 20 mg loaded sorbent in 30 mL of .05 M EDTA–Cu for 12 h, then filter/wash/dry. Explicit text cycle-two/three values 312.25/120.52 mg/g at initial 500 mg/L. The first figure bar is visually consistent with the separately reported fresh-material measurement 485.87, but is not separately numerically annotated. Chart labels that value **fresh-material reference**, avoiding fabrication of an exact separately measured first-cycle point. Absolute scale 0–550 mg/g; relative scale 0–100%, normalized to the reported reference: 100/64.266.../24.804...%. Values display 100/64.27/24.8%. Normalization and approximate narrative 64.3/24.8% are explicit teaching calculations. Three adsorption cycles are not three regeneration operations; capacity declines strongly.
9. **Desorption clarification:** §3.6, Figure 6a. Caption calls it desorption efficiency, but vertical axis qe mg/g compares parent/MeOH/HCl-treated samples. Page does not infer stripping recovery percentages from this axis, nor exclude a mechanism without considering framework damage.
10. **Irradiation selector, three branches:** §3.6 and all SI Figure S2 panels. Calibrated electron-beam beta irradiation at 50/100/200 kGy. S2a irradiated bars retain substantial uptake but sit below the unirradiated bar; no exact percentages or equivalence/significance claims digitized. S2b major FTIR features remain but are not identical. S2c–f retain recognizable polyhedral outlines, while irradiated surfaces are visibly rougher; all scale bars 8 µm. Selected fields do not quantify bulk defect fractions. No irradiated-series PXRD/BET/Cu-BTC mass balance supplied. Pre-irradiation exposure is not continuous operation in radioactive effluent.
11. **Mechanism selector, three branches:** §3.7, Figure 7a–d. Author-assigned FTIR 538 cm⁻¹ Lu–O feature and spectral changes support the proposed O-group participation, without unique coordination geometry/number. Original figure label La–O is disclosed as inconsistent. XPS relative Cu 3.99→3.16 at%, Lu .11 at% after uptake, reported as surface association rather than atom exchange stoichiometry. Partial substitution/ion exchange remains the authors' proposal; release/balance/structure needed for degree of exchange. No original or AI atomic structure redrawn.
12. **Thermodynamic detail:** §3.4, Table 2 and Eqs 6–7. DeltaH −34.694 kJ/mol, deltaS +.135 kJ/mol/K, deltaG −6.217 at 303 K; H−TS = −75.599 kJ/mol is a transparent algebraic consistency check, not a sourced correction. Reported deltaG at 313/323 K is −7.650/−9.995. Positive entropy versus entropy-decrease prose also conflicts. K units differ (Table 1 L/g vs explanatory text L/mg), and standard-state treatment is not established. No thermodynamic calculator or asserted corrected parameters.
13. **Limits/related links:** implications drawn from the specific evidence gaps, explicitly framed as follow-up experiments. No comparisons under unmatched conditions or claims about other HKUST-1 formulations.
