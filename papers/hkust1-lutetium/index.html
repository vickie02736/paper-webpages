从“捕集到 Lu”到“Lu 替换 Cu”，还差什么？What separates Lu capture from proof of Cu replacement?

  1. 先确认液体浓度改变Confirm the liquid concentration change

    按开始和结束的 Lu 浓度计算减少量，并用无材料等空白检查容器、沉淀等损失。浓度下降先支持从水相移走。Calculate loss from initial and final Lu concentrations, using blanks such as no-sorbent controls to assess vessel and precipitation losses. A decrease first supports removal from water.

  2. 再看固体局部信号Inspect local signals in the solid

    红外光谱（FTIR）和 XPS 的变化支持 Lu 与固体相关，作者据此提出含氧位点参与。峰的归属仍不是完整配位结构。Changes in infrared spectra (FTIR) and XPS support Lu association with the solid; the authors propose oxygen-site involvement. Peak assignments do not supply a complete coordination structure.

  3. 检验交换的收支Test the exchange balance

    要证明原 Cu 被 Lu 替换，需要溶液与固体中的 Cu、Lu 总量及电荷／结构证据。只相减 XPS 原子百分数不能完成这个衡算。Proving replacement of Cu by Lu requires total Cu/Lu in solution and solid plus charge and structural evidence. Subtracting XPS atomic percentages does not close that balance.

第三步是讲解者指出的进一步验证需要，不是声称本文已完成全部收支。作者的离子交换提案与直接观察分开保留。The third step identifies further validation needs; it does not claim the paper completed all balances. The authors’ exchange proposal remains distinct from direct observations.
