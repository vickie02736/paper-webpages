把两个 MOF 结合，改善什么？What improves when two MOFs are combined?

论文用 PVP 调节 UiO-66-NH₂ 与 ZIF-8 的复合形貌，研究 Nd、Eu、Gd、Er 的捕集。核心价值是把水稳定性、位点与吸附表现放在一起考虑。The paper uses PVP to control UiO-66-NH₂/ZIF-8 composite morphology and studies Nd, Eu, Gd and Er capture. Its central question joins water stability, sites and uptake performance.

形貌Morphology

UiO-66-NH₂ 核与片状 ZIF-8 壳，或在 ZIF-8 表面分布 UiO 颗粒。A UiO-66-NH₂ core with a lamellar ZIF-8 shell, or UiO particles on ZIF-8.

容量Capacity

复合物优于 UiO 载体，但 SI 中纯 ZIF-8 的拟合容量更高。Composites exceed UiO, while pure ZIF-8 has greater fitted capacities in the SI.

耐久Durability

酸浸后保留更多固体；第四、五轮再生仍出现功能与结构损失。More solid survives acid exposure, while later regeneration runs lose function and structure.

“稳健”是相对所测条件的比较结果，不代表材料在任意酸度、无限循环中保持完整，也不代表容量、选择性和寿命都同时最优。Robustness is a comparison within tested conditions. It does not establish intact material at every acidity or through unlimited cycles, or simultaneous optimal capacity, selectivity and lifetime.

PVP 与溶剂共同改变组装PVP and solvent jointly change assembly

U6N 是 UiO-66-NH₂。在 U6N@ZIF-8-x 中，作者将 U6N 描述为核、ZIF-8 为片状壳。x = 20、30、40、50 表示投入的 PVP 质量（mg），不是最终复合物中的质量百分比。U6N denotes UiO-66-NH₂. For U6N@ZIF-8-x, the authors describe a U6N core with a lamellar ZIF-8 shell. x = 20, 30, 40 or 50 is the PVP feed mass in mg, not the final composite mass percentage.

合成从 10 mg U6N、19 mL 甲醇、PVP 开始，加入 0.2 g 硝酸锌及 0.25 g 2-甲基咪唑的甲醇溶液。PVP 越多，SEM 中片层聚集越明显；甲醇用量减半则得到 ZIF-8/U6N，即 U6N 分布在十二面体 ZIF-8 表面。Synthesis starts with 10 mg U6N, 19 mL methanol and PVP, followed by 0.2 g zinc nitrate and a methanolic solution of 0.25 g 2-methylimidazole. More PVP accompanies greater lamellar aggregation in SEM. Halving methanol instead gives ZIF-8/U6N, with U6N distributed on dodecahedral ZIF-8.

SEM/TEM、元素分布、两组衍射特征和 XPS 支持复合形成。PVP 降低界面能并引导生长是作者的解释；这些表征没有独立解析所有界面原子连接。本页用文字比较形貌，不另造晶体拓扑图。SEM/TEM, elemental maps, diffraction features of both components and XPS support composite formation. Reduced interface energy and directed growth by PVP are the authors’ interpretation; the measurements do not independently resolve every interfacial atomic connection. Morphologies are compared in words without inventing crystal topology.

把 mg/g 换成 mmol/gConvert mg/g into mmol/g

原文将 Nd 249.90 mg/g 写成 1.73 mol/g，也将混合液 Er 213.48 mg/g 写成 1.28 mol/g。按摩尔质量换算，二者应约为 1.73 和 1.28 mmol/g；原单位相差 1000 倍。下面仅做单位教学，不生成新的吸附数据。The source expresses Nd 249.90 mg/g as 1.73 mol/g and mixed-feed Er 213.48 mg/g as 1.28 mol/g. Molar-mass conversion instead gives approximately 1.73 and 1.28 mmol/g, a factor of 1000 in the unit. The exercise below converts units without generating new uptake measurements.

采用 Er 摩尔质量 167.259 g/molUsing Er molar mass 167.259 g/mol

换算式：q(mmol/g) = q(mg/g) / 167.259；再除以 1000 得到 mol/g。容量的质量单位与物质的量单位各有用途，不能混用来放大材料表现。Conversion: q(mmol/g) = q(mg/g) / 167.259; dividing by another 1000 gives mol/g. Mass-based and molar capacities have distinct uses and must retain their units.

如何把容量、选择性与稳定性放在一起Read capacity, selectivity and stability together

单离子拟合容量回答“在这套浓度与模型中可装多少”；混合 REE 曲线回答竞争偏好；SI 对数选择性比较 REE 与过渡金属的分配；浸泡残余质量和循环 PXRD 回答耐久的不同侧面。它们都不能互相替代。Single-ion fitted capacities describe loading in a particular concentration/model setting. Mixed-REE curves address competition. SI logarithmic selectivity compares partitioning against transition metals. Immersion mass and cycling PXRD address different aspects of durability. These metrics cannot substitute for each other.

工艺发展还需要真实工作液的离子、酸与有机物背景，完整物料衡算、回收产品纯度、溶剂消耗、成型颗粒和长期穿透。此文提供了形貌调节与多性能权衡的出发点。Process development also needs actual feed chemistry, full mass balances, product purity, solvent use, shaped particles and long-term breakthrough. This work offers a starting point for morphology control and performance trade-offs.
