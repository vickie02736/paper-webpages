先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

先认识两种框架与中间的聚合物Meet the two frameworks and the polymer used between them

U6N 是 UiO-66-NH₂，含锆节点和带氨基的有机连接体；ZIF-8 是锌与咪唑类连接体形成的多孔网络。PVP 是聚乙烯吡咯烷酮，一种用于辅助组装的聚合物。复合物不是把两种粉末的优点简单相加。U6N is UiO-66-NH₂, with zirconium nodes and amino-bearing organic linkers; ZIF-8 is a porous zinc–imidazolate network. PVP, polyvinylpyrrolidone, is the polymer used to assist assembly. A composite does not simply add the advantages of two powders.

核与壳Core and shell

作者依据显微形貌描述一种核外长出片状壳的组装。它说明颗粒尺度的分布，不等于已解析所有界面化学键。The authors describe a core with a lamellar outer shell from microscopy. This concerns particle-scale arrangement, not a complete atomic map of interface bonds.

吸附量与进料量Uptake and feed inventory

mg/g 是每克固体上的稀土质量。浓度下降能换算吸附量的前提，是知道体积、固体质量以及是否存在其他损失。mg/g is REE mass per gram of solid. Converting a concentration drop requires volume, solid mass and checks for other losses.

对数选择性Logarithmic selectivity

把目标与竞争者的分配比相除，再取 log。相等的分配得到 0，不是 1；这与常见直接分离因子有不同基线。Divide target partitioning by competitor partitioning, then take log. Equal partitioning gives 0, not 1, unlike a direct separation factor.

先看“比了什么”，再看“有没有取对数”Read what is divided, then whether a logarithm is applied

第一层：每个离子的分配First: partitioning of each ion

qₑ/Cₑ 比较每克固体留下的量与液体剩余浓度。两离子使用一致单位、同一个竞争体系，才可进一步比较。qₑ/Cₑ compares loading per gram with concentration remaining in liquid. Use consistent units and the same competition system for both ions before comparing them.

第二层：比值取 logSecond: apply log to the ratio

SI 式 11 再对两者比值取 log。比值为 1 时，log(1)=0；正值表示偏向稀土。0.71 不能按“直接比值小于 1”解释。SI Eq. 11 then applies log to their ratio. A ratio of 1 gives log(1)=0; a positive result favors the REE. Thus 0.71 must not be read as a direct ratio below 1.

SelREE/X = log[(qₑ/Cₑ)REE ÷ (qₑ/Cₑ)X]SelREE/X = log[(qₑ/Cₑ)REE ÷ (qₑ/Cₑ)X]

qₑ 用 mg/g，Cₑ 用 mg/L，因此单个 qₑ/Cₑ 用 L/g；两者相除后单位抵消。X 是共存金属。原式没有给出 log 底数，本页不把值反变换成精确倍数。qₑ uses mg/g and Cₑ mg/L, so each qₑ/Cₑ has units L/g; the ratio cancels those units. X is a competing metal. The source does not specify the log base, so values are not back-transformed to exact fold preferences.

这些值仍不是去除百分数或最终产品纯度。切换两种进料时，是稀土从 20 增至 40 mg/L、其他金属维持 20 mg/L；不能把该差异说成竞争金属浓度增加。These values are neither removal percentages nor final product purity. Across the two feeds, REEs increase from 20 to 40 mg/L while other metals remain at 20 mg/L; this must not be described as increasing competitor concentration.

如何把容量、选择性与稳定性放在一起Read capacity, selectivity and stability together

单离子拟合容量回答“在这套浓度与模型中可装多少”；混合 REE 曲线回答竞争偏好；SI 对数选择性比较 REE 与过渡金属的分配；浸泡残余质量和循环 PXRD 回答耐久的不同侧面。它们都不能互相替代。Single-ion fitted capacities describe loading in a particular concentration/model setting. Mixed-REE curves address competition. SI logarithmic selectivity compares partitioning against transition metals. Immersion mass and cycling PXRD address different aspects of durability. These metrics cannot substitute for each other.
