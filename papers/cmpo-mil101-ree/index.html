DE DECKER ET AL. · 2016 · CMPO@MIL-101(Cr)

CMPO@MIL-101：
Eu 吸附与稳定性证据CMPO@MIL-101:
Eu uptake and stability evidence

耐酸的载体、牢固连接的螯合基团，以及混合进料中的分配偏好，需要各自的证据。这篇早期研究把它们放在同一套材料设计中，但 SI 也显示了比较时必须保留的批次与指标差异。Acid-resistant support, retained chelating groups and preference in a mixed feed each need evidence. This early study combines them in one material design, while its SI reveals batch and measurement distinctions that must be retained.

Jeroen de Decker · Jeriffa de Clercq · Pieter Vermeir · Pascal van der Voort
Journal of Materials Science 51, 5019–5026 (2016)

把螯合基团固定在耐酸多孔载体上Anchor chelating groups on an acid-resistant porous support

框架承担支撑A framework support

MIL-101(Cr) 提供多孔骨架。吸附剂必须同时保留骨架与连接螯合基团的化学键；只保留 PXRD 峰并不回答全部问题。MIL-101(Cr) supplies the porous scaffold. Both the framework and the linkage to chelating groups must survive; retained PXRD peaks answer only part of the stability question.

功能基团承担结合Functional groups bind ions

经氯甲基化、二胺接枝和酰胺偶联，引入作者称为 CMPO 型的含磷螯合基团。P 含量用于估计负载量约 0.5 ± 0.15 mmol/g。Chloromethylation, diamine attachment and amide coupling introduce what the authors call CMPO-type phosphorus-containing chelates. P content estimates a loading of about 0.5 ± 0.15 mmol/g.

混合进料检验偏好A mixed feed tests preference

Eu/Y/Zn 竞争实验报告 Eu/Y 分离系数 3.2、Eu/Zn 为 8.5。它们是分配系数之比，不是回收百分比。Eu/Y/Zn competition gives reported Eu/Y and Eu/Zn separation factors of 3.2 and 8.5. These are ratios of distribution coefficients, rather than recovery percentages.

三步改性，孔隙与负载逐步改变Three functionalization steps change porosity and loading

先在芳香连接体上建立锚点，再通过 1,4-二氨基丁烷连接臂与二乙基膦酰乙酸偶联。这里保留作者的“CMPO 型”称呼，不把本研究的基团等同于每一种萃取剂中的 CMPO 分子。An anchoring site on the aromatic linker is followed by a 1,4-diaminobutane spacer and coupling to diethylphosphonoacetic acid. “CMPO-type” retains the authors’ terminology without equating this group to every CMPO extractant molecule.

完整合成与表征数据Synthesis context and complete characterization data

MIL-101 母体为 Cr(NO₃)₃·9H₂O/对苯二甲酸水热合成，210 °C、8 h。氯甲基化在硝基甲烷中进行，100 °C、10 h；二胺步骤为 DMF、80 °C、24 h。最终 CDI 偶联在无水 DMF、惰性气氛、室温进行，混合后反应 12 h。The parent is hydrothermally synthesized from Cr(NO₃)₃·9H₂O and terephthalic acid at 210 °C for 8 h. Chloromethylation uses nitromethane at 100 °C for 10 h; the diamine step uses DMF at 80 °C for 24 h. Final CDI coupling uses anhydrous DMF under inert atmosphere at room temperature, followed by 12 h reaction after mixing.

12.46 mg/g 是拟合上限，不是任意条件的吸附量12.46 mg/g is a fitted maximum, not uptake under every condition

单离子等温线Single-ion isotherm

50 mg 吸附剂 / 50 mL 溶液，pH 4.00 ± 0.05，25 °C、200 rpm、24 h，重复两次。图 7 的 Langmuir 拟合最大容量为 12.46 mg Eu/g，约 0.082 mmol/g。The isotherm uses 50 mg adsorbent in 50 mL at pH 4.00 ± 0.05, 25 °C, 200 rpm for 24 h, in duplicate. Fig. 7 gives a Langmuir fitted maximum of 12.46 mg Eu/g, about 0.082 mmol/g.

对照与解释边界Controls and interpretation

在初始 Eu 100 mg/L、pH 4、24 h 的对照中，氯甲基化和胺化中间体没有报告可测 Eu 吸附。结果支持完整功能基团的重要性；不能把它推广为所有胺化 MIL-101 都不吸附 Eu。At initial Eu 100 mg/L, pH 4 and 24 h, the chloromethylated and aminated intermediates have no reported Eu uptake. This supports the completed functional group in this study, without establishing zero Eu uptake for every aminated MIL-101.

作者同时报告 Langmuir 与 Freundlich 拟合的 R² > 0.95。较好的拟合不能单独确认唯一配位机制。pH 5 仅额外测试了初始 50 与 100 mg/L 的两个点，未纳入原拟合；这不是完整的 pH 性能地图。Both Langmuir and Freundlich fits have reported R² > 0.95. Good fits alone do not establish a unique coordination mechanism. The pH 5 check adds only two points, at initial 50 and 100 mg/L, excluded from the original fit; it is not a full pH performance map.

来源：正文“Adsorption experiments”“Sorption isotherm”、图 7。摘要将容量舍入为 12.5，结论写为 12.45 mg/g；本页主要采用结果段的 12.46 mg/g，不把这些末位差异解释为独立实验。Sources: main “Adsorption experiments”, “Sorption isotherm” and Fig. 7. The abstract rounds capacity to 12.5 and the conclusion gives 12.45 mg/g. This page follows the results value 12.46 mg/g without treating the last-digit differences as separate experiments.

分离系数描述相对分配，回收率还取决于固液比Separation factors describe partitioning; recovery also depends on dosage

三离子混合液中 Eu、Y、Zn 各约 50 mg/L，pH 5、25 °C、24 h，V/m = 400 mL/g。表 2 报告 Kd：Eu 149、Y 48、Zn 17 mL/g。每个 Kd 都属于这组竞争条件。The three-ion feed contains about 50 mg/L each of Eu, Y and Zn, at pH 5, 25 °C for 24 h and V/m = 400 mL/g. Table 2 reports Kd = 149, 48 and 17 mL/g for Eu, Y and Zn. These coefficients belong to this competitive setting.

分配系数固定量尺 0–160 mL/g；原表未给出误差线。Distribution coefficients use a fixed 0–160 mL/g scale; no error bars are supplied in the table.

Eu/Y：3.2Eu/Y: 3.2

Eu 相对 Y 的分配偏好，属于不同稀土之间的一次竞争试验。它不是 3.2% 回收率，也没有验证相邻镧系元素的普遍分离。A reported Eu-over-Y partition preference in one competitive REE test. It is not 3.2% recovery and does not establish general separation of adjacent lanthanides.

Eu/Zn：8.5Eu/Zn: 8.5

Eu 相对 Zn 的偏好，回答稀土与一种过渡金属竞争的问题。不能与 Eu/Y 当作同一分离难度。The reported Eu-over-Zn preference addresses competition between a REE and one transition metal. It is a different separation problem from Eu/Y.

Raw replicate concentrations are not supplied to reconstruct the difference. This page retains both reported quantities rather than presenting the teaching calculation as a correction.

约五小时的平衡时间属于特定进料The roughly five-hour equilibration belongs to one feed

动力学实验使用 250 mg / 250 mL、Eu 50 mg/L、pH 4.0 ± 0.05、25 °C、1000 rpm，取样 0–24 h。作者称约 5 h 达到平衡，并报告 PFO 拟合 qₑ = 9.93 mg/g、k = 0.0071 min⁻¹、R² = 0.965。Kinetics uses 250 mg in 250 mL, Eu 50 mg/L, pH 4.0 ± 0.05, 25 °C and 1000 rpm, sampled over 0–24 h. The authors describe equilibrium within about 5 h and report PFO fitted qₑ = 9.93 mg/g, k = 0.0071 min⁻¹ and R² = 0.965.

dq/dt = k(qₑ − q)
q(t) = qₑ [1 − exp(−kt)]

条形量尺为 0–100% 的拟合平衡进度。第二式是对第一式取 q(0)=0 得到的教学解，不是新增实测点。300 min 处模型仍低于其渐近上限；有限时间内“达到平衡”是实验操作判断，不能当成数学上精确的 100%。The bar spans 0–100% of fitted equilibrium progress. The second equation solves the first with q(0)=0 for teaching, rather than adding measurements. At 300 min the model remains below its asymptote; finite-time experimental equilibration is an operational judgement, not an exact mathematical 100%.

9.93 mg/g 是这组动力学条件下的拟合平衡量，与等温线拟合的最大容量 12.46 mg/g 不同。PFO 的拟合优度也不能单独确定传质或化学反应中的唯一限速步骤。The 9.93 mg/g kinetic-run fitted equilibrium amount differs from the 12.46 mg/g isotherm maximum. A PFO fit alone does not establish a unique transport or chemical rate-limiting step.

稳定性证据必须连同样品和处理条件阅读Read stability evidence with the sample and exposure

选择一项证据，看它检验了什么，以及哪些结论仍没有得到验证。三组观察不能合并为同一批材料的一条寿命曲线。Choose an evidence set to see what it tests and what remains unverified. These observations cannot be combined into one lifetime curve for a single batch.

可以据此设计更严格的回收试验A basis for a more complete recovery test

结果支持在 MIL-101(Cr) 上固定含磷螯合基团，并在指定酸性水溶液中优先分配 Eu。论文没有展示多轮吸附–解吸再生、实际废液、连续柱突破曲线或最终产品纯度。长期母体耐酸不替代这些验证。The results support anchoring phosphorus-containing chelates on MIL-101(Cr) and preferential Eu partitioning in the specified acidic solutions. Repeated adsorption/desorption regeneration, real waste feeds, continuous-column breakthrough and final product purity are not demonstrated. Long-term acid exposure of the parent does not substitute for these tests.

比较另一篇材料时，需核对 Langmuir/BET 面积口径、螯合基团负载、Eu 浓度、pH、固液比，以及容量是模型上限还是特定进料下的实测量。更大的每克容量，不足以单独证明位点更有效或工艺更可行。When comparing another material, check Langmuir versus BET area, chelate loading, Eu concentration, pH, solid/liquid ratio, and fitted maximum versus measured feed-specific uptake. A larger capacity per gram alone does not establish more effective sites or a more viable process.
