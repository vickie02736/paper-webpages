## Teaching mass balance: derivation and boundaries

This interactive calculation is **explainer analysis**, not an experimental dataset, fitted physical simulation, adsorption isotherm or prediction of an attainable operating point.

Let rᵢ be the fraction of ion i adsorbed from its initial amount. Assuming a closed system with constant liquid volume and only aqueous and adsorbed phases, Cₑ,ᵢ = (1 − rᵢ)C₀,ᵢ. Substitution into SI Eq. 7 gives:

`Kd,i = [rᵢ / (1 − rᵢ)] · V/m`

For both ions in the same batch, V/m cancels in SF. With α = Kd,L/Kd,H and a selected heavy-ion adsorbed fraction h = rᴴ:

`α = [rᴸ/(1 − rᴸ)] / [h/(1 − h)]`

`rᴸ = αh / (1 − h + αh)`

For **equal initial moles** of the two ions, the displayed quantities are:

- L recovery into the adsorbed solid: `rᴸ`.
- L molar purity of the adsorbed solid: `rᴸ/(rᴸ + h)`.
- H molar purity in the filtrate: `(1 − h)/(2 − rᴸ − h)`.

The slider varies h from 0.1% to 99.0%; reset returns to 1.0%. The selected paper SF is held constant to expose the information missing from SF alone. This assumption does **not** establish that all such h values can be reached by this material. The calculation omits saturation, sorbent dose, changing competition, hydration/speciation, precipitation, kinetic limitations and elution losses. “Solid purity” refers to the two adsorbed REE components, not total solid mass including the MOF. “Recovery into solid” is not recovery of a final isolated product. Kd is in mL/g when V is in mL; using V in L gives L/g and requires conversion.

## DFT evidence and interpretation

SI p. 6 specifies VASP, PBE GGA, PAW, a 520 eV plane-wave cutoff, a Γ-centred 1 × 1 × 1 mesh and forces below 0.02 eV/Å. SI Eq. 10 defines:

`Eads = E(metal-loaded NCU-1) − E(NCU-1) − E(isolated metal ion)`

Main p. 5 reports −3.29 eV for Pr, −2.58 eV for Dy and −1.66 eV for Al. More negative means more favourable under that particular reference and model. Main Figure 5's caption and labels identify a/d as Pr, b/e as Dy, c/f as Al; this identification is used in the reader.

The reported calculation is not a complete aqueous free-energy cycle. No full hydration-reservoir treatment, desolvation free energy, entropy contribution or exhaustive configurational/speciation sampling is supplied. No Boltzmann conversion from these energies to experimental SF is performed. Charge redistribution and Bader charges are calculated analyses, not measured ion charges or unique proof of binding strength. The explainer does not repeat the questionable main-text wording equating a larger positive Bader charge with “more electrons obtained.”

## Source discrepancies and editorial decisions

- **Capacity:** main p. 2 and SI Table 3 disagree; both are exposed with exact numbers. No explanation of the discrepancy is invented.
- **pH maxima:** main p. 4 gives a general statement about maxima at 4.5, but SI Table 5 shows differing heavy-REE maxima. The page uses the table's per-ion values and limits the general conclusion to strong light-REE preference around 4.5.
- **Stability pH:** SI p. 3's method lists 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 and 13, whereas SI Figure 5 and main p. 2 describe 2–13. The page's 2–13 statement follows the figure caption/main claim and does not claim separate verified pH 1 stability.
- **DFT panel order:** one main p. 5 sentence lists Al/Dy/Pr for Figure 5a–c, contrary to the figure caption and labelled artwork. The reader follows the caption/artwork: Pr/Dy/Al.
- **SI Figure 69 identity:** SI p. 42 captions its model as Dy, while main p. 5 refers to Figure 69 for Al. This model is not used to assign a particular ion's distances in the page.
- **FT-IR directions:** main p. 4 calls 1574.5 → 1552.4/1554.5 cm⁻¹ a blue shift and 1519.2 → 1531.9/1530.2 cm⁻¹ a red shift, opposite standard direction terminology. The page summarises spectral changes without reproducing those labels.
- **Kinetic units:** SI p. 4 prose gives PFO k₁ units inconsistent with its equation; Table 4 gives min⁻¹. No erroneous k₁ units are carried into the page.
- **Other transcription issues:** SI Table 3 prints the Eu Freundlich Kꜰ entry as “54.8.0”; the page does not use it. SI p. 2 gives a 100 K crystal-collection temperature, while Table 1 reports 293(2) K; the page does not claim a temperature. The column method's “Figure S9” cross-reference is followed to the actual apparatus Figure 53 rather than relying on that erroneous cross-reference.

The distinction between paper measurements, author-proposed mechanisms, teaching calculations and explainer analysis is maintained on the page. The main evidence limits are adjacent-element selectivity, independent phase purity/recovery, actual aqueous binding/speciation, long-term dissolution/durability and scale-up performance. No validation or deployment result is asserted in this scientific ledger.
