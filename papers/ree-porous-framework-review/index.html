多孔框架不是只有一种连接，也不是只有一种用法Porous frameworks differ in bonding and use

MOF 用金属与有机连接体组成网络；COF 主要由有机单元通过共价键连接。两者都可以有孔，但不能据“共价”二字就保证耐水。本文既讨论粉末吸附，也讨论膜中的传输与分离。MOFs connect metals and organic linkers; COFs mainly connect organic building blocks through covalent bonds. Both can be porous, but “covalent” alone does not guarantee water resistance. The review covers powder adsorption as well as membrane transport and separation.

配位结合Coordination binding

基团上的氧或氮等部位可参与与金属离子的结合。离子原先周围的水、其他竞争物和 pH 也会影响这个过程。Sites such as oxygen or nitrogen in a group can bind a metal ion. Its surrounding water, competitors and pH also influence the process.

印迹Imprinting

制作材料时引入目标作模板，再移除模板，希望留下更适合目标的环境。必须验证模板已去净及对照材料的表现。Introduce a target as a template during preparation, then remove it to leave a potentially better-matched environment. Template removal and non-imprinted controls require verification.

膜的两侧Two sides of a membrane

进料来到膜的一侧；穿过膜的液体是透过液，未穿过的部分是截留侧。离子没有透过，不代表都已经储存在膜内。Feed reaches one side; liquid that crosses is permeate and material remaining upstream is on the retained side. An ion not passing through is not necessarily stored in the membrane.

以稳定性、竞争对象与测量定义读懂多孔框架综述。Read the porous-framework review through stability, competitors and metric definitions.

这篇综述同时讨论 MOF 和 COF，并把“稀土与非稀土分开”和“不同稀土彼此分开”分成两条主线。水中稳定性是两者的前提，配位、交换、电荷与孔道则共同参与捕集。This review covers both MOFs and COFs, separating REE/non-REE discrimination from inter-REE separation. Aqueous stability underpins both, while coordination, exchange, charge and pores jointly contribute to capture.

吸附指标与膜指标分开解释Interpret adsorption and membrane metrics separately

留在吸附剂上，与没有穿过膜，不能画等号Being adsorbed and not crossing a membrane are different

批次吸附Batch adsorption

比较接触前后液体浓度，再用液体体积与吸附剂质量换算 mg/g。应排除沉淀、容器损失和材料本身释放。Compare liquid concentration before and after contact, then use liquid volume and sorbent mass to obtain mg/g. Exclude precipitation, vessel loss and release from the material.

膜截留Membrane rejection

常见截留率用 1 − 透过液浓度/进料浓度表示。高截留可让离子留在上游溶液；还要测流量、膜内积累和两侧物料。A common rejection definition is 1 minus permeate concentration divided by feed concentration. High rejection can retain ions upstream in solution; flow, membrane accumulation and material on both sides also matter.

这里的截留率是通用读图定义；采用具体论文数值时仍需核对其定义与条件。不能把 90% 膜截留改写成“膜吸附了 90% 的进料稀土”，也不能把透过选择性直接当作 Kd 比值。The rejection expression is a general reading definition; numerical use requires the specific paper’s definition and conditions. A 90% membrane rejection must not be relabeled as adsorption of 90% of feed REEs, nor transport selectivity as a Kd ratio.

自测：COF 由共价键连接，就能默认耐强酸强碱吗？Check: does covalent bonding make every COF resistant to strong acid and base?

不能。不同连接化学对水和酸碱的响应不同，例如硼氧类连接可能水解。必须查具体连接、处理条件、时长及处理后的结构与功能。No. Linkage chemistry changes response to water, acid and base; boron–oxygen linkages can hydrolyze, for example. Check the actual linkage, treatment, duration and post-treatment structure and function.

怎样把“有空腔”变成可检验的识别主张How to test a claim of template recognition

  1. 制备并去模板Prepare and remove the template

    模板参与形成结合环境，随后用规定处理移除。若有模板残留，测得的目标含量可能包含原先就有的目标。The template helps form a binding environment and is then removed by a defined treatment. Residual template can contribute target that was already present before adsorption.

  2. 比较非印迹材料Compare a non-imprinted control

    尽量匹配其他制备与测试条件。差异才更能关联到印迹步骤，而不是不同面积、化学组分或酸洗损伤。Match other preparation and test conditions as closely as possible. Differences can then be related more convincingly to imprinting rather than area, composition or acid-treatment damage.

  3. 加入真正的竞争者Add relevant competitors

    分别测目标与竞争离子的分配，并检查模板清除和循环后材料。高单离子容量本身不能证明识别。Measure partitioning of both target and competitors, and check template removal and the cycled solid. High single-ion capacity alone does not prove recognition.

这是依据综述的印迹实例提出的读证据方式，不宣称本页已独立验证所有被引 COF。孔的气体测量尺寸也不能直接当作水合离子的硬筛孔。This organizes evidence reading around the review’s imprinting examples; it does not claim independent verification of all cited COFs. Gas-derived pore dimensions also are not automatically hard apertures for hydrated ions.
