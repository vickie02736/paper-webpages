把萃取剂放入 MOF，三个问题要分开An extractant in a MOF raises three separate questions

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

同一颗粒里，有支架也有功能分子One particle contains both a support and functional molecules

MIL-101 的 Cr 节点与有机连接体提供多孔支架。加入的含磷有机分子提供新的结合环境。REE 是稀土元素，本文重点比较 Nd、Gd、Er（钕、钆、铒）；Er³⁺ 指带三正电荷的铒离子。Cr nodes and organic linkers in MIL-101 provide the porous support. Added phosphorus-containing organic molecules provide new binding environments. REEs are rare-earth elements; Nd, Gd and Er denote neodymium, gadolinium and erbium, with Er³⁺ the triply charged erbium ion.

C、H、TC, H and T

分别代表 Cyanex-272、HDEHP 和 TBP 三种含磷萃取剂。C/H 有可失去质子的酸性基团；TBP 是中性分子，不能把三者的机制画成完全相同。Cyanex-272, HDEHP and TBP are three phosphorus-containing extractants. C/H have acidic groups that can lose protons; TBP is neutral, so their mechanisms must not be drawn identically.

萃取剂与固体吸附剂Extractant and solid sorbent

萃取剂常用于帮助离子转入另一相；这里将其引入多孔固体。最终效果取决于它是否可达、是否保留，以及水溶液条件。An extractant helps transfer ions into another phase; here it is introduced into a porous solid. Performance depends on accessibility, retention and solution conditions.

浸出Leaching

材料中的组分进入周围液体。测溶液的 Cr 可追踪含铬组分，P 可追踪含磷组分；元素浓度不自动给出完整分子身份。Components enter the surrounding liquid. Solution Cr tracks chromium-containing components and P phosphorus-containing components; elemental concentration does not identify complete molecules by itself.

读完应能解释：为什么同样保留 PXRD 峰，酸再生后的三种材料仍可有不同的吸附表现。You should explain why the three materials can differ in post-acid uptake despite retaining PXRD features.

更高负载、更强偏好和更好循环，可能属于不同改性剂。Higher loading, stronger preference and better reuse can belong to different modifiers.

这项工作把三种有机磷萃取剂引入 MIL-101(Cr)，比较 Er、Gd 和 Nd 的吸附。最高 Er 负载、混合离子中的偏好和酸再生后的功能保持，各自需要不同实验。This study introduces three organophosphorus extractants into MIL-101(Cr) and compares Er, Gd and Nd uptake. Maximum Er loading, preference in ion mixtures and function after acid regeneration each require a different experiment.

容量Capacity

H50 的最高实测 Er 负载较高，但不是通用分离效率。H50 has the highest reported measured Er loading; this is not a general separation efficiency.

选择性Selectivity

T50 对 Er 的偏好较强，但吸附相组成不等于进料去除率。T50 strongly favors Er, but sorbed-phase composition is distinct from feed removal.

循环Reuse

C50 的循环保持较好；晶体仍在也不保证功能基团仍在。C50 retains function better across cycles; retained crystallinity alone does not establish retained functional groups.

先固定功能分子，再分别检查支架和分子Introduce functional molecules, then check support and molecules separately

  1. 制备改性液Prepare the modification mixture

    材料名中的“50”是相对于甲苯的改性剂投料质量百分数。它不是最终固体中实测 50% 萃取剂。“50” is the modifier feed mass percentage relative to toluene. It is not a measured 50% extractant content in the final solid.

  2. 接触、洗涤、表征Contact, wash and characterize

    将功能分子引入 MIL-101 后，用孔隙、光谱与组分信息检查变化。不同批次或正文/SI 的面积差异仍分别记录。After introducing functional molecules into MIL-101, examine pores, spectra and composition. Batch differences and main/SI area discrepancies remain separately recorded.

  3. 经过吸附和酸再生再检查Recheck after adsorption and acid regeneration

    不仅看固体衍射，也看含磷基团光谱和液体元素浓度。支架有序、功能分子保留与吸附恢复是三种证据。Examine solid diffraction, phosphorus-group spectra and solution elemental concentrations. Support order, functional-molecule retention and recovered uptake are three distinct observations.

这不是保证分子永久固定的路线。T50 在首次再生后吸附明显失败，不能因剩余衍射峰把它归为功能完全稳定。This route does not guarantee permanent immobilization. T50 loses uptake after the first regeneration and cannot be called fully functionally stable from retained diffraction alone.

动手区分：去除率与吸附相组成Try it: removal versus sorbed-phase composition

教学假设：沿用混合离子实验的五种金属、每种初始 50 mg/L、10 mL 溶液与 10 mg 吸附剂；给定任意去除率，假设溶液损失全部来自吸附，并令四种竞争离子的去除率相同。这些滑块不是论文测量或预测模型。Teaching assumptions: use the five-metal feed, 50 mg/L each, 10 mL solution and 10 mg sorbent. Set hypothetical removal fractions, assume all solution loss is adsorption, and assign the same removal to all four competitors. The sliders are neither paper measurements nor a predictive model.

qₑ,Er = mEr,ads / msorbent
fEr,ads = mEr,ads / (mEr,ads + Σmcompetitor,ads)

默认示例：Er 去除 80%，吸附 Er 为 0.400 mg，四种竞争离子合计 0.020 mg；因此 Er 占吸附金属约 95.24%。两个百分数描述不同问题。当所有吸附量为零时，组成百分数没有定义。Default example: 80% Er removal gives 0.400 mg sorbed Er and 0.020 mg total sorbed competitors, so Er constitutes about 95.24% of sorbed metals. The percentages answer different questions. When all sorbed amounts are zero, the composition fraction is undefined.

Main Figure 4c compares subsequent uptake; SI Figures S12–S14 provide PXRD, SEM and FTIR after three cycles. A single characterization does not establish retained complete sorbent function.

评价应分三层：晶体与颗粒是否保留、P 基团是否保留、下一次能否继续吸附。正文称循环后晶体结构仍在，而 T50 的 P=O 特征与吸附功能下降；因此“有衍射峰”不足以证明完整吸附剂可再生。Assess three layers: retained crystal/particle structure, retained P-containing groups, and uptake in the next use. The main text reports retained crystallinity but loss of T50 P=O features and uptake. Diffraction peaks alone therefore do not establish regenerability of the complete sorbent.

想知道的量：原负载损失比例Desired quantity: fraction of original loading lost

还需要液体体积、原始固体中 P 总量，以及释放物种或分子是否完整的信息。一个浓度数值本身没有“原负载总量”的分母。This also needs liquid volume, original total P in the solid and information about released species or molecular integrity. A concentration alone lacks the original-loading denominator.

29958.7 μg/L ÷ 1000 = 29.9587 mg/L29958.7 μg/L ÷ 1000 = 29.9587 mg/L

1000 μg = 1 mg。这只换单位，没有把 P 换成 TBP 分子质量，也没有把浓度换成百分数。1000 μg equals 1 mg. This only changes units; it neither converts P into TBP molecular mass nor concentration into percentage loss.

P 浸出与酸循环中的功能下降是互补线索；不同实验的 pH、处理和时间仍需匹配，不能把一条 pH 浸出曲线当作酸循环的完整因果证明。P leaching and functional loss during acid cycling are complementary clues, but pH, treatment and time must be matched. A leaching-versus-pH series is not a complete causal demonstration of cycling failure.

哪些结果仍需谨慎解释？Which results still require caution?

SI 表 S5 显示不同模型给出不同拟合容量：例如 C50 的 Langmuir qₘ 为 41.17 mg/g，而 Toth qₘ 为 59.96 mg/g；这两者都不是正文最高实测负载 48.91 mg/g。拟合优度和模型假设不直接证明位点种类或化学吸附机制。SI Table S5 gives model-dependent fitted capacities: C50 Langmuir qₘ = 41.17 mg/g versus Toth qₘ = 59.96 mg/g. Neither is the main-text maximum measured loading of 48.91 mg/g. Fit quality and model assumptions do not directly establish site types or a chemisorption mechanism.

表 S6、图 S8–S10 比较两种进料浓度下的动力学拟合。线性化与非线性拟合的参数和 R² 不同，不用“伪二级拟合好”作为唯一速控机理证据。论文没有给出真实工艺流量、长期柱实验或混合料洗脱产品纯度。Table S6 and Figures S8–S10 compare kinetic fits at two feed concentrations. Linearized and nonlinear fits yield different parameters and R² values. A favorable pseudo-second-order fit alone does not identify the rate-controlling mechanism. The paper does not establish process flow rates, long-term column behavior or eluate product purity from mixtures.

正文与 SI 的 BET 数值差异、可能作用方式的示意性质，以及质量负载比与分配系数比的区别均保留说明。结论适用于文中的批量条件；更强 Er 偏好仍要与浸出及再生保持共同评价。The main/SI BET discrepancies, proposed interaction schemes and distinction between uptake ratios and distribution-coefficient ratios remain explicit. Conclusions apply to the batch conditions studied; stronger Er preference must be weighed against leaching and retention after regeneration.
