同样叫“水稳定”，有的材料只接触了空气，有的经历了沸水；有的只保留衍射峰，有的保留了可用孔隙。这篇观点论文试图给这些不同证据一套更清楚的描述语言。“Water stable” can describe exposure to room air or boiling water, retained diffraction peaks or retained accessible pores. This perspective proposes a clearer vocabulary for these different tests and outcomes.

Benjamin S. Gelfand · George K. H. Shimizu
Dalton Transactions 45, 3668–3678 (2016)

首次在线：2015-11-27。下文解释作者的分类提案，不将其描述为已普遍采用的量化标准。First online: 27 November 2015. The page explains the authors’ classification proposal without presenting it as a universally adopted quantitative standard.

一个“稳定”标签包含多个问题One stability label conceals several questions

接触了什么？What was the exposure?

液态水与水蒸气不等价。温度、相对湿度、溶液成分、pH 与暴露时长都会改变检验的含义。Liquid water and vapour are different exposures. Temperature, relative humidity, solution composition, pH and duration change what the test establishes.

保留了什么？What survived?

结晶有序、质量、气体可及孔隙与目标功能是不同对象。单一表征不能自动代表所有对象。Crystalline order, mass, gas-accessible porosity and target function are different observables. One measurement cannot automatically stand for all of them.

后来还会下降吗？Does degradation continue?

一次处理后的容量下降，可能继续发展，也可能达到较稳定的剩余状态；需要后续暴露来区分。A loss after the first treatment may continue or approach a more stable residual state. Further exposure is needed to distinguish these outcomes.

论文的核心贡献是将“暴露条件”和“处理后证据”分开描述，并要求补充时间、反复暴露与活化信息。数字和字母一起使用，仍然不能省掉这些记录。The contribution is to separate exposure conditions from post-treatment evidence, while retaining time, repeated exposure and activation information. A number-letter grade does not replace those records.

为什么不能只写“室温空气中放置”？Why is “stored in room air” incomplete?

环境相对湿度可能变化；未完全活化的孔道中，残余客体还可能阻止水接近内部位点。密闭容器中材料与空气重新平衡后，湿度也不一定等于外部环境。Ambient relative humidity can vary. Guests left in incompletely activated pores may also hinder water access to internal sites. Re-equilibration in a closed vessel can make its humidity differ from the surrounding air.

原文讨论饱和盐溶液、水蒸气吸附仪和湿度烘箱等控制方法；方法选择影响条件的可知程度和内部孔道是否真正接触到水。The article discusses saturated salt solutions, water-sorption instruments and humidity ovens. The delivery method affects how well exposure is known and whether water reaches internal pores.

依据正文表 3。这些类别没有给出“保留多少百分比算保留”的统一数值阈值。A 不是 100% 完整的证明；B 也不代表材料一定失去全部应用功能。These categories follow Table 3, which gives no universal numerical cutoff for “retained.” Category A does not prove complete integrity, and B does not necessarily imply loss of every useful function.

“未测量孔隙”与“测量显示没有孔隙”必须区分。下面的教学工具只在两项证据都已输入时给出类别，不将缺测补成零。Unmeasured porosity differs from measured loss of porosity. The teaching tool assigns a category only after both observations are supplied; missing measurements are not converted to zero.

三类表征为什么互补？Why are these assessments complementary?

先做质量与组分检查Start with mass and composition

材料可能部分溶解，但剩余部分仍给出原来的衍射峰。正文建议检查质量变化与母液中的组分，避免只观察“活下来的固体”。Part of a sample can dissolve while the residue still gives the original peaks. The article recommends mass checks and analysis of components in solution, rather than examining only the surviving solid.

PXRD：看到有序部分PXRD sees ordered material

非晶组分增加、表面缺陷或孔口阻塞，未必都在常规衍射图中显著表现。相变也不必然等于所有孔隙消失。An amorphous fraction, surface defects or blocked pore entrances may not be obvious in routine diffraction. A phase change also need not eliminate all porosity.

气体吸附：探针也重要Gas adsorption depends on the probe

77 K N₂ 与接近室温的 CO₂，受到扩散与框架动态的影响可能不同。需报告气体、温度、活化及孔径模型，不能只写“BET 保留”。N₂ at 77 K and CO₂ near room temperature can respond differently to diffusion and framework motion. Report gas, temperature, activation and pore-size model instead of an isolated BET-retention claim.

对于催化、传感或吸附分离，还应直接测试对应功能。本文将“是否仍有用途”与“结构是否完全保留”分开，允许讨论部分降解后仍可使用的材料。Catalysis, sensing and adsorption separation also need direct functional tests. The paper distinguishes remaining usefulness from complete structural preservation, allowing meaningful discussion of partially degraded but functional materials.

首次下降之后，继续测试Continue after the first loss

起点Starting pointCALF-28：不同制备样品的表面积约 700–800 m²/g。CALF-28 preparations have surface areas of about 700–800 m²/g.
首次暴露First exposure80 °C、90% RH、24 h 后，约降至 500 m²/g，孔径分布也改变。After 80 °C, 90% RH for 24 h, area falls to about 500 m²/g and the pore-size distribution changes.
后续暴露Further exposure在文中讨论的后续测试范围内，面积与孔径分布不再继续变化。Within the further tests discussed, area and pore-size distribution no longer change.

这个例子不代表“没有发生变化”，而是提示一次损失后可能留下更稳定的状态。相对地，文中 MIL-53(Al) 沸水案例的 γ-AlO(OH) 表面层会随处理增长，说明仅看首次下降不足以判断长期趋势。This example records an altered material that can approach a more stable residual state. In contrast, the discussed boiling-water MIL-53(Al) case develops a growing γ-AlO(OH) surface layer, showing why the first loss alone cannot establish the later trajectory.

如何使用这一提案，同时保留边界？Use the proposal while retaining its limits

论文建议以 24 h 作为初始处理时间，并明确记录后续暴露。时长不是六级编号内置的变量；24 h 与一周的同一编号试验仍须分别描述。The article proposes 24 h as an initial treatment and explicit records of further exposure. Duration is not built into the six-level number, so a 24 h test and a week-long test at the same level still require separate descriptions.

原文内部有一些标注差异：§5.2 写温和条件为 20 °C / 50% RH，表 2 与 §5.5 为 25 °C / 50% RH；部分案例的分级也与条件或表征定义不完全一致。本页的教学工具采用表 2–3，不自动把这些案例标注作为标准答案。The source contains differences: §5.2 lists mild conditions at 20 °C / 50% RH, while Table 2 and §5.5 use 25 °C / 50% RH. Some case labels also do not fully match the condition or assessment definitions. The teaching tool follows Tables 2–3 rather than treating every case label as an answer key.

论文特别提到生物相关研究可另外测试磷酸盐缓冲液条件；这与纯水测试不同。用于 REE 吸附时，还要另行记录酸度、盐、竞争离子、配体和节点金属浸出及再生条件。本文自身没有给出 REE 吸附容量或选择性。The article separately proposes phosphate-buffer conditions for biologically relevant tests, which differ from pure water. REE adsorption additionally needs acidity, salts, competing ions, linker/node-metal leaching and regeneration conditions. The perspective itself reports no REE adsorption capacity or selectivity.
