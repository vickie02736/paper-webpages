01

为什么相邻稀土难分？Why are neighbouring rare earths hard to separate?

同为三价离子，性质相近。材料既要让目标离子到达内部位点，又要让位点对不同离子产生足够不同的亲和力。Most are trivalent ions with similar chemistry. A material must make its internal sites accessible and make those sites discriminate between ions.

容量：能装多少？Capacity: how much fits?

qₘ 是单离子等温线拟合得到的最大吸附量。容量大，说明能装得多；它本身不能证明从混合物中选得准。qₘ is the maximum uptake fitted to a single-ion isotherm. A large capacity describes how much can be held; it does not establish discrimination in a mixture.

亲和力：更愿意去哪？Affinity: which phase is favoured?

Kd 比较固相吸附量与液相剩余浓度；它随 pH、浓度和混合组分变化。KL 则来自单离子 Langmuir 拟合。Kd compares solid-phase uptake with residual liquid concentration and depends on conditions. KL comes from a separate single-ion Langmuir fit.

分离：两者差多少？Separation: how different are they?

二元分离因子 SF 比较同一混合体系中两种离子的分配。还需结合进料组成、吸附比例和回收率，才能讨论产物流的组成。A binary SF compares the distribution of two ions in the same mixture. Feed composition, the fraction adsorbed and recovery are also needed to discuss an output stream.

研究推进：此前 NCU-1 的纳米陷阱主要区分轻／重稀土。本研究进一步引入离子型骨架中的可交换反离子，尝试兼顾内部传输与相邻元素识别。Research progression: earlier NCU-1 nanotraps mainly separated light from heavy rare earths. This study adds exchangeable counterions in an ionic framework to combine internal transport with discrimination between neighbouring elements.

结构与合成细节Structure and synthesis details

ATZ 为 4-氨基-4H-1,2,4-三唑，BTC 为苯三甲酸。材料结晶于 P2₁/c 空间群；阴离子链通过水分子氢键等作用组装。正文给出的矩形通道为 9.9 × 7.8 Å，层间口袋约 4.4 Å；CO₂ 孔径分析中心约 0.46 nm。这三种描述不能直接当作水合离子的同一个硬筛孔。ATZ is 4-amino-4H-1,2,4-triazole and BTC is benzene tricarboxylic acid. The material crystallizes in P2₁/c; anionic chains assemble through interactions including water-mediated hydrogen bonds. The reported rectangular channel is 9.9 × 7.8 Å, the interlayer pocket ~4.4 Å, and the CO₂-derived pore-size distribution centres near 0.46 nm. These are not interchangeable hard-sieve apertures for hydrated ions.

SI §2.1：BTC、ATZ 与锌盐各约 0.10 mmol，在乙腈／水 2/8 mL 中于 140 °C 加热 3 天，再冷却 1 天。“一步分离”指吸附分离的操作概念，并非材料在瞬间制成。SI §2.1 uses about 0.10 mmol each of BTC, ATZ and zinc salt in 2/8 mL acetonitrile/water, heated at 140 °C for 3 days and cooled for 1 day. “One-step separation” refers to the adsorption operation, rather than instantaneous synthesis.

离子交换证据能说明什么？What does the exchange evidence establish?

SI 表 S10：在 pH 5.5、6 h 后，含 Ce 溶液中的 Zn 为 19.889 ppm、Ce 由 51.867 降至 21.603 ppm；纯水对照中的 Zn 仅 0.004 ppm。它支持“Ce 去除伴随 Zn 释放”，但不唯一确定交换位点、价态补偿或完整反应计量。SI Table S10: after 6 h at pH 5.5, Zn in the Ce solution is 19.889 ppm while Ce falls from 51.867 to 21.603 ppm; the water control contains only 0.004 ppm Zn. This supports Ce removal accompanied by Zn release, without uniquely resolving exchange sites, charge compensation or full reaction stoichiometry.

Zn²⁺ 与 Ln³⁺ 电荷不同。作者根据 XPS／EDS 提出硝酸根、亚硝酸根参与电荷补偿；这里不画成未经验证的 1:1 配平反应，也不把所有水合 Ln³⁺ 固定画成六配位。Zn²⁺ and Ln³⁺ carry different charges. The authors propose nitrate/nitrite incorporation for charge compensation from XPS/EDS. The schematic does not assert a balanced 1:1 reaction or a universal six-coordinate hydration shell for Ln³⁺.

哪些结论成立，哪些问题还在？What is supported, and what remains open?

把作者报告、机制假说与讲解者分析分开，才能将这项设计继续用于下一轮研究。Separating reported results, mechanistic proposals and explainer analysis makes the design useful for the next study.

分离因子与纯度Separation factor and purity

二元 SF 支持特定条件下的差异分配。实际多组分体系中单一稀土产品的纯度、回收率、洗脱步骤与物料衡算仍需进一步验证。Binary SF supports differential partitioning under specific conditions. Single-element product purity, recovery, elution and mass balance in practical multicomponent feeds need further validation.

机制的唯一性Uniqueness of the mechanism

XPS、FT-IR、PXRD、Zn 释放、Cryo-EM 与计算共同支持作者的解释，但未唯一确定水相中每个 Ln 的配位结构。计算中的 Ln···NH₂ 距离不是直接测出的 Ln–N 键。Spectra, diffraction, Zn release, Cryo-EM and calculations support the proposed explanation without uniquely resolving each aqueous adsorption structure. Calculated Ln···NH₂ contacts are not directly measured Ln–N bonds.

时间与规模Time and scale

约 15 min 来自单离子动力学；二元批次方法使用 6 h。柱突破结果还需要流量、压降、产品切割和长期运行数据，才能评价处理能力。The ~15 min result comes from single-ion kinetics; the binary batch protocol uses 6 h. Column throughput requires flow, pressure drop, fraction collection and long-term operating data.
