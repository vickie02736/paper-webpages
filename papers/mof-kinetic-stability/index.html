MOF 稳定性：
结构为何能留下来？MOF stability:
why does the structure persist?

材料有更容易到达的最终状态，并不表示它会立刻变成那个状态。这篇综述用“重排有多难”解释多孔框架为何能在某些腐蚀性气体中继续工作。A material having a more favorable final state does not mean it transforms immediately. This review asks how difficult rearrangement is to explain persistence in some corrosive gases.

Adam J. Rieth · Ashley M. Wright · Mircea Dincă
Nature Reviews Materials 4, 708–725 (2019)

多孔结构可以被动力学“留住”Kinetics can preserve a porous structure

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

终点倾向与到达速度，是两件事A preferred destination and the speed of reaching it differ

MOF 的连接点和分子连接体围出孔道。气体可以留在孔里，也可能攻击连接。即使变成较致密的结构更有利，原子仍需经过连接断开、移动和重新结合的过程。Junctions and molecular linkers enclose pores in a MOF. Gas can occupy these pores or attack their connections. Even when a denser structure is more favorable, atoms must still break, move and remake connections to reach it.

热力学倾向Thermodynamic preference

在指定条件下，比较起点与终点的自由能。自由能综合能量与热运动相关的因素；较低的终点不直接告诉我们转变要多久。Compare the free energies of starting and final states under specified conditions. Free energy combines energetic and thermal contributions; a lower final state does not directly give the time needed to reach it.

动力学障碍Kinetic barrier

转变途中要经过较难达到的排列。相对于起点的自由能上升，就是所讨论的能垒；它影响转变速度。Transformation passes through a harder-to-reach arrangement. Its free-energy rise above the starting state is the barrier discussed here; this affects transformation speed.

可逆捕集Reversible capture

先吸入气体，再在再生条件下释放，并保留下一轮工作的材料。只测一次高吸附量还没有完成这个检验。Capture gas, release it under regeneration conditions and retain material for the next run. One high uptake measurement does not complete this test.

本页的能量图回答“改变哪一部分会让重排更难”，容量图回答“在什么测试中留住了多少氨”。两张图不能互相换算。The energy diagram asks which change makes rearrangement harder. The capacity chart asks how much ammonia is retained in a particular test. These plots cannot be converted into one another.

难以发生的配位重排，可以保护多孔相。理解金属惰性、异裂键强度与气体化学，再判断高容量是否意味着可再用。Slow coordination rearrangements can preserve porosity. Connect metal inertness, heterolytic bond strength and gas chemistry before interpreting high capacity as reuse.

这篇 2019 年综述用动力学稳定性理解 MOF 在腐蚀性、可配位气体中的表现。量热研究表明，若干脱除客体后的多孔固体，相对于相同组分构成的致密相处于亚稳态；它们仍可因重排能垒而长时间存在。This 2019 review uses kinetic stability to interpret MOFs exposed to corrosive, coordinating gases. Calorimetry shows that several evacuated porous solids are metastable relative to dense phases of the same constituents; barriers to rearrangement can nevertheless preserve them.

热力学：比较两个状态Thermodynamics: compare states

终态与初态的自由能差描述转变的驱动力。比较必须说明组成、客体和条件；空孔相与含溶剂相不是同一个参照。The free-energy difference between final and initial states describes a driving force. Composition, guests and conditions define the comparison; empty and solvent-filled pores are different references.

动力学：比较状态与过渡态Kinetics: compare a state with a barrier

即使终态更有利，也需要经过配位键断裂、交换或几何重排。提高相应能垒，可以延缓多孔相的变化。Even a favourable final state may require bond dissociation, substitution or geometric reorganization. Raising the relevant barrier can delay change.

多孔相 → 同组成致密相，与 MOF + 水 → 氢氧化物/质子化连接体，是不同反应。本文的能量框架不能替代每个水解体系的自由能计算，也不证明所有 MOF 在所有条件下都具有同一相稳定性。Porous-to-dense conversion at fixed composition differs from reaction with water to form hydroxides/protonated linkers. The energy framework does not replace reaction-specific hydrolysis free energies or establish identical phase stability for every MOF under every condition.

水合离子的交换速率，是线索而非寿命Aquo-ion exchange is a clue, not a framework lifetime

详细比较与操作可在机制层展开；本节保留核心判断。Detailed comparisons and controls open at the Mechanism level; the core conclusion remains here.

综述用金属水合配合物的水交换速率说明“动力学惰性”。这些溶液配合物跨越很宽的速率范围，但其 s⁻¹ 数值不能直接换算成 MOF 在湿气中的使用寿命。真实节点还受配位几何、连接体和反应环境约束。The review uses water-exchange rates of aquo complexes to illustrate kinetic inertness. Their rates span a wide range, but those solution-complex s⁻¹ values cannot directly yield MOF lifetimes in humid gases. Geometry, linkers and the environment constrain real nodes.

为何 Cu²⁺ 是重要例子？Why is Cu²⁺ instructive?

Cu²⁺ 配合物可以具有很高的热力学形成常数，却仍发生快速配体交换。综述用 Jahn–Teller 畸变及轴向交换解释这种区别，提醒不能把 Irving–Williams 趋势当成固体耐久性排序。Cu²⁺ complexes can have high thermodynamic formation constants yet rapid ligand exchange. The review discusses Jahn–Teller distortion and axial exchange, illustrating why Irving–Williams trends do not directly rank solid durability.

连接体也在改变反应路径Linkers also alter reaction paths

对某些后过渡金属，较强供电子的唑类连接体可增强异裂金属–配体键。但继续增强碱性也可能增加质子化/水解的驱动力；不能无限外推“碱性越强越稳定”。For some late transition metals, strongly donating azolate linkers strengthen heterolytic metal–ligand bonds. Further basicity can also favour protonation/hydrolysis, so stability cannot be extrapolated indefinitely from basicity.

更多连接、螯合式约束与位阻也能抑制重排；疏水保护同时可能限制目标气体进入。它们改变的是相关路径和可接近性，而不是给所有反应加上同一个固定能垒。Additional connections, chelate-like constraints and shielding can hinder rearrangement. Hydrophobic protection may also obstruct target gases. These effects are pathway- and access-dependent, rather than a fixed barrier added to every reaction.

换一种气体，可能换了一套化学Different gases can introduce different chemistry

同一材料，“稳定”也随压力和时间而变Stability of one material depends on pressure and time

Co₂Cl₂BBTA 在约 1 mbar NH₃ 条件下可以保持，而在 1 bar NH₃ 下失去结晶性。这说明高纯气体等温线与痕量穿透实验考察不同暴露情形。Ni 同类材料更惰性，却不能因此把它在一种气体下的证据直接扩展到所有混合进料。Co₂Cl₂BBTA can persist near 1 mbar NH₃ but loses crystallinity at 1 bar NH₃. Pure-gas isotherms and trace breakthrough tests therefore probe different exposures. Its more inert Ni analogue still needs testing for each mixture.

判断“可再用”还需跟踪解吸是否完整、孔隙与局部配位是否恢复、循环中容量如何改变，以及固体是否产生不可逆化学产物。单次高去除量可能来自消耗性反应。Reuse requires tracking complete desorption, recovery of porosity/local coordination, capacity across cycles and irreversible products. High removal in one exposure may result from a consumptive reaction.

为什么不把加速暴露直接变成年限？Why not convert accelerated exposure directly into years?

综述提出严苛气体中的短期稳定性有望帮助筛选更温和条件下的长期材料，但没有给出适用于全部 MOF 的外推公式。反应路径、湿度和扩散变化可能改变速率控制步骤。The review proposes harsh-gas screening as a possible guide to longer service under milder conditions, but gives no universal extrapolation formula. Changes in pathways, humidity and transport can change the rate-controlling step.

吸得多、放得出、结构留下来Uptake, release and structural survival are separate

卤素实例把这三个问题区分得很清楚。NH₂ 官能化材料可通过芳环氯化及酸捕获增加 Cl₂ 去除，但该路径不可循环。Co₂Cl₂BTDD 则通过 Co²⁺/Co³⁺ 氧化还原配位捕获 Cl₂ 或 Br₂；加热可释放卤素并恢复母体。Halogen examples distinguish these questions. NH₂-functionalized materials may remove Cl₂ through ring chlorination and acid capture, an irreversible route. Co₂Cl₂BTDD instead uses Co²⁺/Co³⁺ redox coordination to capture Cl₂ or Br₂, followed by thermal release and recovery of the parent framework.

主文图 6 及相关段落报告 Br₂ 捕获–释放进行了 3 次循环。这里仅以文字解释反应类型，不重画原子的配位结构，也不由三次循环推断工业使用寿命。Main Fig. 6 and its discussion report three Br₂ capture–release cycles. This page explains the reaction type in text, without redrawing atomic coordination or inferring an industrial lifetime from three cycles.

对 REE 水相吸附，动力学提供什么启发？What can kinetics contribute to aqueous REE capture?

以下为讲解者延伸：选择更惰性的支撑节点，可能帮助保存固定在框架上的 REE 配体；但支撑骨架稳定、REE 配位快、以及可逆解吸，是三个需要同时验证的目标。本文没有给出 REE 容量、选择性或液相交换速率。Explainer extension: inert support nodes may help retain framework-bound REE ligands. Support durability, rapid REE binding and reversible stripping are three separate objectives requiring verification. This review reports no REE capacity, selectivity or liquid-phase exchange rates.

保护支撑Preserve the support

在真实进料和解吸剂中监测节点金属、连接体及官能团释放；不要把金属水合离子的交换速率当作固体浸出速率。Monitor node metal, linker and functional-group release in actual feeds and stripping agents. Aquo-ion exchange rates are not solid leaching rates.

保留分离功能Retain separation function

检查水合 REE 的扩散、竞争离子选择性及可回收量。延缓骨架反应并不保证目标离子能够迅速到达、结合并再次释放。Check hydrated-ion transport, mixed-ion selectivity and recoverable amount. Slower framework reactions do not guarantee fast target access, binding or release.

如何限定综述的证据？Bound the review evidence

这是截至 2019 年的综述。材料性能汇自不同研究，不能把其“最高”或“首次”描述当作今天的实时纪录。本文没有把多孔相的亚稳性扩展成每一种含客体、所有温度下的普遍自由能结论。This is a review of the literature available in 2019. Performance comes from different studies; historical “highest” or “first” statements are not current records. Metastability is not generalized to every guest-loaded material at every temperature.
