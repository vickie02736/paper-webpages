稀土回收面前，还有大量铵Recovering REEs in the presence of abundant ammonium

论文把 DTPA 衍生基团通过酰胺化引入 MIL-101(Cr)-NH₂，研究 La、Eu、Er 的吸附，以及高浓度 NH₄⁺-N 共存时能否继续回收稀土。The paper introduces DTPA-derived groups to MIL-101(Cr)-NH₂ through amidation, studying La, Eu and Er uptake and recovery in the presence of concentrated NH₄⁺-N.

位点Sites

引入含羧基配体后，面积下降但捕集提高。Carboxyl-containing modification increases capture despite lower area.

负载Loading

更多配体可增加滴定酸性位点，同时降低孔隙可用性。More ligand can increase titratable acidity while reducing pore accessibility.

工作液Feed

模拟含铵体系支持耐受性；没有证明镧系之间的竞争分离。Simulated ammonium feeds support tolerance, without establishing competitive lanthanide separation.

这里的“选择性”主要指 REE 相对铵背景的保留吸附，不是 La / Eu / Er 同时共存时的分离系数，也不是经过真实工业废水验证的产品纯度。Selectivity here primarily concerns retained REE capture against an ammonium background. It is not a measured separation coefficient for a mixed La/Eu/Er feed or product purity validated with actual industrial wastewater.

后修饰让可用化学环境发生变化Post-functionalization changes the chemical environment

载体在 150 °C 反应 24 h；正文后修饰配方为 MIL-101(Cr)-NH₂ 0.5 g、DTPA 二酸酐 0.5 g、DMF 50 mL，在 60 °C 反应，防止水进入，洗涤后 60 °C 真空干燥 12 h。The support is prepared at 150 °C for 24 h. The main post-functionalization recipe uses 0.5 g MIL-101(Cr)-NH₂, 0.5 g DTPA dianhydride and 50 mL DMF at 60 °C while excluding water, followed by washing and vacuum drying at 60 °C for 12 h.

DTPA 含多个羧酸与胺供体；接枝后其局部环境、质子状态与可及性会改变。不能把游离 DTPA 的供体总数直接当成每个表面 Eu 的实测配位数。FTIR、XPS、滴定与孔隙变化提供互补线索。DTPA contains multiple carboxylic-acid and amine donors. Grafting changes local environments, protonation and accessibility. The donor count of free DTPA is not a measured coordination number for each surface-bound Eu. FTIR, XPS, titration and porosity changes provide complementary evidence.

面积最大，不代表捕集最多The greatest area does not give the most uptake

The 60 °C material performs best within this comparison. Uncertainties absent from this prose series are not invented from the plotted bars.

作者把更高捕集与羧基含量联系，图 2e 的相关性支持这种解释。温度同时改变负载、孔隙和表面环境；相关性不能独立排除这些共同变化。The authors relate improved capture to carboxyl content, supported by correlations in Figure 2e. Temperature also changes loading, porosity and surface environments; correlation alone does not exclude these covarying effects.

配体越多，不一定越好More ligand is not always better

这是制备时 DTPA / 载体的进料质量比，不是最终测得的接枝质量百分比。正文报告滴定羧基由 0.317 增至 0.473 mmol/g，但 1:1 材料的捕集表现最好；作者提出孔阻塞与团聚的解释。These are DTPA/support feed mass ratios, not measured final grafting mass percentages. The prose reports titratable carboxyl groups increasing from 0.317 to 0.473 mmol/g, while the 1:1 material captures best. The authors propose pore blockage and aggregation.

Boehm 滴定用 NaHCO₃ 消耗估计酸性位点，是操作条件相关的测量；它不独立计数所有可及配位位点。正文结论又写 0.357 mmol/g，与正文主要讨论的 0.317 不同，本页不将二者混合。Boehm titration estimates acidic groups from NaHCO₃ consumption and is operationally defined. It does not independently count every accessible coordination site. The conclusion instead quotes 0.357 mmol/g, differing from the main discussion’s 0.317; they are not merged here.

pH 同时改变表面和溶液pH changes both surface and solution

正文不同位置把有利条件写为 pH 3.0–4.0、峰值 pH 4 或 pH 3.5。作者讨论羧基质子化、表面电荷与 REE 水解物种的影响；本页保留这个条件区间，不重建一条不存在完整数表的精确 pH 曲线。Different passages give favorable pH 3.0–4.0, a peak at pH 4 or pH 3.5. The authors discuss carboxyl protonation, surface charge and REE hydrolysis species. The condition range is retained without reconstructing an exact pH curve from an absent numerical table.

正文称等电点约 3.5，但图 3h 红线在所绘 pH 2–3 之间过零；图 3g 的实际横轴是 NH₄⁺ 浓度，与 pH 图注不一致。这些不一致限制了用该图精确证明 pH 机制。The prose calls the isoelectric point about 3.5, whereas Figure 3h’s red curve crosses zero between the plotted pH 2 and 3. Figure 3g actually has an NH₄⁺ concentration axis despite its pH caption. These inconsistencies constrain precise pH-based mechanistic conclusions.

正文称铵氮减少约 1–2%，SI 图 S14 是吸附前后浓度对照。小的百分比变化不足以独立证明铵完全不占位，也不是完整的产品纯度、吸附相组成或检测误差评价。NH₄⁺-N 按 N 的质量报告，不能未经换算当作 NH₄⁺ 质量。The prose reports around 1–2% ammonium-N decrease; SI Figure S14 compares before/after concentrations. 去除率高，单位固体的负载也高吗？Does high removal mean high loading per gram?

教学质量守恒：假设初始 REE = 20 mg/L，固定去除率为 75%，只改变剂量 d。则 q = 20 × 0.75 / d。本例不预测真实剂量曲线；真实去除率本来会随剂量变化。Teaching mass balance: assume initial REE = 20 mg/L and fixed removal of 75%, varying only dose d. Then q = 20 × 0.75 / d. This does not predict an experimental dose curve; real removal can change with dose.

Spectral changes, loading correlations and speciation calculations support an interpretation without directly resolving each REE’s coordination number, ligand stoichiometry or every parallel contribution. 把含铵耐受性带到真实工作液之前Before transferring ammonium tolerance to an actual feed

论文的实用启示是把位点化学与孔隙可用性一起优化，而不是无限增加配体。要进入实际工艺，还需真实废水、共存稀土和过渡金属竞争、溶液/固相质量守恒、组分浸出、酸耗、成型颗粒及长期穿透实验。The practical lesson is to optimize site chemistry together with pore accessibility instead of increasing ligand indefinitely. Actual processing needs real wastewater, competing REEs/transition metals, solution/solid mass balances, component release, acid use, shaped particles and long-term breakthrough tests.
