“少量背景变化”还要乘上很大的起始量A small background fraction must be multiplied by its starting amount

DTPA 是二乙烯三胺五乙酸；本研究使用其衍生物修饰带氨基的 MIL-101，引入额外结合环境。铵是 NH₄⁺，属于另一种共存离子。页面必须同时记录稀土去除、铵变化和材料自身是否改变。DTPA is diethylenetriaminepentaacetic acid; a derivative modifies amino-bearing MIL-101 to add binding environments. Ammonium, NH₄⁺, is a different coexisting ion. REE removal, ammonium changes and changes to the sorbent all require separate records.

NH₄⁺–NNH₄⁺–N

按铵中氮元素质量报告浓度。1000 mg N/L 不是 1000 mg 整个 NH₄⁺/L，也不是稀土的摩尔浓度。Concentration reported by the mass of nitrogen in ammonium. 1000 mg N/L is not 1000 mg of whole NH₄⁺ per liter and is not an REE molar concentration.

滴定基团量Titrated group content

用已知浓度试剂反应，再根据消耗量估算酸性基团。它是依赖操作和模型的总量，不直接证明每个基团都能被水合稀土接近。React with a known reagent and estimate acidic groups from its consumption. This is an operational estimate, not proof that every group is accessible to hydrated REEs.

剂量与负载Dose and loading

剂量是每升液体投入多少克固体；负载是每克固体留下多少毫克目标。同样去除率下，固体用得更多，平均每克分到的量会更少。Dose is grams of solid added per liter; loading is milligrams of target retained per gram. At the same removal fraction, more solid divides the retained target among more grams.

读完应能区分对模拟铵背景的耐受、对其他杂质的选择性，以及最终回收产品的组成。You should distinguish tolerance to simulated ammonium background, selectivity against other impurities and final recovered-product composition.

从羧基、孔隙与工作液条件，理解 DTPA 改性的收益和边界。Read DTPA functionalization through carboxyl groups, pores and feed conditions.

论文把 DTPA 衍生基团通过酰胺化引入 MIL-101(Cr)-NH₂，研究 La、Eu、Er 的吸附，以及高浓度 NH₄⁺-N 共存时能否继续回收稀土。The paper introduces DTPA-derived groups to MIL-101(Cr)-NH₂ through amidation, studying La, Eu and Er uptake and recovery in the presence of concentrated NH₄⁺-N.

引入含羧基配体后，面积下降但捕集提高。Carboxyl-containing modification increases capture despite lower area.

负载Loading

更多配体可增加滴定酸性位点，同时降低孔隙可用性。More ligand can increase titratable acidity while reducing pore accessibility.

工作液Feed

模拟含铵体系支持耐受性；没有证明镧系之间的竞争分离。Simulated ammonium feeds support tolerance, without establishing competitive lanthanide separation.

这里的“选择性”主要指 REE 相对铵背景的保留吸附，不是 La / Eu / Er 同时共存时的分离系数，也不是经过真实工业废水验证的产品纯度。Selectivity here primarily concerns retained REE capture against an ammonium background. It is not a measured separation coefficient for a mixed La/Eu/Er feed or product purity validated with actual industrial wastewater.

Grafting changes local environments, protonation and accessibility. The donor count of free DTPA is not a measured coordination number for each surface-bound Eu.
