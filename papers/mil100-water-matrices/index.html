材料“还在”，水却已经变了The solid persists while the water changes

这篇研究把 MIL-100(Fe) 放入去离子水和真实污水处理出水，分别追踪材料的结构与水相的变化。保留衍射峰、保持孔隙、减少浸出和控制 pH，是不同的证据问题。This study immerses MIL-100(Fe) in deionized water and actual wastewater effluent, tracking both the solid and the water. Retained diffraction, retained porosity, limited leaching and controlled pH are distinct evidence questions.

为何酸化：证据支持的解释与未解之处Why acidification occurs: interpretation and unresolved details

同一批 MOF 连续接触五份新鲜去离子水，每次 1.5 h、期间不干燥。后续接触的 pH 下降较弱，与易接近羧基逐步去质子化的解释相容；这不是五次吸附—再生的容量测试。The same MOF contacts five successive fresh DI-water portions for 1.5 h each without intermediate drying. Weaker acidification in later contacts is consistent with progressive deprotonation of accessible carboxyl groups. This is not a five-cycle uptake/regeneration capacity test.

pH 下降 3，究竟改变了多少？What does a pH decrease of three mean?

教学计算：固定初始 pH = 6，只改变终点。按 pH = −log₁₀(aH⁺)，终点与起点的名义氢离子活度比为 10^(6 − pH终)。它解释对数尺度；未计算所需加酸量，也不是 MIL-100(Fe) 的酸化预测。Teaching calculation: fix initial pH = 6 and vary the endpoint. From pH = −log₁₀(aH⁺), the nominal final-to-initial hydrogen-ion activity ratio is 10^(6 − final pH). This explains the logarithmic scale; it calculates neither acid dose nor a prediction of MIL-100(Fe) acidification.

pH 漂移实验回答了什么？What does the pH-drift experiment establish?

作者由漂移法报告 pHPZC = 2.13，并据此解释表面电荷符号。这不是直接测得的 ζ 电位；零差点位于所测范围低端，而且大范围酸化表明材料与溶液发生反应。因此不能用这一数值单独证明特定离子的选择性吸附。The authors report pHPZC = 2.13 from the drift method and use it to discuss surface-charge sign. This is not a direct zeta-potential measurement. The zero-difference point lies at the low end of the tested range, and broad acidification shows material–solution reactions. This value alone does not establish selective adsorption of an ion.

用于 REE 工作液，还需要哪些证据？What remains to test in REE working solutions?

本研究最有用的启示是双向评价：不仅问水是否破坏 MOF，还问 MOF 是否改变工作液。对 REE，应在真实酸度、盐和竞争离子条件下，同时测量容量、竞争选择性、骨架及功能基团浸出、孔隙和循环后的功能。The useful lesson is a two-way assessment: ask both whether water changes the MOF and whether the MOF changes the working solution. For REEs, measure capacity, competitive selectivity, framework/modifier leaching, porosity and retained function under the actual acidity, salts and competing ions.

本文对吸附表现的影响主要是推论，尚无配套吸附数据；“生物相容”也不能替代接收水体的毒理和生态评价。长期流动条件、成型材料、批次差异和连续浸出仍需检验。Consequences for uptake are primarily inferred without paired adsorption data. Biocompatibility is not a substitute for toxicological and ecological testing in receiving waters. Long-term flow conditions, shaped materials, batch variability and continuing leaching remain to be tested.

正文与 SI 的铁浓度、BET 降幅、TGA 比例和碱度示例存在差异。这些问题不会消除“同时检查材料与水相”的教学价值，但限制其定量结论的精确程度。Discrepancies affect iron concentrations, BET losses, TGA ratios and the alkalinity example. They do not remove the value of assessing both solid and water, but constrain the precision of quantitative conclusions.
