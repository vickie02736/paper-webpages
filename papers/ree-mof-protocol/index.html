MOF 捕集与回收 REE：
四个程序，三种质量守恒MOF capture and REE recovery:
four procedures, three mass balances

让稀土从水里消失，只是流程的一站。这篇实验程序把材料制备、捕集、重复加载和产品回收连起来；其中的酸消解回收会消耗原框架。Making rare earths disappear from water is only one stage. This protocol connects material preparation, capture, repeated loading and product recovery; its acid-digestion recovery consumes the original framework.

把“吸走离子”拆成可验证的步骤Turn ion removal into a testable sequence

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

跟着稀土走，也跟着框架中的铜走Track both the rare earth and framework copper

这里的 Cu 基 MOF 是含铜连接网络，REE 是待回收的稀土离子。测液体中的 REE 下降可以估计捕集，但材料可能释放 Cu；最后的产品也可能夹杂其他物质。因此每一站都需要说明物质去了哪里。The Cu-based MOF here is a copper-containing connected network; REEs are the ions being recovered. A decrease in liquid REE can estimate capture, but the sorbent may release Cu and the final product may contain other species. Each stage must therefore locate the material.

ICP-OESICP-OES

电感耦合等离子体发射光谱将液体样品中的元素激发并读特征光，用标准液校准浓度。它数元素，不直接识别原来的分子结构。Inductively coupled plasma optical emission spectroscopy excites sample elements and reads characteristic light, calibrated with standards to obtain concentration. It measures elements, not their original molecular structures.

解吸与消解Desorption and digestion

解吸把目标从表面释放，可能保留吸附剂；消解则破坏材料使组分进入溶液。本文的最终酸处理属于后者。Desorption releases a target from a surface and may preserve the sorbent. Digestion breaks down the material into solution. The final acid treatment here is the latter.

沉淀与分离Precipitation and separation

把溶液条件调到生成难溶固体，再把固体与液体分开。固体出现不自动证明高纯或所有目标都已回收。Change solution conditions to form a poorly soluble solid, then separate it from liquid. Formation of a solid does not by itself establish high purity or complete target recovery.

读完应能说出：连续处理四份新溶液与“再生四次”有什么区别；哪一步取回稀土，却失去原来的 MOF。You should distinguish four successive fresh-solution loadings from four regeneration cycles, and identify the step that retrieves REEs while sacrificing the MOF.

把溶液去除、骨架耐久与产品回收分开测量，才能理解一次高吸附量究竟证明了什么。Measure solution removal, framework durability and product recovery separately to understand what high uptake establishes.

这篇 Nature Protocols 实验协议整合既有研究，以 Cu 基 MOF 为实例，串联材料制备、吸附测试、重复使用与 REE 回收。它提供工作流程，并不证明每种 MOF 都适用于所有水样。REE 指稀土元素；这里重点展示 Nd 和 Y。This Nature Protocols article assembles earlier studies into a workflow for Cu-based MOFs: preparation, adsorption, reuse and REE recovery. It provides procedures rather than validation of every MOF in every water sample. REE means rare-earth element; Nd and Y are central examples here.

三个需要分别回答的问题Three separate questions

溶液少了多少目标离子？骨架留下了多少功能？最终取回了什么产品？去除率、吸附量、结构保持和回收率各有分母，不能互换。How much target leaves the solution? How much framework function survives? What product is recovered? Removal, uptake, structural retention and recovery use different denominators.

从粉体走向可处理形态From powders to manageable forms

实例包括 HKUST-1、名义 Fe 掺杂 HKUST-1、二维 Cu–咪唑材料 BNMG-1、磁性 iMOF，以及醋酸纤维素（CA）包埋 BNMG-1 微珠。成形有助于回收固体，也会改变活性物质比例和传质。Examples include HKUST-1, nominally Fe-doped HKUST-1, the 2D Cu–imidazolate material BNMG-1, magnetic iMOF and cellulose-acetate (CA) beads containing BNMG-1. Shaping aids solid handling while changing active fraction and transport.

核心区分：不解吸的连续加载、解吸后再生、以及消耗骨架的酸消解回收，是不同的操作。Key distinction: successive loading without stripping, regeneration after desorption, and recovery through destructive digestion are different operations.

同一组浓度，可以回答不同问题One concentration pair, different questions

下方是可编辑的质量守恒教学计算，不是论文新增测量。假定体积不变、溶液均一，且浓度下降全部来自吸附；沉淀、容器损失和取样损失需要实验对照排除。This editable mass-balance calculation is a teaching tool, not an additional measurement. It assumes constant volume, a uniform solution and loss entirely through adsorption. Precipitation, container loss and sampling loss require controls.

R = 100(C₀ − Cₑ)/C₀
qₑ = (C₀ − Cₑ)V/m
Kd = qₑ/Cₑ

C 用 mg L⁻¹、V 用 L、m 用 g 时，Kd 的单位为 L g⁻¹；换成表 3 的 mL g⁻¹ 需乘 1000。若 Cₑ = 0，不能给出有限 Kd；低于检出限应报告界限。With C in mg L⁻¹, V in L and m in g, Kd is in L g⁻¹; multiply by 1000 for the mL g⁻¹ used in Table 3. At Cₑ = 0 a finite Kd cannot be calculated; values below detection need a bound.

高去除率可以出现在低单位质量负载下；Kd 也依赖条件。真正的分离因子需要同一混合体系中两种离子的 Kd，不能把两个独立单离子实验的数值直接相除当作邻近 REE 分离证据。High removal can coexist with a low mass-specific loading, and Kd depends on conditions. A separation factor needs both ions in the same mixture; dividing separate single-ion experiments does not establish separation of neighbouring REEs.

四次高去除：连续加载的证据Four high-removal loadings

两种“再用一次”，中间步骤不一样Two kinds of reuse have different intermediate steps

连续加载：逐步装满Successive loading: progressively fill sites

图 6C 使用同一份 BNMG-1 接触新溶液，各次加载之间不解吸。四次高去除说明这些加载条件下仍有可用捕集能力。Fig. 6C exposes the same BNMG-1 to fresh solutions without desorption between loadings. Four high-removal loadings support remaining uptake ability under those loading conditions.

再生循环：取出后再装Regeneration cycle: unload and reload

需要在两次吸附之间释放目标，并检查再生后的固体与下一轮表现。它额外检验释放过程及材料承受再生的能力。Requires release between adsorption runs, then checking the regenerated solid and its next-run performance. It additionally tests unloading and resistance to regeneration.

原图 6C 条件为每次 10 mg/L 初始浓度、500 mg/L 吸附剂、12 h。不能把这些连续加载柱条重新命名为四次酸洗再生。程序 4 的破坏性回收也与保留框架的循环不同。Fig. 6C uses 10 mg/L initial concentration, 500 mg/L sorbent and 12 h per loading. 自测：四次连续加载去除率都高，为什么不能推出四次再生成功？Check: why do four high-removal loadings not prove four successful regenerations?

因为中间没有取出已吸附的稀土。它没有测试解吸率、再生剂造成的损伤或再生后的选择性；也不说明最终产品纯度。Because the adsorbed REEs were not removed between loadings. This does not test desorption, regenerant damage, post-regeneration selectivity or final product purity.

图 6C / Source Data 6C 使用同一 BNMG-1 连续处理新溶液，不在相邻加载间解吸。条件为 C₀ = 10 mg L⁻¹、吸附剂 500 mg L⁻¹、每次 12 h。下图纵轴为去除率，量尺 0–100%，原始表未给这组数据的误差。Fig. 6C / Source Data 6C uses the same BNMG-1 with successive fresh solutions, without desorption between loadings. Conditions are C₀ = 10 mg L⁻¹, 500 mg L⁻¹ sorbent and 12 h per loading. The chart uses a 0–100% removal scale; the source sheet supplies no uncertainty for these points.

按相同 V/m、忽略固体损失的教学估算，每次即使去除 100%，也仅加载 20 mg g⁻¹；四次最多合计 80 mg g⁻¹。这低于表 3 约 350 mg g⁻¹ 的条目，说明四次高去除可以发生在逐渐占据剩余位点的过程中。A teaching estimate at constant V/m with no solid loss gives only 20 mg g⁻¹ per loading even at 100% removal, or at most 80 mg g⁻¹ over four loadings. This is below the approximately 350 mg g⁻¹ Table 3 entries: high removal over four loadings can arise as remaining sites fill.

该结果支持这些条件下的重复捕集；它没有展示四次“吸附—解吸—恢复”的再生闭环，也没有给出长期饱和寿命。The result supports repeated capture under these conditions. It does not demonstrate four adsorption–desorption–restoration cycles or a long-term saturation lifetime.

产品回收中的三条物料去向Follow material through product recovery

  1. 负载固体进入酸液Put the loaded solid into acid

    程序 4 用 1% HNO₃、80 °C、12 h 消解。稀土和框架 Cu 进入液体；这一步不能画成原 MOF 洗净后返回下一轮。Procedure 4 digests the solid in 1% HNO₃ at 80 °C for 12 h. REEs and framework Cu enter the liquid; this step cannot be drawn as a washed MOF returning intact to another cycle.

  2. 调整溶液化学Change solution chemistry

    加入 NH₄OH 后，目标路径是形成稀土氢氧化物沉淀，而 Cu 主要留在含氨上清液。需要分析两相，而不是只看沉淀颜色。After NH₄OH addition, the intended route forms REE hydroxide precipitates while Cu stays mainly in the ammonia-containing supernatant. Analyze both phases rather than judging precipitate color.

  3. 取得并评价产品Obtain and assess the product

    分离、洗涤及后续处理会改变回收量与杂质。最终需报进料目标有多少进入产品、产品里还有什么，以及铜废液如何处理。Separation, washing and further treatment affect yield and impurities. Report how much feed target reaches the product, what else the product contains and how the Cu-containing liquid is handled.

这是物料去向导读，不能代替论文中的完整实验操作与安全条件。Cu“主要留在液体”不意味着产品已被证明绝对无 Cu。This is a material-flow guide, not a replacement for the complete experimental and safety instructions. Cu remaining mainly in liquid does not establish an absolutely Cu-free product.

NH₄OH then precipitates REE hydroxides while Cu mainly remains in the ammoniacal supernatant. This is product recovery through framework consumption.

配液和取样：先做守恒检查Stock solutions and sampling: check conservation

程序 2A 将“5 mL、1000 mg L⁻¹ 储备液 + 95 mL 水”写在“制备 50 mL、100 mg L⁻¹”附近；实际所得是 100 mL、50 mg L⁻¹。按 C₁V₁=C₂V₂，目标 50 mL、100 mg L⁻¹ 应为 5 mL 储备液定容至 50 mL。该算术例是讲解校核，不是新增实验。Procedure 2A places “5 mL of 1000 mg L⁻¹ stock + 95 mL water” near a target of “50 mL at 100 mg L⁻¹”. That mixture actually gives 100 mL at 50 mg L⁻¹. By C₁V₁=C₂V₂, the stated target needs 5 mL stock made up to 50 mL. This is an arithmetic check, not a new experiment.

相邻储备液质量说明也存在十倍浓度差异。取样段落同时出现不超过 10% 与至少 50% 上清液的说法；使用独立时间点样品可避免反复取样改变体积，但实际实验仍须明确记录方案。Adjacent stock-mass instructions also contain a tenfold concentration discrepancy. Sampling text gives both at-most-10% and at-least-50% supernatant instructions. Separate samples for each time point can avoid changing volume through repeated withdrawals, while the actual sampling plan still needs documentation.

热力学、剂量与原始表的边界Thermodynamics, dose and raw-data boundaries

表 4 的 BNMG-1 对 Cd、Mn、Ni、Nd 给出正 ΔH，对 Pb、Y 给出负 ΔH，不能全部概括成放热。含单位的 qₑ/Cₑ 不能不加标准态处理就代入 ln K 求绝对 ΔG。Source Data 5D 的 Nd log Kd 在 500 mg L⁻¹ 剂量后降低，也不支持“随剂量一直增大”。Table 4 gives positive ΔH for Cd, Mn, Ni and Nd on BNMG-1, and negative values for Pb and Y; the set is not uniformly exothermic. Dimensional qₑ/Cₑ needs a defined standard state before use in ln K for absolute ΔG. The Nd log Kd in Source Data 5D decreases above a 500 mg L⁻¹ dose, rather than rising throughout.

Source Data 4B 的部分列在初始气体吸附区段后附有 P/P₀ > 1 的衍射样数列。本页没有把整列当作有效吸附等温线，也没有据此重算 BET。BNMG-1 的三维堆积尚未完整解析；本页不补画未经证实的原子连接。Some columns in Source Data 4B contain diffraction-like sequences with P/P₀ > 1 after the initial gas-sorption block. The full columns are not treated as valid sorption isotherms or used to recalculate BET. BNMG-1 stacking is not fully resolved in three dimensions; no unsupported atomic connectivity is drawn.
