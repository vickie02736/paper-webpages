# AI-SAM：钙钛矿太阳能电池界面设计

[讲解页 / Explainer](index.html) · [通用化学与材料 / General Chemistry & Materials](../../?theme=chemistry-materials#themes)

把逆合成预测、高通量 DFT 和化学知识接成筛选链，从超过 90,000 个结构中识别可合成、界面匹配并具有长期稳定性的自组装单分子层 AI-1。器件达到 27.2% 功率转换效率，在 65 °C 最大功率点跟踪 2,000 小时后保留超过 95% 初始效率。

Links synthesis-aware AI screening to high-throughput DFT and chemical knowledge to identify the AI-1 self-assembled monolayer. Devices reach 27.2% power-conversion efficiency and retain more than 95% after 2,000 h of maximum-power-point tracking at 65 °C.
