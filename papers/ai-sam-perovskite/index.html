02 · 为什么要做这篇文章？Why this paper?

最难的不是找到“漂亮分子”，而是让它做得出、装得上、撑得久。The challenge is not finding a “pretty molecule,” but one that can be made, installed and kept working.

1候选太多：理论组合空间超过 90,000 种，靠直觉一个个试太慢。Too many candidates: over 90,000 potential combinations make intuition-only testing too slow.
2界面很挑剔：分子能吸附、传空穴，还要阻挡不该通过的电子。The interface is picky: a molecule must bind, transport holes and block unwanted electrons.
3真实器件会老化：光、热和界面缺陷会让效率掉下来。Real devices age: light, heat and interface defects can reduce efficiency.
所以论文的核心问题是：So the paper asks: 能不能先用 AI + 计算 + 化学经验，把最值得合成和测试的分子挑出来，再交给实验验证？Can AI, computation and chemical experience first narrow the field to molecules worth synthesizing and testing, before experiments validate them?

先认识这层只有分子尺度的接触层Meet a molecular-scale contact layer

钙钛矿吸收光后产生可移动的电子和空穴。空穴可以理解为电子留下的空位；要输出电流，两种载流子需要被分别收集。自组装单分子层（SAM）位于透明电极与钙钛矿之间，帮助收集空穴，并减少界面处电子和空穴重新结合造成的能量损失。Light absorption creates mobile electrons and holes. A hole is a missing electron; current requires collecting the two carriers separately. The self-assembled monolayer (SAM) lies between the transparent electrode and perovskite, helping collect holes while limiting energy losses when carriers recombine at the interface.

这里的设计难题是同时满足几件事：分子能做出来、能形成合适的接触层、能传输电荷，并在光照和加热时保持有效。论文没有直接训练一个“预测效率”的模型，而是用可合成性和分子性质来挑选值得实验的候选。The design must combine feasible synthesis, a suitable contact layer, charge transport and durability under light and heat. The paper does not directly train a device-efficiency predictor: molecular and synthesis descriptors prioritize candidates for experiments.

06 · 边界Limits

这篇论文证明了什么，还没有证明什么？What does the paper show, and what remains open?

已经支持Supported here

作者合成了 AI-1，并以 NMR 与 XPS 支持 S–Pb 相互作用的解释；器件达到 27.2% PCE，并在规定温度与最大功率点跟踪条件下保持超过 95% 的初始效率。AI-1 was synthesized; NMR and XPS support the authors’ interpretation of S–Pb interactions. Devices reach 27.2% PCE and retain more than 95% under the stated temperature and MPP-tracking conditions.

仍需谨慎Still needs care

这些结果属于 AI-1 与论文器件条件；它们不自动证明所有候选、所有钙钛矿组成或长期户外运行都具有同样稳定性。多指标排序也不等于因果权重。The evidence concerns AI-1 and the paper’s device conditions. It does not establish equal stability for every candidate, perovskite composition or outdoor lifetime. A multi-descriptor ranking is not a causal weighting.
