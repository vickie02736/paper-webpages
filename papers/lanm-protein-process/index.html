01 / 研究问题01 / THE QUESTION

高亲和力还不够：复杂进料里，谁先出来？High affinity is not enough: who comes out first from a messy feed?

液–液萃取可以处理大通量，却依赖多级有机相和严格的预处理。LanM 提供另一种路线：把生物识别位点变成可再生的固定床。Liquid–liquid extraction handles large throughput, but often needs many organic-phase stages and careful pretreatment. LanM offers another route: turn a biological recognition site into a regenerable fixed bed.

旧问题OLD BOTTLENECK

吸附、回收、纯度常被混在一起Capture, recovery and purity get conflated

从溶液里抓住 REE，不代表能把它们分成可用的产品。每一个步骤都要同时看突破、解吸窗口和杂质。Capturing REEs from solution does not mean they are separated into useful products. Breakthrough, elution windows and impurities must be tracked together.

06 / 工艺路线06 / PROCESS ROUTE

把一次柱实验读成四个决策点Read one column run as four decisions

逐步点选，检查每一段的目标。这样能避免把“突破晚”误读成“产品纯度高”。Step through the run and check the goal of each segment. This prevents reading late breakthrough as high product purity.

装载的目标是让 REE 留在柱上；论文中 PRB 浸出液约 30 BV 后才出现 REE 突破。The goal of loading is to keep REEs on the column; in the paper's PRB leachate, REE breakthrough appeared only after about 30 BV.

10 次 pH 3 吸附 / pH 1.5 解吸循环容量无明显下降（Fig. 2）。这是可再生性的证据，不等于长期工业寿命测试。Capacity did not visibly decline over 10 pH 3 adsorption / pH 1.5 desorption cycles (Fig. 2). This supports regenerability, not a long-term industrial lifetime test.
07 / 局限与联系07 / LIMITS & CONNECTIONS

它证明了流程可行，也把放大问题说清楚It proves a process concept and makes the scale-up questions explicit

LanM 不是直接替代所有高品位矿液–液萃取的通用答案。它最有说服力的场景，是低品位、杂质多、需要温和水相处理的浸出液。LanM is not a universal replacement for liquid–liquid extraction of every high-grade ore liquor. Its strongest case is a low-grade, impurity-rich leachate that benefits from mild aqueous processing.

容量与表面积还要提升Capacity and surface area still need improvement

论文报告 5.77±0.67 μmol Nd/mL 微珠的柱容量；作者把更高孔隙率、表面积和蛋白表达量列为后续方向。The paper reports 5.77±0.67 μmol Nd/mL beads; higher porosity, surface area and protein expression are listed as next steps.

Fe / Si 预处理仍然重要Fe / Si pretreatment remains important

复杂浸出液中的 Fe、Si 可能造成胶体或堵塞；作者指出需要预处理，并未把柱子当成万能净化器。Fe and Si in complex leachates can create colloids or fouling; the authors call for pretreatment rather than treating the column as a universal purifier.

从半连续到连续设备From semi-continuous to continuous equipment

旋转柱、连续切换和真实进料波动仍需工程验证；当前工作是流程概念和低品位案例验证。Rotating columns, continuous switching and feed variability still need engineering validation; this work is a process concept plus a low-grade case study.

联系CONNECTION

蛋白质识别Protein recognition

与后续 Hans-LanM 二聚体工作相连：柔性位点与协同结合可以放大离子尺寸差异。It connects to later Hans-LanM dimer work, where flexible sites and cooperative binding amplify small ionic-size differences.

联系CONNECTION

固定床工艺Fixed-bed process

与 MOF、树脂和膜分离相比，LanM 的优势来自识别化学；工程瓶颈则仍是容量、传质和再生。Compared with MOFs, resins and membranes, LanM's advantage is recognition chemistry; the engineering bottlenecks remain capacity, mass transfer and regeneration.
