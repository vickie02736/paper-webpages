01 / RESEARCH LOGIC

研究问题不是“能不能吸附”，而是“配体负载改变了什么”。The question is not whether REEs adsorb, but what ligand loading changes.

已有工作知道功能化 MOF 可以吸附 REE，但三维孔道中多个配体如何协同配位、负载量如何改变配位数，仍缺少结构—性能解释。Prior work showed that functionalised MOFs can adsorb REEs. What was missing was a structure–performance explanation of how several ligands cooperate in 3D pores and how loading changes the coordination number.

Previous approach

液–液溶剂萃取Liquid–liquid solvent extraction

The industrial benchmark, with high throughput and tunable selectivity; but reaching commercial-grade purity needs a complex multistage system — a recent pilot demonstration used more than 150 stages — with heavy chemical use and a large environmental footprint.

What the paper does: prepares MIL-101 with 1.2, 2.1 and 3.0 mmol g⁻¹ PMIDA; compares adsorption of Nd³⁺, Gd³⁺ and Dy³⁺ and competition from Fe(III); and uses MD to test a mechanism in which ligand O replaces water O while the total coordination number stays at about 9.

为什么不是“配体越多，容量越线性增加”？Why doesn’t capacity grow in step with the ligand?

孔道中每个 REE 的总氧配位数保持在约 9。新增 PMIDA donor atoms 主要替换水合壳中的水 O，提升多齿复合物稳定性，而不是无限增加独立吸附位点。

Each REE in the pore keeps a total oxygen coordination number of about 9. Extra PMIDA donor atoms mainly replace water O in the hydration shell, stabilising a multidentate complex, rather than adding ever more independent adsorption sites.

教学提示：容量是“抓住多少”；Nligand–REE 是“一个被抓住的 REE 周围有多少配体 donor atoms”。两者不是同一个指标。Tip: capacity is how much is captured; Nligand–REE is how many ligand donor atoms surround each captured REE. They are different measures.

三条关键关系Three key relations

式 (4) 可用配位位点Eq. (4) available coordination sites

Nligand–REE = ηn / qmax

η 是 PMIDA 负载，n 是每个配体的配位原子数，qmax 是 Langmuir 饱和容量。作者假设每个带电基团（–COO⁻、–PO₃H⁻）贡献一个有效 donor site，即 n = 2。

η is the PMIDA loading, n the number of coordinating atoms per ligand, and qmax the Langmuir saturation capacity. The authors assume one effective donor site per charged group (–COO⁻, –PO₃H⁻), i.e. n = 2.

式 (5) 剩余水配位数Eq. (5) remaining water coordination

CNwater = CNtotal − Nligand–REE

体相水中 REE 的总配位数为 8.97–9.01（SI Table S6）。模拟显示吸附后总 CN 仍保持约 9，说明配体 O 部分替代了水 O。

In bulk water an REE has a total coordination number of 8.97–9.01 (SI Table S6). Simulations show the total CN stays at about 9 after adsorption, so ligand O partly replaces water O.

MD / RDF

对 REE–O 部分径向分布函数积分到第一极小值，得到局部配位数；总配位强度（TCS）把各类配体 O 与 REE 的键能按配位数加总。

Integrating the partial REE–O radial distribution function up to its first minimum gives the local coordination number; the total coordination strength (TCS) sums ligand-O–REE bond energies weighted by coordination number.

06 / CRITICAL THINKING

强结论与未解决问题要同时保留。Keep strong conclusions and open questions side by side.

论文的实验和 MD 共同支持局部配位机制，但并没有把所有竞争效应都解释完。Experiments and MD together support the local coordination mechanism, but not every competing effect is explained.

Claim

PMIDA–REE 形成更高阶复合物PMIDA–REE forms higher-order complexes

证据：3 mmol g⁻¹ 时 Nligand–REE 为 4.98–5.42；Nd³⁺ 的 MD CNligand 从 4.46 增至 5.80，总配位强度从 11.28 增至 15.26 ×10⁻¹⁸ J（正文数值）。

Evidence: at 3 mmol g⁻¹, Nligand–REE is 4.98–5.42; for Nd³⁺ the MD CNligand rises from 4.46 to 5.80 and the total coordination strength from 11.28 to 15.26 ×10⁻¹⁸ J (values in the text).

边界：MD 基于力场，不等同于直接解析的晶体结构。

Boundary: MD relies on a force field and is not a directly resolved crystal structure.

Claim

对 Fe(III) 有选择性Selective over Fe(III)

证据：等摩尔 0.2 mmol L⁻¹ 混合液中，Nd³⁺ 吸附相对单溶质平均降低 12.9%；REE:Fe = 1:100 时只降低约 0.3%。

Evidence: in an equimolar 0.2 mmol L⁻¹ mixture, Nd³⁺ uptake falls by 12.9% on average versus single-solute conditions; at REE:Fe = 1:100 it falls by only about 0.3%.

边界：Fe(III) 未纳入 MD；抑制作用随负载升高而增强，其分子机制仍待研究。

Boundary: Fe(III) was not included in the MD; inhibition grows with loading, and its molecular mechanism remains open.

Claim

可再生，但存在材料损失Regenerable, but with material loss

证据：0.01 M HCl 洗脱 83.2–91.9%，0.1 M HCl 为 89.0–98.2%；用 0.1 M HCl 再生一次后，吸附量下降 2–8.7%。

Evidence: 0.01 M HCl elutes 83.2–91.9% and 0.1 M HCl 89.0–98.2%; after one regeneration with 0.1 M HCl, uptake drops by 2–8.7%.

边界：按 Cr 浓度估算，每次吸附和解吸分别损失约 7.96% 和 11.86% 的 MOF；只做了一次再生循环，长期稳定性尚未评估。

Boundary: based on dissolved Cr, each adsorption and desorption step loses about 7.96% and 11.86% of the MOF; only one regeneration cycle was run, so long-term stability is untested.

最值得继续做的实验：在不同 PMIDA 负载下直接测 Fe–PMIDA 配位或沉积，并用更温和的解吸条件打破“选择性越强、再生越难”的耦合。

Most useful next experiment: measure Fe–PMIDA coordination or deposition directly at each PMIDA loading, and try milder desorption to break the coupling between stronger selectivity and harder regeneration.

五个要点Five key takeaways

01

PMIDA 改性后，MIL-101 保留了晶体结构和较高比表面积，同时引入对 REE 亲和的 O donor atoms。

After PMIDA modification, MIL-101 keeps its crystal structure and a high surface area while gaining REE-affine O donor atoms.

02

3 mmol g⁻¹ 负载下，Nd / Gd / Dy 的饱和容量约 160 / 175 / 195 mg g⁻¹。

At 3 mmol g⁻¹ loading, saturation capacities for Nd / Gd / Dy are about 160 / 175 / 195 mg g⁻¹.

03

负载增加对容量的边际收益变小，但每个 REE 周围的配体配位增强。

Higher loading gives diminishing gains in capacity, but stronger ligand coordination around each REE.

04

MD 给出“部分脱水、配体 O 替换水 O、总 CN 保持约 9”的分子图像。

MD gives a molecular picture of partial dehydration, ligand O replacing water O, and a total CN that stays at about 9.

05

选择性和稳定性有代价：酸再生有效，却伴随不可忽略的容量与框架损失。

Selectivity and stability come at a cost: acid regeneration works, but with non-negligible losses of capacity and framework.

自我检验Explain it yourself

1. 为什么 PMIDA 负载增加后，容量没有同比增加？1. Why doesn’t capacity rise in proportion to PMIDA loading?
因为新增 donor atoms 主要进入同一个 REE 的配位壳，替换水合壳中的水氧，增强多齿复合物稳定性；它不等于同数量的新独立位点。Because the extra donor atoms mostly join the coordination shell of the same REE, replacing hydration-shell water oxygens and stabilising the multidentate complex; they are not the same number of new independent sites.
2. Figure 5 的 CN 和 TCS 各自说明什么？2. What do CN and TCS in Figure 5 each tell you?
CN 统计局部配位原子的数量；TCS 把相应的键能贡献合并为整体配位强度。两者一起把“几何上的更多接触”连接到“能量上的更强结合”。CN counts the local coordinating atoms; TCS combines their bond-energy contributions into an overall coordination strength. Together they connect more contacts in geometry with stronger binding in energy.
3. 这篇论文是否证明了对所有杂质都高选择性？3. Does the paper show high selectivity against all impurities?
没有。它只直接测试了 Fe(III) 竞争，并在给定浓度、pH 与材料条件下观察到有限影响；更广泛的杂质体系与长期运行仍需验证。No. It directly tested only Fe(III) competition and found a limited effect under the given concentrations, pH and material conditions; broader impurity mixtures and long-term operation still need testing.
