Gd 容量与选择性Dual-functional UiO-66:
Gd uptake and selectivity

同时加入含氧和含氮的结合基团后，UiO-66 的拟合 Gd 容量提高，但达到平衡更慢。论文的价值在于让“装多少”和“多久装好”一起接受检验。Adding oxygen- and nitrogen-containing groups increases fitted Gd capacity in UiO-66 but slows equilibration. The study asks us to examine how much can load alongside how long loading takes.

Imteaz Ahmed · Yu-Ri Lee · Kwangsun Yu · Samiran Bhattacharjee · Wha-Seung Ahn
Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research 58, 2324–2332 (2019)

更多结合基团，较少气体可及孔隙More binding groups, less gas-accessible porosity

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

更多结合环境，不自动意味着更快的通道More binding environments do not automatically give faster transport

UiO-66 是锆节点与有机连接体构成的多孔材料。Gd³⁺ 是水中的钆离子。本研究先引入额外羧酸基团，再用乙二胺（ED）修饰，试图增加含氧、含氮的结合环境，同时改变了孔内空间。UiO-66 is a porous material of zirconium nodes and organic linkers. Gd³⁺ is the gadolinium ion in solution. The study first adds extra carboxylic-acid groups and then ethylenediamine (ED), seeking more oxygen/nitrogen binding environments while also changing pore space.

平衡Equilibrium

在给定条件下，液体和固体间的净转移不再造成明显浓度变化。平衡量与达到平衡的速度是两个问题。Under the specified conditions, net transfer between liquid and solid no longer gives an appreciable concentration change. Equilibrium loading and the speed of reaching it differ.

Q₀ 与 CₑQ₀ and Cₑ

Q₀ 是 Langmuir 模型的容量极限参数，单位 mg/g；Cₑ 是接触后平衡液体浓度，单位 mg/L。Cₑ 不是开始加入的浓度。Q₀ is the Langmuir capacity-limit parameter in mg/g; Cₑ is the liquid concentration after equilibration in mg/L. Cₑ is not the initial concentration.

BET 面积BET area

由氮气吸附估计每克固体可达表面积。水合 Gd 的进入与结合还受溶液和基团影响，不能只按气体面积比例预测。Accessible area per gram estimated from nitrogen sorption. Entry and binding of hydrated Gd also depend on solution chemistry and groups, not simply a gas-area ratio.

读完应能解释：为什么 79 mg/g 不能当成任意浓度下的吸附量，为什么容量提升与吸附变慢并不矛盾。You should explain why 79 mg/g is not uptake at every concentration and why improved capacity can coexist with slower equilibration.

从连接体上的羧基出发，接入乙二胺，提高 Gd 结合能力。理解这项结果，关键是把模型容量、竞争偏好与循环稳定性分别读清楚。A carboxyl-bearing linker route introduces ethylenediamine and increases Gd binding. Understanding the result requires reading model capacity, competitive preference and reuse stability separately.

连接体提供入口A linker-based route

用 TPA/TMA 混合连接体引入未配位羧基，再与乙二胺 ED 反应。作者提出形成酰胺连接，并留下可参与结合的含氮基团。Mixed TPA/TMA linkers introduce uncoordinated carboxyls, followed by ethylenediamine (ED). The authors propose amide attachment with nitrogen-containing groups available for binding.

容量提升有代价Uptake gains have a trade-off

最强改性样品的 BET 从 1305 降至 835 m²/g，而拟合最大 Gd 容量从 16 增至 79 mg/g。面积本身不能预测功能基团的亲和力。For the most functionalized material, BET falls from 1305 to 835 m²/g while fitted maximum Gd uptake rises from 16 to 79 mg/g. Area alone does not predict functional-group affinity.

选择性取决于竞争者Preference depends on competitors

对 Na、Ca、Mg、Al、Fe 的优势，并不等同于在稀土之间优先分离 Gd。论文分别测试了这两种竞争情形。Preference over Na, Ca, Mg, Al and Fe does not establish Gd preference among REEs. The paper tests these two competitive settings separately.

怎样把结果用于下一项 REE 研究Using the result in a new REE study

这项研究支持“含结合基团的连接体改性可以在 BET 降低时提高 Gd 容量”，也显示对非稀土杂质与对稀土内部的选择性需要分别测量。它没有建立实际废液的连续柱分离、产品纯度或长期运行寿命。The study supports increased Gd uptake through linker functionalization despite lower BET, and shows why preference over non-REE impurities and within REEs need separate tests. It does not establish continuous-column separation of real waste, product purity or long-term service life.

后续比较应同时记录：单离子或混合进料、质量或摩尔浓度、pH、固液比、平衡时间、容量是否来自拟合，以及酸洗后金属和有机组分去向。2019 年此样品的循环表现不能自动替代其他 UiO-66 配方在缓冲液中的验证。A follow-up comparison should record single-ion versus mixed feed, mass versus molar concentration, pH, solid/liquid ratio, equilibration time, fitted versus measured capacity, and metal/organic release during regeneration.
