AHMED ET AL. · 2019 · UiO-66–COOH–ED

双官能团 UiO-66：
Gd 容量与选择性Dual-functional UiO-66:
Gd uptake and selectivity

从连接体上的羧基出发，接入乙二胺，提高 Gd 结合能力。理解这项结果，关键是把模型容量、竞争偏好与循环稳定性分别读清楚。A carboxyl-bearing linker route introduces ethylenediamine and increases Gd binding. Understanding the result requires reading model capacity, competitive preference and reuse stability separately.

Imteaz Ahmed · Yu-Ri Lee · Kwangsun Yu · Samiran Bhattacharjee · Wha-Seung Ahn
Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research 58, 2324–2332 (2019)

更多结合基团，较少气体可及孔隙More binding groups, less gas-accessible porosity

连接体提供入口A linker-based route

用 TPA/TMA 混合连接体引入未配位羧基，再与乙二胺 ED 反应。作者提出形成酰胺连接，并留下可参与结合的含氮基团。Mixed TPA/TMA linkers introduce uncoordinated carboxyls, followed by ethylenediamine (ED). The authors propose amide attachment with nitrogen-containing groups available for binding.

容量提升有代价Uptake gains have a trade-off

最强改性样品的 BET 从 1305 降至 835 m²/g，而拟合最大 Gd 容量从 16 增至 79 mg/g。面积本身不能预测功能基团的亲和力。For the most functionalized material, BET falls from 1305 to 835 m²/g while fitted maximum Gd uptake rises from 16 to 79 mg/g. Area alone does not predict functional-group affinity.

选择性取决于竞争者Preference depends on competitors

对 Na、Ca、Mg、Al、Fe 的优势，并不等同于在稀土之间优先分离 Gd。论文分别测试了这两种竞争情形。Preference over Na, Ca, Mg, Al and Fe does not establish Gd preference among REEs. The paper tests these two competitive settings separately.

容量增加，并没有让吸附更快More uptake does not mean faster equilibration

作者报告的时间尺度Reported timescales

正文称 COOH(25) 与 COOH(25)-ED 分别在约 2 h 和 3 h 达到约 90% 的平衡进度，完全平衡约需 4 h 和 6 h。作者将 ED 样品较慢的吸附与孔隙减少、扩散受阻联系起来。The text reports about 90% of equilibrium after roughly 2 h and 3 h for COOH(25) and COOH(25)-ED, respectively, and full equilibration at about 4 h and 6 h. The authors relate slower ED-sample uptake to reduced porosity and hindered diffusion.

拟合优度的边界Limits of model fits

SI 表 S1 的 PSO R² 分别为 0.994 与 0.965，高于对应 PFO 的 0.971 与 0.817。较好的 PSO 拟合可以描述数据，但不能独立证明化学吸附、螯合交换或唯一限速步骤。PSO R² values in SI Table S1 are 0.994 and 0.965, above the PFO values of 0.971 and 0.817. Better PSO fits describe the data but do not independently establish chemisorption, chelate exchange or a unique rate-limiting step.

pH 同时影响位点质子化与离子形态pH affects both binding groups and ion speciation

在所测 pH 2–7 中，Gd 吸附量随 pH 上升，到约 pH 6 较高。SI 图 S5 的 ζ 电位从 pH 2 附近的约零值变得更负，支持表面电性随去质子化改变的解释。Across the tested pH 2–7 range, Gd uptake increases toward a high value around pH 6. SI Fig. S5 shows zeta potential changing from near zero at pH 2 to more negative values, supporting a surface-charge change with deprotonation.

作者因 Gd(OH)₃ 形成的风险而不将吸附研究延伸到 pH > 7。这不是独立测定的通用沉淀边界；实际边界还依赖浓度和其他配体。高 pH 下溶液浓度降低，不能自动归因于吸附。The authors avoid pH > 7 because of Gd(OH)₃ formation. This is not an independently measured universal precipitation boundary; concentration and other ligands also matter. A lower dissolved concentration at high pH cannot automatically be assigned to adsorption.

吸附前后 FTIR 变化与 pH 趋势支持作者提出的含氧、含氮基团参与结合机制。论文没有给出唯一的 Gd 局部配位数、位点几何或吸附后单晶结构。FTIR changes before and after uptake and the pH trend support the authors’ proposal that oxygen- and nitrogen-containing groups participate in binding. A unique Gd coordination number, site geometry or post-adsorption single-crystal structure is not established.

温度实验在 25–45 °C 下观察到吸附量增加。正文热力学表的熵单位与数值之间存在疑点，且 Kd 是有量纲的操作分配系数；本页不据此重算标准自由能或给出可外推的热力学预测。Uptake increases in the 25–45 °C temperature study. The entropy value and unit in the main thermodynamic table require care, and Kd is a dimensional operational distribution coefficient. This page does not use these values to recalculate standard free energies or extrapolate thermodynamic predictions.

换一组竞争离子，结论也随之改变Change the competitors, change the conclusion

两组实验都使用 15 mg 吸附剂 / 15 mL 混合液，25 °C、pH 6、6 h；每种离子的初始质量浓度为 40 mg/L。相同 mg/L 不是相同摩尔浓度。Both tests use 15 mg adsorbent in 15 mL mixed solution at 25 °C, pH 6 for 6 h. Each ion starts at 40 mg/L; equal mass concentrations do not mean equal molar concentrations.

Kd,i = [(Ci,0 − Ci,f) / Ci,f] × V/m

SI 式 S6 中 V 取 mL、m 取 g，因此 Kd 单位为 mL/g。正文称将各离子的 Kd 按百分比比较来报告约 75% 选择性，但没有提供逐离子的原始浓度。本页保留作者的指标称谓，不将 75% 换写成 Gd 进料回收率、解吸液纯度或对邻近稀土的分离系数。In SI Eq. S6, V is in mL and m in g, giving Kd in mL/g. The text reports about 75% selectivity by comparing the ions’ Kd values on a percentage basis, without providing each raw concentration. This page retains the authors’ metric and does not relabel 75% as Gd feed recovery, eluate purity or a separation factor against neighbouring REEs.

作者用硬酸–硬碱相互作用、螯合和镧系收缩解释偏好。这些是机制解释，不意味着 Na、Ca、Mg 完全不能配位，也不能单靠离子半径预测所有浸出液中的竞争结果。The authors interpret preference using hard acid/base interactions, chelation and lanthanide contraction. These interpretations do not imply that Na, Ca and Mg cannot coordinate, or that ionic radius alone predicts competition in every leachate.

五次循环支持可重复使用，仍需追踪组分Five cycles support reuse; composition still needs tracking

  1. 吸附Adsorb

    100 mg/L Gd，pH 6，吸附至平衡。Adsorb from 100 mg/L Gd at pH 6 to equilibrium.

  2. 酸洗解吸Acid-strip

    0.1 mol/L HCl，振荡 2 h。Shake in 0.1 mol/L HCl for 2 h.

  3. 回收与洗涤Recover and wash

    过滤回收，充分洗涤、干燥，再次吸附。Recover by filtration, wash thoroughly, dry and reuse.

  4. 五轮后检查Check after five cycles

    正文报告第五轮保留约 92% 初始容量，SI 图 S6 保留主要 PXRD 特征。The text reports about 92% of initial capacity at cycle five; SI Fig. S6 retains major PXRD features.

正文摘要称“没有明显恶化”，但 §3.7 报告第二轮 >95%、第五轮约 92%。本页采用较具体的循环描述，不表述为完全无损。PXRD 与容量并不能单独证明 Zr、连接体或 ED 均没有浸出；本研究未提供完整组分收支与循环后 BET。The abstract states no noticeable deterioration, while §3.7 reports >95% at cycle two and about 92% at cycle five. This page uses the more specific cycle description rather than claiming no loss. PXRD and capacity alone do not establish zero release of Zr, linkers or ED; a full component balance and post-cycle BET are not reported.

怎样把结果用于下一项 REE 研究Using the result in a new REE study

这项研究支持“含结合基团的连接体改性可以在 BET 降低时提高 Gd 容量”，也显示对非稀土杂质与对稀土内部的选择性需要分别测量。它没有建立实际废液的连续柱分离、产品纯度或长期运行寿命。The study supports increased Gd uptake through linker functionalization despite lower BET, and shows why preference over non-REE impurities and within REEs need separate tests. It does not establish continuous-column separation of real waste, product purity or long-term service life.

后续比较应同时记录：单离子或混合进料、质量或摩尔浓度、pH、固液比、平衡时间、容量是否来自拟合，以及酸洗后金属和有机组分去向。2019 年此样品的循环表现不能自动替代其他 UiO-66 配方在缓冲液中的验证。A follow-up comparison should record single-ion versus mixed feed, mass versus molar concentration, pH, solid/liquid ratio, equilibration time, fitted versus measured capacity, and metal/organic release during regeneration. Reuse of this 2019 sample does not automatically validate other UiO-66 formulations in buffers.
