水先如何破坏一个局部位点？How does water first disrupt a local site?

这篇研究用反应力场与元动力学考察七种 Zn 基 MOF。问题是低水含量极限下，一个水分子靠近金属—配体连接处后，发生局部水解需要跨越怎样的能垒。This study uses a reactive force field and metadynamics for seven Zn-based MOFs. It asks what barrier a local hydrolysis event faces when one water molecule approaches a metal–linker connection in the low-water-loading limit.

靠近Approach

水与 Zn 位点相互作用。Water interacts with a Zn site.

解离Dissociate

水的 O 与 H 进入不同局部结合环境。Water O and H enter different local bonding environments.

解释Interpret

比较局部反应能垒，再说明与整体稳定性的距离。Compare local reaction barriers and state the gap to whole-material stability.

它没有进行 REE 吸附、液态水浸泡或循环再生实验。对水解早期步骤的解释有助于稳定性讨论，但不能替代真实工作液中的评价。It does not measure REE uptake, liquid-water immersion or regeneration cycles. Early hydrolysis mechanisms inform stability discussions but do not replace testing in actual working solutions.

用两个距离追踪反应，再用偏置促进采样Track two distances and apply bias to improve sampling

CV-1 是 Zn 与水氧 Oᵥ 的距离；CV-2 是配体 O（或 ZIF 中 N）与水氢 Hᵥ 的距离。它们把“水靠近”和“质子转移”投影到二维自由能空间。CV-1 is the distance from Zn to water Oᵥ. CV-2 is the distance from linker O, or N in a ZIF, to water Hᵥ. They project water approach and proton transfer onto a two-dimensional free-energy space.

三种“时间”对应三种含义Three kinds of time mean different things

k = (kBT / h) exp[−ΔG‡ / (RT)]

文中用 300 K 下一阶局部反应假设，将能垒转换为反应时间。表 2 极长或极短的数字仍是这一模型的推算，未由浸泡、失重、浸出、孔隙保持或动态穿透实验验证。The authors convert barriers to reaction times using a first-order local-event assumption at 300 K. 小的能垒差，为什么会放大成速率差？Why can a small barrier difference amplify a rate difference?
