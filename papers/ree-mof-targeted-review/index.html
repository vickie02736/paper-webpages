稀土元素（REE）在水中通常带电，并被水分子围绕。它们彼此性质相近，所以“优先捕集稀土”不一定能区分稀土内部的成员。MOF 是带孔的连接网络，功能团则是安排在网络上的特定化学基团。Rare-earth elements (REEs) are typically charged and surrounded by water in solution. Their similar chemistry means preferential REE capture need not distinguish individual REEs. A MOF is a porous connected network; functional groups are specific chemical groups placed on it.

群组分离Group separation

目标是把多种稀土一起从 Fe、Al 等背景中富集。输出可以是稀土混合物，还不是某一种纯稀土。Enrich several REEs together from backgrounds such as Fe and Al. The output may remain an REE mixture, not one pure rare earth.

单元素分离Individual separation

目标是区分性质很接近的稀土离子。必须给出竞争对象；钕对铁的证据不能替代钕对镨的证据。Distinguish closely related REE ions. Name the competitor: evidence for Nd versus Fe cannot replace evidence for Nd versus Pr.

后合成修饰Post-synthetic modification

先形成框架，再加入或改变基团。这可能改变结合环境，也可能堵孔、造成浸出或改变骨架；需要重新表征。Form the framework first, then add or modify groups. This can change binding but also block pores, leach or alter the framework, requiring renewed characterization.

几轮循环之后，继续测什么？What to measure beyond a few cycles?

两阶段路线：每一阶段留下什么、放走什么A two-stage route: what is retained or passed onward?

  1. 先定群组富集目标Define group enrichment

    从复杂进料中优先留下稀土、减少非稀土背景。要同时量进入固体的目标与杂质；浓度下降本身不证明选择性。Preferentially retain REEs from a complex feed while reducing non-REE background. Quantify both targets and impurities entering the solid; concentration loss alone does not establish selectivity.

  2. 制备下一阶段进料Prepare the next-stage feed

    从负载材料释放或转移稀土，记录损失、酸碱用量与新组成。第一阶段吸得多，不保证可得到适合下一阶段的溶液。Release or transfer REEs from the loaded material, recording losses, chemical use and the new composition. High first-stage loading does not guarantee a suitable next-stage feed.

  3. 再区分稀土成员Separate individual REEs

    对富集后的稀土混合物，检验定向基团或印迹位点的实际分离，再量最终产品的纯度和回收率。Test targeted groups or imprinted sites on the enriched REE mixture, then measure final product purity and recovery.

综述提出这条混合路线；没有在同一个端到端实验中证明所有候选材料组合。中间转移与物料衡算是验证此建议所需的步骤。The review proposes this hybrid route; it does not demonstrate every candidate pairing in one end-to-end experiment. Intermediate transfer and mass balances are requirements for validating the proposal.
