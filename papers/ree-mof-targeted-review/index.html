颗粒、聚合物和混合基质膜有助于收集及连续操作，但阻塞、稀释、溶胀和界面疲劳会影响长期工作容量。以整颗复合物计量，比较压降、穿透和再生之后的性能。Granules, polymers and mixed-matrix membranes aid handling and continuous use, while obstruction, dilution, swelling and interfacial fatigue affect long-term working capacity. Normalize to the whole composite and compare pressure drop, breakthrough and post-regeneration performance.

膜中的低透过量可能来自目标离子被保留，不能直接叫“低吸附能力”；拒绝率、透过选择性、吸附去除率和回收率需要各自的定义。来源：§3.2.3 的混合基质膜案例。Low membrane passage can reflect target retention rather than weak adsorption. Rejection, transport selectivity, adsorptive removal and recovery need separate definitions. Source: mixed-matrix membrane example in §3.2.3.

几轮循环之后，继续测什么？What to measure beyond a few cycles?

综述指出，多数材料只测试数轮再生。需要吸附与解吸的分步质量衡算、晶相／孔隙与浸出分析，以及真实进料变化下的更长运行。不能把容量保留百分比当作溶液去除率。The review notes that most materials undergo only a few regeneration cycles. Measure separate adsorption/desorption balances, phases, pores, release and longer operation with changing real feeds. Capacity retention is not aqueous removal efficiency.

耐酸测试也必须说明酸的种类、浓度、接触时间及温度。更强的解吸可能加快失活；低剂量药剂的效率与完全再生的寿命之间需要权衡。Acid tests require identity, concentration, duration and temperature. More aggressive stripping may accelerate deactivation, creating a trade-off between reagent demand and regeneration/lifetime.

两阶段策略是建议，尚需流程证明The two-stage strategy is a proposal needing process validation

§4 建议先群体富集，再定向分离单一或相邻稀土。这样可降低下一阶段的基质负担，但也新增转移、洗脱与再调节步骤。必须检验总回收率、产品纯度、浓缩倍数及物料消耗。Section 4 proposes group enrichment followed by targeted individual/neighboring-REE separation. This can reduce matrix burden while adding transfer, elution and readjustment. Test total recovery, purity, concentration factor and material consumption.
