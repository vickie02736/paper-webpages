区分吸水、保孔与循环功能；用补充材料看清“稳定”二字的实验边界。Separate water uptake, pore retention and cycling function; use the SI to see exactly what a stability claim covers.

Nicholas C. Burtch · Himanshu Jasuja · Krista S. Walton
Chemical Reviews 114, 10575–10612 (2014)

高中知识起步。读完应能解释：两篇文献结论相反时，先核对什么；为什么“稳定类别”、证据置信度和真正的化学机制是三件事。Start with high-school knowledge. Learn what to check when two reports disagree, and why stability category, evidence confidence and chemical mechanism are three different things.

晶体还在，孔和功能也一定还在吗？If the crystal survives, do its pores and function survive?

先认识对象：有孔的分子支架Meet the object: a porous molecular scaffold

金属有机框架（MOF）由含金属的连接中心和有机分子连接体组成，内部留有微小空隙。气体或水分子可以进入，并停留在内表面，这叫吸附。水也可能破坏连接，这叫水解损伤；两件事不能只凭“吸进了多少水”区分。A metal–organic framework (MOF) joins metal-containing junctions with organic molecular linkers, leaving tiny internal spaces. Gas or water molecules may enter and attach to internal surfaces: adsorption. Water can also damage the links by hydrolysis. Water uptake alone does not distinguish these processes.

MIL-100(Fe)、UiO-66 和 La-BTB 是材料的名称；括号中的 Fe 表示铁元素。它们不是仪器，也不是稳定性分数。MIL-100(Fe), UiO-66 and La-BTB are material names; Fe denotes iron. These names are neither instruments nor stability scores.

这篇 2014 年综述把水吸附、耐水性和应用性能放在同一个问题中：MOF 接触水后，是否仍能完成原来的任务？它整理了当时的文献与稳定性证据，不能作为所有材料在今天、所有条件下的稳定性认证。This 2014 review connects water adsorption, water resistance and performance: after contact with water, can a MOF still do its intended job? It organizes the literature and evidence available then, rather than certifying every material under every present-day condition.

晶体结构Crystal structure

用 X 射线照射粉末，记录各角度的散射强度：粉末 X 射线衍射（PXRD）。有序重复的原子排列产生特征峰，可以对比处理前后的峰形。Illuminate powder with X-rays and record scattered intensity at different angles: powder X-ray diffraction (PXRD). Repeated ordered atomic arrangements produce characteristic peaks that can be compared before and after exposure.

PXRD 比较长程有序结构；衍射图接近，并不排除局部断键、缺陷变化或少量无定形产物。PXRD probes long-range order. Similar patterns do not rule out local bond cleavage, changing defects or minor amorphous products.

可用孔隙Accessible porosity

让气体进入材料，测定吸附量，再按 BET 模型估计每克固体可接近的表面积。单位 m²/g 就是“每克有多少平方米”；这里的克通常指回收并处理后的固体。Admit gas, measure uptake, then use the BET model to estimate accessible surface area per gram of solid. m²/g means square metres per gram, usually of the recovered and prepared solid.

再测吸附与 BET 表面积，检查孔道是否仍可进入。干燥和活化步骤本身也可能影响结果。Repeat adsorption and BET surface-area measurements to test pore access. Drying and activation can themselves affect the result.

循环功能Cycling performance

先吸附，再用加热或改变压力等方式让被吸附物离开，这叫再生。重复操作，比较每轮能捕集、又能释放的量，才是在检查循环功能。Adsorb, then release the adsorbed substance, for example by heating or changing pressure: regeneration. Repeating this and comparing what is captured and released tests cycling function.

在实际湿度、混合物和再生条件下比较工作容量；饱和吸水量不是循环寿命。Compare working capacity under the actual humidity, mixture and regeneration protocol. Saturated uptake is not cycle life.

SI 的价值：它记录了相同材料在不同温度、介质、暴露时间和表征方法下的不同结果，而不只给出一个“稳定／不稳定”标签。Why the SI matters: it records different outcomes for the same material under different temperatures, media, exposure times and characterization methods, beyond a single stable/unstable label.

需要观察What to observe

PXRD：粉末 X 射线衍射。BET：由气体吸附推算的比表面积。SEM／TEM／AFM：分别观察形貌或表面。多种观测共同约束结论，但没有任何一种单独覆盖全部损伤机制。PXRD means powder X-ray diffraction; BET is a specific surface area inferred from gas adsorption; SEM, TEM and AFM examine morphology or surfaces. Combining observations constrains the conclusion, but no single measurement covers every failure mechanism.

四类标签，与证据置信度分开读Read the four categories separately from evidence confidence

综述以耐受条件将材料分为四类，并另列证据置信度。标签回答“报告耐受了什么”，置信度回答“支持它的观测是否充分”。这里保留 2014 年原分类的历史含义。The review places materials in four categories by their reported exposure tolerance and separately assigns evidence confidence. The category asks what conditions were survived; confidence asks how well observations support that assignment. The original 2014 taxonomy is retained here.

类别轴：耐受条件Category axis: exposure tolerance

中性液水、酸碱水溶液、沸水、水蒸气和低湿气氛是不同实验。低湿空气中保存完好，不能代替浸液测试。Neutral liquid water, acidic/basic solutions, boiling water, vapour and low-humidity atmospheres are distinct exposures. Storage in low humidity cannot substitute for immersion.

证据轴：观测充分程度Evidence axis: strength of support

原文高／中／低置信度综合表征、测试条件与文献支持。低置信度不等于材料一定差，高置信度也不是某个数值成功概率。The original high/medium/low confidence reflects characterization, conditions and literature support. Low confidence does not mean intrinsically poor material, and high confidence is not a numerical success probability.

*“热力学稳定”在这里操作性地包括室温中性水至少一周，或酸／碱／沸水至少一天的耐受证据；这类观测不能单独确定指定水解反应的 ΔG 符号。具体条目仍须查看 SI 的条件与观测。*Here “thermodynamically stable” is an operational category including at least a week in neutral room-temperature water, or at least a day in acidic/basic/boiling water. Survival alone cannot establish the sign of ΔG for a specified hydrolysis reaction. Inspect each SI entry’s actual conditions and observations.

实际例子：La-BTB 的 SI 条目记录了热水、酸／碱条件下保留 PXRD，却没有相应 BET 测试。因此它可以进入原文较耐受的类别，同时只有低置信度；“试过很强的条件”和“各种损伤都查过”不是同一件事。（SI 表 S4，p. S7）A concrete example: the La-BTB SI entry reports retained PXRD in hot water and acidic/basic conditions but supplies no matching BET test. It can therefore enter a resistant historical category with low confidence. A severe exposure and comprehensive damage checks are different things. (SI Table S4, p. S7)

吸水多少，与怎样吸水，是两个问题How much water and how it adsorbs are different questions

先固定温度、逐步增加气体压力，并记录每克材料吸了多少，这一组数据叫吸附等温线。水蒸气压力越接近该温度下的饱和值，空气越潮湿。Hold temperature fixed, increase gas pressure step by step, and record uptake per gram: this produces an adsorption isotherm. As water-vapour pressure approaches saturation at that temperature, the air becomes more humid.

等温线固定温度，观察吸附量随水蒸气压力的变化。低相对压力区的吸附有助于判断水与位点的亲和性；高负载区还受可用孔体积影响。横坐标 P/P₀ 是该温度下压力相对于饱和蒸气压的比值。An isotherm holds temperature fixed and tracks uptake as water-vapour pressure changes. Low-relative-pressure uptake informs water–site affinity, while high loading also depends on accessible pore volume. P/P₀ is pressure divided by saturation vapour pressure at that temperature.

早开始吸附Early adsorption

较低 P/P₀ 即出现明显吸附，可与强水–框架作用有关。较大的饱和容量仍不能仅用这个起始区预测。Substantial uptake at low P/P₀ can indicate strong water–framework interactions. The onset region alone does not determine the eventual saturation capacity.

推迟孔填充Delayed pore filling

较弱的初始亲和性可伴随水团簇形成与突跃式孔填充。吸附／脱附滞后也可能来自孔结构或柔性变化，不能单凭滞后认定塌陷。Weak initial affinity can coexist with water clustering and abrupt pore filling. Hysteresis can also involve pore structure or flexibility; it does not by itself demonstrate collapse.

吸附热管理关注可在吸附与再生条件之间反复转移的水量。最大吸水量、适宜的吸附台阶、脱附能耗和循环后保留量共同决定应用价值，不能只按单个饱和值排名。Adsorption heat management depends on water reversibly transferred between adsorption and regeneration conditions. Maximum uptake, step location, desorption energy and retained cycling performance jointly matter; one saturated value cannot rank overall usefulness.

怎样阅读综述中的等温线分类？How should the review’s isotherm classes be read?

原文采用当时引用的 IUPAC 等温线类别并讨论 I、II、IV、V 型。页面用亲和性、孔填充和滞后来解释物理含义，不把 2014 年文中的类别描述宣称为今天的完整分类标准；也不绘制没有原始数据的定量曲线。The source uses the IUPAC isotherm classes it cited at the time, discussing types I, II, IV and V. This page explains affinity, pore filling and hysteresis without presenting the 2014 account as a complete current classification or drawing quantitative curves without source data.

可逆“呼吸”，与不可逆损伤分别检查Separate reversible breathing from irreversible damage

有些框架的连接可以像铰链一样转动，使孔变宽或变窄；“呼吸”指这种结构变化，不是生命活动。活化指移走原先占据孔隙的分子，让孔可以接受新分子。是否恢复，必须实际测量。Some framework connections can move like hinges, widening or narrowing pores. “Breathing” describes this structural change, not biological activity. Activation removes molecules already occupying pores so that new molecules can enter. Recovery must be measured.

MIL-53 等柔性框架可在大孔和窄孔状态之间转换。水诱导的衍射变化并不自动意味着化学分解；但只看到一个新晶相，也不能自动证明可以恢复。Flexible frameworks such as MIL-53 can switch between large-pore and narrow-pore states. A water-induced diffraction change does not automatically indicate chemical decomposition, but a new crystalline phase alone does not prove reversibility.

They combine different studies and samples, rather than a controlled lifetime comparison of one batch.

所报告的耐受或损伤Reported survival or damage

反例、条件差异与证据缺口Counterconditions, protocol differences and evidence gaps

特别注意：UiO-66 和 MIL-100(Fe) 的条目都包含“PXRD 没有大变化、BET 却大幅下降”的结果。看起来仍结晶，不等于可用孔隙和分离功能已被证明保留。UiO-66 and MIL-100(Fe) both include observations of major BET loss with little change in PXRD. Retained-looking crystallinity does not establish retained accessible porosity or separation function.

稳定性机制：终态差，与到达终态的障碍Mechanism: endpoint energetics and the barrier between them

综述讨论怎样延缓水解：金属与连接体结合牢固；连接体不容易被替换（交换惰性）；较大的周围基团阻挡接近（空间保护）；或降低对水的亲和性（疏水性）。这些策略可能影响不同步骤，不能只凭一种结构特征保证耐水。The review discusses ways to delay hydrolysis: strong metal–linker bonding; slow replacement of linkers (substitution inertness); bulky surrounding groups limiting approach (steric protection); or reduced affinity for water (hydrophobicity). These affect different steps; one structural feature cannot guarantee water resistance.

ΔG：反应前后ΔG: before and after reaction

ΔG = G(产物) − G(反应物)。必须规定水、溶液物种、产物、温度和组成，才能讨论水解是否有利。ΔG = G(products) − G(reactants). Specify water, solution species, products, temperature and composition before judging whether hydrolysis is favourable.

ΔG‡：沿路径的障碍ΔG‡: the barrier along a pathway

ΔG‡ = G(过渡态) − G(反应物)。较慢的配体交换或受阻的水接近可延缓分解；短期存活不确定终态能量差。ΔG‡ = G(transition state) − G(reactants). Slower ligand exchange or hindered water access can delay decomposition; short-term survival does not determine the endpoint difference.

强键、高连接度和适合的酸碱匹配是设计线索，而非单参数保证。节点连接度描述一个簇连向多少配体／连接体，不是把簇内每个金属原子的配位数都写成同一个数字。Strong bonding, high connectivity and suitable acid–base matching are design guides, rather than single-parameter guarantees. Node connectivity counts links from a cluster; it is not the coordination number of every metal atom within that cluster.

模拟能不能直接证明耐水？Can simulation directly establish water stability?

巨正则蒙特卡洛（GCMC）：让计算机随机尝试插入、移走或移动分子，按统计规则接受或拒绝，估计给定温度和外界供气条件下平均吸附多少。分子动力学（MD）则按力与运动方程推进位置，研究随时间变化的运动。两者回答的问题不同。Grand canonical Monte Carlo (GCMC) randomly attempts insertion, removal or movement of molecules, accepts or rejects moves by statistical rules, and estimates mean uptake at a chosen temperature and external gas conditions. Molecular dynamics (MD) advances positions using forces and equations of motion to study change over time. They address different questions.

GCMC 可研究给定框架上的吸附平衡；普通固定拓扑模型不会自行断键。MD 的输运、反应力场或第一性原理反应路径也回答不同问题。水模型、电荷、极化、缺陷、有限尺寸和模拟时间都会影响结论，不能把“在模型里吸水”直接转成“真实框架不会水解”。GCMC can study adsorption equilibrium on a chosen framework; a conventional fixed-topology model cannot break bonds. MD transport, reactive force fields and first-principles pathways address different questions. Water models, charges, polarization, defects, finite size and simulation time affect the conclusion. Adsorption in a model cannot directly establish survival against real hydrolysis.

水还会改变其他组分的捕集Water also changes capture of other components

水可能竞争位点，也可能促进另一组分的结合或化学反应。因此“湿条件下容量较高”和“材料可逆、稳定”必须由不同证据支持。Water can compete for sites, promote binding of another component or enable a chemical reaction. Higher uptake under humid conditions and reversible material stability therefore need separate evidence.

CO₂：适量水与过度填孔CO₂: limited water versus pore filling

综述讨论 HKUST-1 预吸附少量水后 CO₂ 吸附增强、而充分水合时容量下降的案例。一次提高不能证明长期湿气循环可靠。The review discusses increased CO₂ uptake after a limited water preload in HKUST-1, followed by lower capacity on extensive hydration. A one-time increase does not establish reliable long-term humid cycling.

NH₃：初次捕集不等于再生NH₃: first capture is not regeneration

HKUST-1 的湿态初次 NH₃ 捕集可较高，但复用和 PXRD、NMR、N₂ 吸附表征揭示不可逆变化。水参与的反应可能消耗原材料。Initial humid NH₃ capture in HKUST-1 can be higher, while reuse and PXRD, NMR and N₂ adsorption reveal irreversible changes. Water-assisted reactions can consume the original material.

来源：正文 §2.2、图 20–22。水相部分还讨论污染物去除与传感；如金属交换或框架变化参与信号，溶液浓度变化本身不能自动归因于孔内物理吸附。Sources: §2.2 and Figs. 20–22. The aqueous section also discusses pollutant removal and sensing. When metal exchange or framework changes contribute to a response, solution concentration changes alone cannot be assigned automatically to physical pore adsorption.

保护策略，要同时看保留下来的功能Protection strategies must preserve useful function

改金属或连接体可以改变结合；用大基团挡住局部位置，称位阻；让多套网络互相穿插，称互穿；给表面加一层保护物，称包覆。下面三种包覆案例比较防水、保孔和分子进出之间的代价。Changing the metal or linker changes bonding; bulky groups can block local access (steric shielding); multiple networks can thread through each other (interpenetration); and added surface layers form coatings. These three coating examples compare water protection, retained pores and molecular access.

疏水化也有两种不同目标：减少水进入孔中，或允许水进入但减少它在易反应节点附近聚集。接触角或整体吸水下降，不能单独辨别哪种机制成立。Hydrophobization can target two different effects: reducing water entry or allowing entry while limiting clusters near reactive nodes. A contact angle or reduced overall uptake alone cannot distinguish those mechanisms.

延伸到 REE 吸附：把保护、捕集和解吸连起来Extension to REE adsorption: connect protection, capture and stripping

REE 是稀土元素的英文缩写。这里关心的是溶液中的稀土离子能否被捕集，并在需要时释放出来；这只是从综述原则向另一应用的延伸。REE stands for rare-earth elements. The question here is whether rare-earth ions in solution can be captured and later released; this extends the review’s principles to another application.

以下是讲解者基于综述原则提出的实验阅读框架。本文不是稀土吸附研究，没有报告 REE 容量或分离系数。水稳定标签只能帮助选择起点。The following is an explainer extension based on the review’s principles. This paper is not a rare-earth adsorption study and reports no REE capacities or separation factors. A water-stability category can only help select a starting point.

  1. 在真实进料中考察支撑体：pH、盐、竞争离子、络合剂、接触时间与温度同时记录。Test the support in the actual feed, recording pH, salts, competing ions, ligands, contact time and temperature.
  2. 同步核对去向：REE 浓度降低、固相 REE、框架金属／配体溶出与沉淀；不能只以溶液去除率证明吸附。Check material balances: decreased solution REE, solid-phase REE, released node metal/linker and precipitates. Removal percentage alone does not establish adsorption.
  3. 检查功能化的收益与损失：选择性位点是否可接近，保护层是否也阻碍传质，修饰后孔隙是否保留。Assess functionalization gains and losses: site access, transport through a protective layer and retained porosity.
  4. 把酸性洗脱、再生与下一轮吸附纳入稳定性测试，分别报告结构、孔隙、浸出和工作容量的变化。Include acidic stripping, regeneration and the next adsorption cycle, reporting changes in structure, porosity, leaching and working capacity separately.
