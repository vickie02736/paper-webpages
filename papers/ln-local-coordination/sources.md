## Site-lab structure audit

On 27 October 2026, the material-selector diagrams were compared against the supplied article Figure 1 (p. 48538), Figure 4 (p. 48540), Figure 5 (p. 48541), and Figure 6 (p. 48543). The earlier teaching schematic incorrectly reused a four-metal motif for every material. It has been replaced with: (1) a three-metal [Cr3O] oxo-trimer motif for Cr-MIL-101 and Cr-MIL-101-NH2; (2) a six-metal [Zr6O4(OH)4] oxo-cluster projection for UiO-66, UiO-66-NH2-ideal, UiO-66-NH2-defective, UiO-66-NH-PAA and UiO-68-NH2; (3) a longer terphenyl-linker cue for UiO-68-NH2; (4) a persistent missing-linker cue for the defective sample; and (5) an amide-linked PAA cue, –NH–CO–CH2–P(O)(OH)2, with explicit N/O/P markers. The drawings remain simplified local schematics, not full unit cells or crystallographic coordinates. Green Ln–O lines terminate at explicit O donor markers; dashed lines indicate neighbor relations.
