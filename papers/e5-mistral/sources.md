## Complete visual inventory

- **Hero vector-space SVG:** original teaching schematic of instruction/text → Mistral → query, positive, hard negative. Based on [§3.2, Eqs. 1–3, PDF p. 4 (11900)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=4). Coordinates, point distances, clusters and connecting segments are illustrative; they are not measured embeddings, cosine values or a projection. The hero caption and SVG description identify this. Adjacent 63.1 / 66.6 are real **Table 1** MTEB-56 averages for synthetic-only / full-data training, [p. 5 (11901)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=5).
- **Four scale tiles:** approximately 500k synthetic examples, 150k unique instructions, 93 languages, fewer than 1k fine-tuning steps. [§4.1, pp. 4–5](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=4) and [Appendix A, p. 12](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=12). These do not count foundation-model pretraining or data-generation compute.
- **Three role cards / central idea:** GPT-4 / GPT-35-Turbo generate supervision; pretrained Mistral learns embeddings by contrastive fine-tuning. [§1, §3–4](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=1). Editorial organization, not an experimental figure.
- **Three-step data pipeline and symmetric/asymmetric branch:** brainstorm task definitions → generate examples → parse, exact-match deduplicate and combine. [Figure 1, p. 3 (11899)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=3), §3.1, Appendix C. Symmetric STS/bitext tasks skip brainstorming; not all templates produce triplets.
- **Six task buttons and example variants:** short-long **167k**, long-short **122k**, short-short **13k**, long-long **17k**, STS **99k**, bitext **89k**, all rounded values from [Figure 2, p. 5](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=5). All six text examples are authored teaching examples, not claimed original samples or model outputs. Short-short / long-long omit explicit negatives, consistent with Appendix C. STS illustrates a positive pair rather than reproducing the entire original template. Bitext keeps the source language visible and provides an English gloss.
- **Language proportion bar:** English **43.1%**, all other languages together **56.9%**, from Figure 2. The two segment widths are proportional. The accompanying bottom-75-languages ≈1k each and 93-language coverage come from §4.1. The aggregated remainder is not the original figure's narrower “Others” slice (19.8%).
- **Generation mix card:** approximately 75% GPT-4 / 25% GPT-35-Turbo, approximately 180M tokens, [§4.1, p. 5](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=5). This is text, not an inferred model-quality chart.
- **Instruction interaction (two states):** paraphrase / sentiment instruction changes the highlighted target in a fixed document collection. [§3.2, Eq. 1, p. 4](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=4) supports query-only instruction and reusable document embeddings. Both states are explicitly human-assigned relationships; no retrieval scores or actual inference are claimed.
- **EOS token/vector diagrams (query and document):** append EOS, use its last-layer state, same backbone for query/document, query has instruction while document does not, output dimension **4096**. §3.2 and Limitations [p. 9 (11905)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=9). E5 uses causal last-token pooling, not the NV-Embed latent-attention module.
- **InfoNCE formulas:** page defines `s = cosine / τ` and uses `exp(s)` in the loss; the original writes `φ = exp(cosine / τ)` in Eqs. 2–3. These are algebraically equivalent, including positive and in-batch / hard negatives. Original temperature **0.02**, [§3.2, p. 4](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=4).
- **Probability/loss slider (all values):** fixed positive cosine **0.80**, ordinary negative **0.30**, adjustable hard-negative **0.40–0.85**, τ **0.01–0.20**. These are labeled toy inputs. Bars calculate a three-candidate softmax exactly, sum to one, and loss is `−log(p_positive)`; they are not empirical training curves or benchmark predictions. Stable softmax subtracts the maximum logit. Both sides of the hard-negative/positive crossover are supported.
- **Expandable training recipe tiles/text:** Mistral-7B-v0.1, LoRA rank **16**, approximately **42M** trainable parameters, batch **2048**, length **512**, **1 epoch**, learning rate **1e−4**, warmup **100** steps, weight decay **0.1**, **1** hard negative/query, full-data mixture of synthetic + **13** public datasets and approximately **1.8M** sampled examples, approximately **18h on 32 V100s**. [§4.2, p. 5](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=5) and [Appendix A, p. 12](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=12). This is the full-data recipe, not synthetic-only compute.
- **Evidence chart, MTEB tab:** full **66.6**, synthetic+MS-MARCO **64.5**, synthetic **63.1**, BGE-large-en-v1.5 **64.2**, GTE-large **63.1**, E5-large-v2 **62.3**, [Table 1, p. 5](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=5). English 56-dataset average. The adjacent **+2.4** is points, not percent.
- **Evidence chart, BEIR tab:** same models/settings, **56.9 / 52.2 / 46.9 / 54.3 / 52.2 / 50.6** from Table 1 retrieval column: 15-dataset average nDCG@10. It is not the 56-dataset aggregate.
- **Evidence chart, instruction tab:** natural-language instruction **64.5**, no instruction **60.3**, task-type prefix **60.3**, [Table 5, p. 8 (11904)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=8). This ablation uses synthetic+MS-MARCO, not full data. All three evidence tabs use a zero baseline and common **0–80** axis; widths are value/80.
- **Contrastive-pretraining callout:** Mistral **66.6→66.7**, XLM-R-large **58.0→63.7**, [Table 7, p. 13 (11909)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=13), also Figure 3. This concerns extra contrastive pretraining, not removal of language-model pretraining.
- **Data-diversity callout:** MS-MARCO-only **62.7 MTEB / 54.4 BEIR** vs synthetic+MS-MARCO **64.5 / 52.2**. Table 5. This correctly shows that the aggregate improvement does not imply retrieval improves.
- **Limit/boundary cards:** MIRACL dev 16-language average nDCG@10 **62.9 vs mE5-large 66.5**, [Tables 2/6, pp. 6/13](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=13). Personalized synthetic passkey retrieval >90% within 32k after window/RoPE changes comes from [§5.2, Figures 4–5, pp. 7–8](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642.pdf#page=7); it is not a real-document QA accuracy claim. Cost/data caveats: Limitations and Appendix B.
- **Three quiz states and two related-paper cards:** original teaching recap of generation role, full-data **66.6** setting and query-only instructions, checked against the sources above. Correct/wrong feedback does not introduce experimental data. Related-paper arrows are reading suggestions, not empirical evidence.

## Review result and limits

- Source values, checkpoint identity, task counts, chart proportions, EOS information flow and formulas match the cited formal paper. No scientific value was changed in this audit; the page now links this ledger.
- Visually inspected the original Figure 1, Figure 2 and Table 1 and checked all remaining source tables/text through the formal PDF. The authored examples are not subject to exact experimental-value matching.
- This ledger certifies provenance and scientific consistency of the represented modules, not independent reproduction of training or verification of unpublished training data. Browser layout / bilingual / keyboard regression is tracked by the repository audit task.
