MOF-B1/B2 是含 Zn（锌）和硼氧六环相关有机单元的框架。硼氧六环由三个硼与三个氧组成；这类连接可以受水影响。本文明确讨论吸附饱和后的不可逆结构塌陷，并把材料用作牺牲型吸附剂。MOF-B1/B2 contain Zn (zinc) and organic units involving boroxine rings, composed of three boron and three oxygen atoms. These linkages can respond to water. The article explicitly discusses irreversible collapse at saturation and use as a sacrificial sorbent.

允许捕集过程中消耗原材料，再从负载产物中取回目标。好坏要结合材料用量、产品收率、杂质和废液，不能用循环次数同可再生材料直接比较。The original material may be consumed during capture, with the target recovered from the loaded product.
