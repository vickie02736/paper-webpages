捕集伴随骨架消耗Capture consumes the framework

MOF-B1 把硼氧六环与 Zn–羧酸配位结合，形成作者提出的层状离子交换捕集体系。它偏好重稀土，但吸附伴随 Zn 释放，饱和后不可逆坍塌；回收路线因此采用牺牲型吸附与后续湿法处理。MOF-B1 combines boroxine units and Zn–carboxylate coordination in a proposed layered ion-exchange capture system. It favors heavy REEs, while uptake releases Zn and saturation irreversibly collapses the framework. Recovery therefore uses sacrificial adsorption followed by wet processing.

从吸附固体到 Yb₂O₃From loaded solid to Yb₂O₃

  1. 2 M HCl 酸浸，溶解吸附后的材料。Leach the loaded material with 2 M HCl.
  2. 搅拌下加入 10 wt% 草酸溶液，再用稀氨水控制 pH；图中没有给具体目标 pH。Add 10 wt% oxalic acid while stirring, then control pH with dilute ammonia; no numeric target pH is shown.
  3. 老化 4 h，离心收集，80 °C 过夜干燥。Age for 4 h, collect by centrifugation and dry overnight at 80 °C.
  4. 以 2 °C/min 升至 800 °C，保温 4 h 煅烧。Heat at 2 °C/min to 800 °C and hold for 4 h.

正文图 6 给出上述工艺与产物 Yb₂O₃ XRD。它支持获得该晶相，不提供独立定量纯度、全流程 Yb 回收率或所有共捕集元素的闭合质量衡算。Zn 留在液相也意味着后续废液处理需求，不能将“自净化”理解为金属消失。Main Figure 6 provides this process and product Yb₂O₃ XRD. It supports the crystalline phase, without independent quantitative purity, overall Yb recovery or closed balances for all co-captured elements. Zn in the liquid remains a downstream effluent issue; “self-purification” does not make metal disappear.

怎样继续验证这条路线？What would further validate this route?

需要共同报告：每克吸附剂的 Yb 工作容量与 Zn 损失、选择性与回收率、原料与氧化物杂质组成、酸／草酸／氨和热处理消耗，以及吸附后固液两相的质量衡算。Joint reporting is needed for Yb working capacity and Zn loss per gram, selectivity and recovery, feed/oxide impurity composition, acid/oxalate/ammonia and heating consumption, and balances over both post-adsorption phases.

本文与再生型 UiO-66 / MIL-101 的过程目标不同。比较时应以最终产品与资源消耗为基准，并纳入接触时间、pH、进料、吸附剂用量和循环定义；文献容量榜不是受控工艺比较。Its process objective differs from regenerable UiO-66/MIL-101 systems. Comparisons should use final product and resource consumption while accounting for time, pH, feed, dose and cycle definition. Literature capacity rankings are not controlled process comparisons.
