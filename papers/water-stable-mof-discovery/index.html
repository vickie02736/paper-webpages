水稳定 × 高吸水：
机器学习如何筛选 MOFWater stability × high uptake:
screening MOFs with machine learning

把稳定性标签、模拟吸附量和预测适用范围分开，才能读懂从数十万结构到 74 个候选的筛选。Keep stability labels, simulated uptake and prediction coverage distinct to understand a screen from hundreds of thousands of structures to 74 candidates.

Akash K. Ball · Gianmarco G. Terrones · Shuwen Yue · Heather J. Kulik
ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces 17, 35971–35985 (2025)

能吸很多水，是否也能留在水中？Does high water uptake imply survival in water?

这项研究把两个不同的预测串联起来：先用已有分类器筛选水稳定性，再训练回归模型预测水吸附量，最后筛选同时满足两项条件的 MOF。它得到的是计算候选集，而非完成实际水处理验证的材料清单。This study combines two different predictions: an existing classifier screens water stability, and a new regressor predicts water uptake. Their intersection gives computational candidates, rather than materials already validated in water-treatment operation.

稳定性分类Stability classification

输入结构特征，输出达到所用文献稳定性标签的概率。本文筛选阈值为 ≥0.7；这不是对任意 pH、盐度和时间的可靠性承诺。Structural descriptors give a probability for the literature-derived stability label. The screening threshold is ≥0.7; this does not guarantee survival at arbitrary pH, salinity or duration.

水吸附回归Water-uptake regression

以 736 个收敛 GCMC 计算训练模型，预测指定模拟条件下的体积水吸附量。刚性框架模拟不允许水解断键，因此不能同时检验骨架稳定。The regressor learns volumetric water uptake from 736 converged GCMC calculations. A rigid-framework adsorption simulation cannot hydrolyse bonds and therefore cannot test framework survival.

主要结果：按 SI 表格，538,228 个候选 → 92,183 个预测水稳定结构 → 5,827 个通过不确定性筛选的水吸附预测 → 74 个高吸水候选。其中仅 4 个展示了后续 GCMC 吸附量核验。Main result, following the SI tables: 538,228 candidates → 92,183 predicted water-stable structures → 5,827 uptake predictions passing the uncertainty screen → 74 high-uptake candidates. Only four have a subsequent GCMC uptake check shown.

训练数据怎样从 98 个标签变成 736 个结构？How do 98 labelled entries lead to 736 structures?

训练集从 WS24 数据库中“热力学稳定”的 98 个 MOF 起步。这个名称在数据库中操作性地指浸水超过一周仍稳定，不能等同于测得水解 ΔG > 0。Training begins with 98 WS24 MOFs labelled “thermodynamically stable.” Operationally, that label denotes survival after more than a week in water; it does not establish a measured positive hydrolysis ΔG.

12 种官能团覆盖羟基、氰基、巯基、胺、羧酸、磺酸、硝基、酰胺与吡咯等化学环境。14 个几何描述符与 176 个 RAC 化学描述符组成初始特征；移除 28 个不变特征后剩 162 个。RAC 用图中原子间的属性相关性编码局部环境，不是显式量子反应计算。Twelve groups span hydroxyl, cyano, thiol, amines, carboxylic/sulfonic acids, nitro, amide and pyrrole environments. The initial features comprise 14 geometric and 176 RAC chemical descriptors; removing 28 invariant features leaves 162. RACs encode local environments through correlations of atomic properties on the graph, rather than explicit quantum reaction calculations.

加亲水基团，也可能损失可用孔体积Hydrophilic groups can also consume usable pore volume

水与位点的相互作用、孔体积和孔径共同影响吸附。一个疏水但孔大的 MOF，与一个较亲水但孔小的 MOF，不会仅按亲水性排序。正文图 3 提供了一个直观例子。Water–site interactions, pore volume and pore size jointly affect uptake. A hydrophobic large-pore MOF and a more hydrophilic small-pore MOF cannot be ranked by hydrophilicity alone. Main Fig. 3 gives an illustrative pair.

GCMC 计算了什么，又没有计算什么？What does the GCMC calculation represent?

规定的计算环境Specified computational conditions

RASPA2；300 K；水组分外部压力 1 atm（101.3 kPa）；刚性 2 × 2 × 2 超胞；TIP4P/2005 水模型，UFF 框架参数与 EQeq 电荷。初始化 80,000 周期、生产 20,000 周期；截断 12.5 Å，静电用 Ewald。RASPA2 at 300 K and an external water-component pressure of 1 atm (101.3 kPa); a rigid 2 × 2 × 2 supercell; TIP4P/2005 water, UFF framework parameters and EQeq charges. Initialization uses 80,000 cycles and production 20,000; the cutoff is 12.5 Å with Ewald electrostatics.

压力和模型的边界Pressure and model limits

这里的 1 atm 不是相对湿度，也不是“总压 1 atm 的普通湿空气”。刚性框架不会发生水解、溶出、柔性开孔或金属交换，未收敛结构也不能被当作吸水量为零。The 1 atm value is neither relative humidity nor ordinary humid air with a total pressure of 1 atm. A rigid framework cannot hydrolyse, dissolve, open flexibly or exchange metal ions. Nonconverged structures cannot be assigned zero uptake.

收敛筛选使用最后 5 个记录周期的相对标准偏差低于 5%。SI 用 73 个结构比较 EQeq 与 DDEC 电荷，吸附量 Pearson r = 0.83，且 73% 结构的 EQeq 结果较高；这支持趋势可比性，但不证明绝对量普遍准确。Convergence requires a relative standard deviation below 5% over the last five recorded cycles. For 73 structures, SI compares EQeq with DDEC charges: uptake has Pearson r = 0.83, with EQeq higher in 73% of cases. This supports trend comparison without establishing universal quantitative accuracy.

SI 文本 S3 与图 S6（第 S19–S20 页）还对照了接近水饱和蒸气压、298 K 的文献数据；条件并非本训练集的 300 K、1 atm。ZIF-8 的亲疏水分类存在偏差，因此不能把这些对照当成全部材料的实验验证。SI Text S3 and Fig. S6 (pp. S19–S20) compare literature data near water saturation pressure at 298 K, which differs from the training calculations at 300 K and 1 atm. The ZIF-8 affinity classification disagrees, so the comparisons do not validate every material experimentally.

换一种划分，模型是在回答另一个问题A different split asks a different generalization question

随机划分可能让同一母体的近亲结构同时出现在训练与测试中。按官能团或母体分组，才分别检验未见修饰或未见骨架的外推。切换查看 SI 表 S14 的完整六模型比较；MAE 越小越好，r 是相关系数。A random split can place close derivatives of the same parent in both training and test sets. Grouping by functional group or parent instead tests new modifications or new frameworks. Switch among all six model results from SI Table S14: lower MAE is better; r is the correlation coefficient.

作者为筛选选择官能团划分训练的 MLP。其 11 个 RFA 特征含 7 个几何、4 个 RAC；SHAP 归因中几何贡献合计 80.7%，化学贡献 19.3%。这是本模型、本数据集的归因，相关特征可能分摊重要性；它不证明化学仅有 19.3% 的因果作用。The screening model is the MLP trained with a functional-group split. Its 11 RFA features comprise seven geometric and four RAC descriptors; SHAP attributes 80.7% to geometry and 19.3% to chemistry. This attribution depends on the model and dataset, and correlated descriptors can share credit. It does not assign a 19.3% causal role to chemistry.

怎样避免数据泄漏？How is leakage controlled?

公开训练脚本按分组划分训练与测试，并用训练集均值和标准差归一化。特征选择从 5 个变量开始，按三折交叉验证增添变量；超参数调节使用训练侧五折验证。MLP 借用其他模型选择的 RFA 特征。测试结果与筛选规则应分开读，不能因最终候选表现好而反过来调优测试集。The released training script uses group-based train/test separation and training-set means and standard deviations for scaling. Feature selection starts with five variables and adds features using threefold cross-validation; hyperparameters use fivefold training-side validation. MLP uses RFA features chosen with other models. Test evaluation and candidate screening must remain distinct; candidate performance cannot justify tuning back against the test set.

拒绝一部分预测，误差才会下降Lower error comes with rejecting predictions

潜空间距离 LSD 衡量一个结构与训练样本在模型表示中的接近程度；它取 10 个最近训练邻居的距离，再归一化。LSD 不是预测正确的概率，也不是置信区间。Latent-space distance (LSD) measures proximity to training examples in the model representation, using the ten nearest training neighbours and normalization. It is neither a probability of correctness nor a confidence interval.

四个候选的核验：仍然是吸附模拟Four candidate checks are still adsorption simulations

正文图 7 对两个 CoRE 结构与两个假想结构报告了 MLP 预测和后续 GCMC 水吸附量。切换查看单个结构；两条共用 0–900 cm³(STP) cm⁻³ 量尺。Main Fig. 7 reports MLP predictions and subsequent GCMC water uptake for two CoRE entries and two hypothetical structures. Select a structure; both bars share a 0–900 cm³(STP) cm⁻³ axis.

这些是 4 个吸附量的模型对照；未测量溶出、断键或水处理性能，不能验证所有 74 个结构的水稳定性。四例中 MLP 均低于 GCMC，也不能推断对所有未见材料预测都是下界。These are four computational uptake comparisons. They do not measure leaching, bond breaking or treatment performance, and cannot validate water stability for all 74 candidates. MLP is lower than GCMC in these four cases; that does not make every unseen prediction a lower bound.

与 REE 吸附有什么联系？What transfers to REE capture?

以下为讲解者延伸，本文没有测量 REE 捕集、盐选择性、脱盐通量或实际再生性能。WOJZOM 的 Sm 是框架节点金属，不是被它从溶液捕获的目标离子。Explainer extension: this article measures no REE capture, salt selectivity, desalination flux or operational regeneration. Sm in WOJZOM is a framework-node metal, rather than an ion captured from solution.

  1. 拆开任务：分别预测进料条件下的骨架存活、REE 结合与可解吸性，不能用高吸水量代理 REE 选择性。Separate tasks: predict framework survival under the feed conditions, REE binding and recoverable desorption; high water uptake cannot proxy REE selectivity.
  2. 按新骨架或配体家族分组划分训练和测试。实际目标是陌生材料时，随机划分的好成绩可能高估可迁移性。Split training and test data by new framework or ligand families. Strong random-split scores may overestimate transfer to unfamiliar materials.
  3. 对候选同时测量目标 REE、节点金属与连接体；报告 pH、竞争离子、固液比、时间、解吸与循环物料衡算，再讨论实际用途。For candidates, measure target REE, node-metal and linker balances together; report pH, competing ions, solid/liquid ratio, time, stripping and cycle balances before claiming practical utility.
