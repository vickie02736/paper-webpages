先认识这座“分子搭成的架子”First picture a framework built from atoms and molecules

节点与连接体Nodes and linkers

MOF 是金属有机框架。把金属原子或金属原子团看成连接点，把含碳分子看成连接杆；真正的连接来自化学键，不是机械卡扣。连接杆称为“连接体”或“配体”。A metal–organic framework (MOF) links metal atoms or clusters with carbon-containing molecules. Think of junctions and struts, joined by chemical bonding. The molecular struts are called linkers or ligands.

孔与吸附Pores and adsorption

架子中留下很小的空隙，称为孔。水分子可以进入，并受到孔壁吸引而停留，这里讨论的是吸附。材料有孔，并不意味着水一定愿意进去。The framework leaves tiny spaces called pores. Water molecules may enter and be retained by attractions to the pore walls: adsorption. Having space does not guarantee that water will favour occupying it.

亲水与修饰Water affinity and modification

“亲水”表示与水相互作用较有利，“疏水”表示相对不利。把连接体上的部分原子换成或接上一个原子小组，称为官能团修饰；它既改变对水的吸引，也会占据空间。Hydrophilic means more favourable interactions with water; hydrophobic means less favourable. Adding or replacing a small group of atoms on a linker is functionalization. The added group can change water affinity and occupy pore space.

金属有机框架（MOF）由金属节点和有机连接体构成多孔网络。水可以进入孔隙，也可能改变金属—连接体的结合。研究者想同时找到能保持框架、又能容纳较多水的材料；几十万个候选逐一合成、浸水、测吸附，成本很高。Metal–organic frameworks (MOFs) are porous networks of metal nodes and organic linkers. Water can enter their pores but can also alter metal–linker bonding. The goal is to find materials that retain a framework and accommodate substantial water; synthesizing and testing hundreds of thousands of candidates is costly.

以往的吸水模拟不一定同时检验水稳定性；仅有稳定性标签也不知道能吸多少水。本文将已有稳定性分类器与新训练的吸水量回归模型组合。分类预测类别，回归预测数值；二者学习的参照答案不同。Earlier uptake simulations did not necessarily assess water stability; a stability label alone does not quantify uptake. This work combines an existing stability classifier with a newly trained uptake regressor. Classification predicts a category; regression predicts a number. They learn from different reference answers.

“特征”是把结构转成可计算的数值，例如孔径、孔体积及原子属性关系。稳定性概率不是任意 pH、盐度和时长下的存活保证；GCMC 参照值也不是实验真值。Features turn structures into numerical inputs such as pore size, pore volume and atomic-property relationships. A stability probability is not a survival guarantee at arbitrary pH, salinity or duration; a GCMC reference is not experimental ground truth.

VPOV：有多少体积被算作孔？VPOV: how much volume counts as pore space?

STP：把水的“数量”换成统一的气体体积刻度STP: express an amount of water on a common gas-volume scale

同样多的气体，温度、压力不同，占的体积也不同。STP 是 Standard Temperature and Pressure（标准温度和压力），用统一条件报告体积，才便于比较。这里将吸附水的分子数量折算成理想气体等效体积，再除以框架体积。The same amount of gas occupies different volumes at different temperatures and pressures. STP means Standard Temperature and Pressure: a common reporting condition makes volumes comparable. Here the amount of adsorbed water is expressed as an ideal-gas-equivalent volume, then divided by framework volume.

吸附水的数量 → STP 气体等效体积 → 每 1 cm³ 框架的吸水量Amount of adsorbed water → STP gas-equivalent volume → uptake per 1 cm³ of framework

GCMC 计算了什么，又没有计算什么？What does the GCMC calculation represent?

GCMC 全称 Grand Canonical Monte Carlo，中文为“巨正则蒙特卡洛”。先把它理解为一种用随机尝试来做统计的计算：框架形状固定，水分子可以加入、移走或改变位置；程序试很多排列，估算稳定条件下平均留下多少水。GCMC stands for Grand Canonical Monte Carlo. Think of a statistical calculation using random trial changes: the framework stays fixed, while water molecules can be added, removed or moved. Many sampled arrangements estimate how much water remains on average under steady conditions.

“蒙特卡洛”强调随机抽样；“巨正则”表示模型区域可以与外界交换分子，区域内的分子数不固定。这里的“平衡”不是水分子停止运动，而是进入与离开在统计上达到平衡，平均吸附量不再系统变化。“Monte Carlo” refers to random sampling. “Grand canonical” allows molecules to exchange with a reservoir, so the number inside the model region can change. Equilibrium does not mean that molecules stop moving: exchanges balance statistically and average uptake no longer changes systematically.

为什么不是只挑“能量最低”的排列？因为在一定温度下，不止一种排列会出现。程序按能量变化、温度和外界条件给尝试一个接受概率；有些不利变化也可能被接受。足够多次抽样才用来估算平均值，不是随意挑几张快照算平均。Why not keep only the lowest-energy arrangement? At a given temperature, multiple arrangements can occur. Trial changes are accepted with probabilities determined by energy changes, temperature and reservoir conditions; some unfavourable changes can also be accepted. A sufficiently sampled collection estimates the average, rather than a few arbitrarily chosen snapshots.

来源：SI 表 S14。RFR 随机森林；GBR 梯度提升；KRR 核岭回归；MLP 多层感知机。RFA 表示递归特征增加。Source: SI Table S14. RFR: random forest; GBR: gradient boosting; KRR: kernel ridge regression; MLP: multilayer perceptron. RFA denotes recursive feature addition.

MLP 是一种从例子中学习“输入 → 数值”关系的神经网络模型。先看一个比较：同一种 MLP 模型（按每种划分分别训练），在按官能团分组的测试中平均误差为 59，在按母体分组的测试中为 128（单位同吸水量，SI 表 S14）。换一种“没见过”的材料，预测难度就会变；详细模型比较放在机制层。这里的平均绝对误差，是先算每个预测错了多少（不论偏高还是偏低），再求平均。An MLP is a neural-network model that learns an input-to-number relationship from examples. Start with one comparison: the same MLP architecture, retrained for each split, has mean absolute error 59 under functional-group splitting and 128 under base-MOF splitting, in uptake units (SI Table S14). A different kind of unseen material changes the prediction task. The Mechanism level contains the full model comparison. Mean absolute error averages how far each prediction misses its reference, regardless of whether it is too high or too low.

MAE：一次算清“平均错多少”MAE: work out the average error

MAE = (1/n) ∑i=1…n |q̂i − qi|

n 是被评价的结构数，q̂ᵢ 是回归预测，qᵢ 是本研究的 GCMC 参照吸附量。绝对值避免高估和低估相互抵消。比较 MAE 时须保留测试集定义；不同划分改变了样本与任务，不能把差值当成同一任务上的纯算法增益。n counts evaluated structures; q̂ᵢ is the regression prediction and qᵢ the GCMC reference uptake here. Absolute values prevent over- and underestimates from cancelling. MAE comparisons must retain the test definition: changing the split changes samples and task, so differences are not pure algorithm gains on an identical task.

教学假设，不是原数据：三项参照为 100、200、300，预测为 120、180、360，则绝对误差是 20、20、60，MAE = 100/3 ≈ 33.3 cm³(STP)/cm³。Teaching assumptions, not reported data: references 100, 200, 300 and predictions 120, 180, 360 give absolute errors 20, 20, 60; MAE=100/3≈33.3 cm³(STP)/cm³.

这里 cm³(STP)/cm³ 是每单位框架体积所吸附水对应的标准状态气体体积，不是孔里实际装了几百倍体积的液态水。该算例只解释误差度量，没有训练或运行论文模型。Here cm³(STP)/cm³ expresses adsorbed water as a standard-state gas volume per framework volume, not hundreds of volumes of liquid inside a pore. The example explains the metric; it does not train or run the paper’s model.

怎样避免数据泄漏？How is leakage controlled?

公开训练脚本按分组划分训练与测试，并用训练集均值和标准差归一化。特征选择从 5 个变量开始，按三折交叉验证增添变量；超参数调节使用训练侧五折验证。MLP 借用其他模型选择的 RFA 特征。测试结果与筛选规则应分开读，不能因最终候选表现好而反过来调优测试集。The released training script uses group-based train/test separation and training-set means and standard deviations for scaling. Feature selection starts with five variables and adds features using threefold cross-validation; hyperparameters use fivefold training-side validation. MLP uses RFA features chosen with other models. Test evaluation and candidate screening must remain distinct; candidate performance cannot justify tuning back against the test set.

拒绝一部分预测，误差才会下降Lower error comes with rejecting predictions

模型先把每种结构变成一串内部数字，这个表示空间叫“潜在空间”。如果新材料的数字表示离训练材料很远，就需要超出以往经验猜测，称为外推。作者因此暂不采用这些预测；靠近已见材料也不保证一定正确。The model represents each structure internally as a set of numbers, called a latent representation. A new structure far from training examples in that representation requires a guess beyond familiar experience: extrapolation. The authors therefore withhold such predictions; proximity still does not guarantee correctness.

潜空间距离 LSD 衡量一个结构与训练样本在模型表示中的接近程度；它取 10 个最近训练邻居的距离，再归一化。LSD 不是预测正确的概率，也不是置信区间。Latent-space distance (LSD) measures proximity to training examples in the model representation, using the ten nearest training neighbours and normalization. It is neither a probability of correctness nor a confidence interval.

假设你的新 MOF 母体及其衍生物都没有进入训练集。哪种论文测试最接近这个使用场景？先选择理由，再查看反馈。Suppose neither your new parent MOF nor its derivatives occur in training. Which reported test best matches that use case? Choose a reason to see feedback.

以下为讲解者延伸，本文没有测量 REE 捕集、盐选择性、脱盐通量或实际再生性能。WOJZOM 的 Sm 是框架节点金属，不是被它从溶液捕获的目标离子。Explainer extension: this article measures no REE capture, salt selectivity, desalination flux or operational regeneration. Sm in WOJZOM is a framework-node metal, rather than an ion captured from solution.

  1. 拆开任务：分别预测进料条件下的骨架存活、REE 结合与可解吸性，不能用高吸水量代理 REE 选择性。Separate tasks: predict framework survival under the feed conditions, REE binding and recoverable desorption; high water uptake cannot proxy REE selectivity.

  3. 对候选同时测量目标 REE、节点金属与连接体；报告 pH、竞争离子、固液比、时间、解吸与循环物料衡算，再讨论实际用途。For candidates, measure target REE, node-metal and linker balances together; report pH, competing ions, solid/liquid ratio, time, stripping and cycle balances before claiming practical utility.
