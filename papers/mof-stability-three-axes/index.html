MOF 稳定性的
三个维度MOF stability:
chemical, thermal and mechanical dimensions

“保留了衍射峰”离“在工艺中仍能工作”还有哪些距离？这篇综述提供了一个跨越溶液、温度和受力条件的评价框架，也说明增强稳定性为何常伴随孔隙与活性的取舍。What separates retained diffraction peaks from continued function in a process? This review links solution, temperature and mechanical challenges, while explaining trade-offs between persistence, porosity and activity.

Ashlee J. Howarth · Yangyang Liu · Peng Li · Zhanyong Li · Timothy C. Wang · Joseph T. Hupp · Omar K. Farha
Nature Reviews Materials 1, 15018 (2016)

“稳定”必须对应一种处理和一种读数“Stable” needs an exposure and a measurement

这篇综述把稳定性定义为结构对降解的抵抗，同时强调：能在使用时间内保持结构的材料，仍可能在热力学上更倾向于转变成致密相。因此，实际使用中的稳定与全局热力学最低能量不是同一个概念。The review defines stability as resistance of the structure to degradation, while noting that a functionally persistent framework can remain thermodynamically disfavoured relative to a denser phase. Operational persistence and global thermodynamic stability are different concepts.

这些是评价维度，不是同一分数的三个加权项。某个材料耐热，不会自动证明它耐缓冲盐、耐酸洗或耐压片。These are evaluation dimensions, not weighted parts of one score. Heat resistance does not automatically establish tolerance to buffer salts, acid regeneration or pellet pressing.

水解、质子化与配体竞争：只给 pH 不够Hydrolysis, protonation and competition: pH alone is insufficient

节点–连接体键Node–linker bonds

水解可以产生质子化连接体和羟基/水配位的节点。酸性条件促进连接体质子化，碱性条件使 OH⁻ 替代连接体成为可能；键强度与置换速率都重要。Hydrolysis can produce protonated linkers and hydroxide/water-coordinated nodes. Acid promotes linker protonation, while base enables OH⁻ substitution. Both bond strength and substitution kinetics matter.

基质的配位能力Matrix coordination

相同 pH 的溶液也可能具有不同破坏性。综述特别指出，强配位阴离子如磷酸根的挑战，不等同于弱配位酸碱的挑战。Solutions at the same pH can differ in chemical stress. The review highlights that strongly coordinating anions such as phosphate are a different challenge from weakly coordinating acids and bases.

结构与孔隙共同测量Measure order and porosity

部分降解样品仍可能保留熟悉的 PXRD 图案。气体吸附可进一步检验可及孔隙；只有 PXRD 的证据应保留这个限制。A partially degraded sample can retain a familiar PXRD pattern. Gas sorption additionally probes accessible porosity; evidence based on PXRD alone retains that limitation.

TGA 平台说明质量变化有限，不独立证明完整孔隙A TGA plateau does not independently establish an intact pore network

TGA 测量随温度变化的样品质量。失重可以来自客体溶剂、节点配位溶剂或框架分解；节点脱水和相变也可能在大规模失重之前发生。热处理气氛、升温程序和持续时间都会影响结论。TGA tracks sample mass with temperature. Mass loss can arise from guest solvent, coordinated solvent or framework decomposition; node dehydration and phase changes can precede major mass loss. Atmosphere, heating programme and duration affect the interpretation.

  1. 先明确处理Specify the exposure

    温度、气氛与保温时间；N₂ 中的结果不自动代表空气或氧化气氛。Temperature, atmosphere and hold time. An N₂ result does not automatically describe air or an oxidizing atmosphere.

  2. 再检查结构Check structure

    原位 PXRD 或热循环后的衍射，区分结构保持与相变。In situ PXRD or post-cycle diffraction distinguishes structural persistence from phase change.

  3. 再检查孔隙Check porosity

    热循环后 N₂/CO₂ 吸附与吸附–脱附行为，补足质量曲线看不到的功能变化。Post-cycle N₂/CO₂ sorption and adsorption/desorption behaviour probe changes that a mass trace cannot resolve.

综述列举的 50–100 °C 客体释放、100–200 °C 配位溶剂释放，是常见示例，不是所有 MOF 的固定温区。本页不绘制一条虚构的“通用 MOF TGA 曲线”。The review’s typical 50–100 °C guest release and 100–200 °C coordinated-solvent release are examples, not fixed intervals for every MOF. No invented universal MOF TGA curve is shown.

热与水共同作用，是另一套挑战Heat and water create a combined challenge

水热稳定性需要记录温度、蒸汽压力或湿度、暴露时间和循环。室温液态水浸泡、湿气暴露与高温蒸汽不能自动互换；疏水孔口对液态水团簇和孤立水分子的阻挡也可能不同。Hydrothermal stability needs temperature, steam pressure or humidity, exposure time and cycles. Room-temperature liquid immersion, humid gas and hot steam are not interchangeable. A hydrophobic aperture can also affect cohesive liquid-water clusters differently from isolated molecules.

综述用 MIL-53 与 MOF-5 的计算研究说明：节点–连接体键强度之外，水置换连接体的活化能也有区别。不同计算模型、反应路径和条件下的能垒，不宜直接拼成统一寿命排序。The review uses computational studies of MIL-53 and MOF-5 to illustrate differences in activation barriers for water-driven linker displacement, in addition to bond strengths. Barriers from different models, pathways and conditions should not be combined into one lifetime ranking.

疏水性可帮助保护骨架，但吸附或催化又需要目标物进入孔道。排斥水的策略与水相功能之间可能存在取舍。Hydrophobicity can protect a framework, while adsorption or catalysis requires target access. Excluding water can create a trade-off with aqueous function.

压片、球磨和干燥都可能造成结构变化Pressing, milling and drying can all change structure

综述把机械失效描述为受力后的相变、孔隙部分塌陷或无定形化。例子中，ZIF-8 在特定原位 XRD 压力实验中于超过 0.34 GPa 时发生不可逆无定形化；这不是所有样品和压片程序的统一允许压力。Mechanical failure can include pressure-induced phase change, partial pore collapse or amorphization. One reviewed in situ XRD study finds irreversible ZIF-8 amorphization above 0.34 GPa. This is not a universal allowable pelletization pressure for every sample or protocol.

干燥也会施加力Drying applies forces too

从孔中移除强相互作用溶剂时，毛细力可以破坏骨架，表面上看起来类似水解。需比较浸泡后湿态、溶剂置换后与活化后的结构和孔隙，才能定位损失发生阶段。Removing strongly associated pore solvent can generate capillary forces that damage a framework and resemble hydrolysis. Comparing wet, solvent-exchanged and activated states helps locate when loss occurs.

真实缺陷影响力学Real defects affect mechanics

理想无缺陷晶体的计算模量可能高于实际缺陷样品。缺失连接体、残留调节剂、孔内溶剂、受力方向和拓扑都可能改变机械响应。Calculated moduli of ideal defect-free crystals can exceed values for real defective samples. Missing linkers, residual modulators, pore solvent, loading direction and topology can change the response.

几个力学指标测的是不同响应Mechanical quantities describe different responses

体积模量描述均匀压缩响应；剪切模量描述形变响应；杨氏模量描述单轴弹性；硬度描述压入阻力。除体积模量之外，综述提醒其他指标可能有明显方向依赖。一个大的模量不等同于对全部加工过程都不失效。Bulk modulus describes uniform compression; shear modulus describes shear distortion; Young’s modulus describes uniaxial elasticity; hardness describes resistance to indentation. The review notes possible anisotropy in properties other than bulk modulus. A large modulus does not guarantee survival of every processing step.

更强的连接有帮助，也会改变合成与功能Stronger connections help, with synthesis and function trade-offs

键强与置换惰性Bond strength and inertness

较高价金属与含氧连接体、金属–氮连接和更慢的配体置换，可提高某些条件下的稳定性。但“键更强”不独立决定动力学，也可能使结晶纠错更困难。Higher-valent metal/oxygen-linker combinations, metal–nitrogen bonding and slower ligand exchange can improve persistence in some conditions. Stronger bonding does not alone determine kinetics and can make crystallization error correction harder.

连接度与缺陷Connectivity and defects

理想 UiO-66 的 Zr₆ 节点为 12 连接；综述也展示 6、8、10 连接 Zr₆ 节点。这里的数字指连接节点的羧酸根数量，不是 Zr 原子数或单个 Zr 的配位数。Ideal UiO-66 has 12-connected Zr₆ nodes; the review also shows 6-, 8- and 10-connected Zr₆ nodes. These numbers count linker-associated carboxylates at a node, not Zr atoms or the coordination number of one Zr.

屏蔽与互穿Shielding and catenation

疏水取代基、节点屏蔽与网络互穿可能降低水接近易损键的机会，也可能减少孔容或反应物可及性。缺陷可提供活性位点，同时改变结构承载能力。Hydrophobic groups, shielding and network catenation can limit water access to vulnerable bonds, while reducing pore volume or reactant access. Defects can create active sites while changing structural load-bearing capacity.

以上是综述归纳的设计策略，不能直接转换成对任何新 MOF 的稳定性保证。为某种 REE 接枝的官能团，也必须单独验证其连接是否经得住吸附和再生基质。These are design strategies synthesized by the review, not guarantees for every new MOF. A functional group grafted for REE capture also needs its own attachment stability tested during adsorption and regeneration.

检查一份“稳定性声明”缺少什么Inspect what a stability claim leaves untested

这是基于综述评价思路的教学工具。选项代表假想报告已提供的证据，不会预测某种材料的寿命，也不计算总分。This teaching tool uses the review’s evaluation logic. Choices represent evidence supplied in a hypothetical report; it predicts no material lifetime and calculates no overall score.

化学、热和机械检查各有自己的处理窗口。勾选更完整的证据仍不意味着所有基质和温度都稳定；真实报告还要给出样品批次、缺陷/活化状态、暴露条件、循环和检测限。Each evidence category has its own exposure window. Selecting fuller evidence does not establish stability in every matrix and temperature. A real report still needs batch, defect/activation state, exposure conditions, cycles and detection limits.

从催化综述连接到 REE 吸附Connecting a catalysis-focused review to REE adsorption

原文的应用主线是液相与高温气相催化：框架不仅要保持结构，还要允许反应物到达活性位点。综述列举的不同极端稳定性记录来自不同材料，明确没有一款 MOF 同时体现其列出的全部极限。The application focus is liquid-phase and hot-gas catalysis: a framework must persist while admitting reactants to active sites. The extreme stability examples come from different materials; the review explicitly states that no single MOF embodies all of its listed limits.

对 REE 吸附的延伸是本页的研究联系：吸附液、竞争阴离子、酸洗、洗涤、干燥和成型可以是不同挑战。需要把最终工作材料作为对象，记录每一步后的孔隙、组分与吸附性能；本综述并未测定 REE 容量或分离系数。The extension to REE adsorption is this page’s research connection: feed, competing anions, acid stripping, washing, drying and shaping impose different stresses. Test the final working material and track porosity, composition and uptake after each step. This review does not measure REE capacities or separation factors.
