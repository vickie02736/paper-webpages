01 · 为什么需要它Why this model

同一套表示，能不能跨过不同性质？Can one representation cross property boundaries?

MOF 的化学空间由金属节点、有机连接体和拓扑共同决定。只为一个应用训练的模型，往往无法直接迁移到另一个应用。A MOF’s chemical space is set jointly by metal nodes, organic linkers and topology. A model trained for one application often does not transfer directly to another.

LOCAL

局部化学Local chemistry

原子类型、邻居和键距离由 CGCNN 变成逐原子的 embedding，保留金属簇与 linker 的差异。Atom types, neighbours and bond distances become atom-wise embeddings through CGCNN, retaining differences between metal clusters and linkers.

GLOBAL

全局孔结构Global pore structure

甲烷探针产生 3D energy grid，再切成 5 Å patch，表达可进入的空间与势能分布。A methane probe produces a 3D energy grid, split into 5 Å patches to describe accessible space and energy distribution.

TRANSFER

迁移学习Transfer learning

先用无监督式的结构任务预训练，再用 5,000–20,000 个带标签样本微调性质头。Pre-train on structure-oriented tasks, then fine-tune a property head with only 5,000–20,000 labelled structures.

03 · 预训练机制Pre-training

三个目标，逼模型学会 MOF 的骨架。Three objectives make the model learn a MOF’s skeleton.

MTP、VFP、MOC 分别约束拓扑、孔隙和局部化学；三者一起训练时，表示同时包含全局与局部线索。MTP, VFP and MOC constrain topology, porosity and local chemistry; together they make the representation carry both global and local cues.

[VOL] 补回把能量网格统一到 30 Å 立方体时丢掉的原始晶胞体积信息。位置 embedding 与 modal-type embedding 逐元素相加。[VOL] restores original cell-volume information lost when energy grids are interpolated to a 30 Å cube. Position and modal-type embeddings are added element-wise.

自检Check yourself
为什么模型需要 [VOL] token？Why does the model need a [VOL] token?
把 energy grid 插值到统一的 30 Å 立方体会抹掉原晶胞体积；[VOL] 把这条全局信息重新提供给 Transformer。Interpolating every energy grid to a 30 Å cube removes the original cell volume; [VOL] gives that global signal back to the Transformer.
为什么 MOC 对 band gap 更有帮助？Why does MOC help band-gap prediction?
MOC 直接要求逐原子区分金属节点与有机 linker，强化了带隙更依赖的局部化学表征。MOC directly distinguishes metal-node and organic-linker atoms, strengthening the local chemical representation that band gaps depend on more strongly.
