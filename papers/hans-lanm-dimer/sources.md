## Teaching model and graphic boundaries

The concentration control uses the paper's **ideal monomer–dimer equilibrium**, not a new fit or experimental titration. With all concentrations expressed in the same unit:

```text
P = M + 2D
K = M² / D
M = (sqrt(K² + 8KP) − K) / 4
D = (P − M) / 2
fraction of protein chains in dimers = 2D / P = 1 − M/P
```

For numerical stability, the equivalent expression `M = 2KP / (sqrt(K² + 8KP) + K)` can be used. The unit of K is concentration. A chain fraction is not the fraction of molecular particles that are dimers; the latter would be `D/(M+D)`.

- Apo and Dy curves may use the measured central K values 117 and 60 µM while explicitly preserving their uncertainties in nearby text. They do not constitute independent curve measurements.
- A La curve evaluated at **0.4 µM** uses the reported **upper limit** as a conservative illustrative boundary. Because the actual K is smaller, the actual equilibrium dimer chain fraction would be higher under this simplified model. Do not label 0.4 µM as an exact measured La constant, and do not plot an invented exact La K value without an explicit teaching assumption.
- No Nd or R100K Kdimer is reported. They are excluded from quantitative dimer-fraction calculations rather than assigned a fabricated value.
- The model assumes one ideal homogeneous solution equilibrium at fixed metal state. It excludes metal loading equilibria, coupled folding, chelator speciation, exchange kinetics, aggregation, resin geometry, transport and flow. Changing P illustrates why assembly depends on concentration; it does not predict column purity, yield or a separation factor.
- Mechanism schematics show ligand denticity and hydrogen-bond connectivity conceptually. They are not atom coordinates or measured distances unless an actual reported distance is labeled. Displacement or line strength in a schematic does not quantify energy.
- Any stage-by-stage column diagram is a reading guide for the reported workflow. It must not imply that a drawn curve or draggable elution window is measured data unless traced to the actual source data.
- The multi-element data and binary data use different feed compositions and equilibrium conditions. Neither can be converted to the 95:5 dynamic-column product purities by a one-step algebraic identity.

## Source handling and unresolved boundaries

The supplied PDFs are the primary evidence even when the publisher website requires an access redirect. Complete article metadata, exact values and figure captions were read from them; rasterized checks were used for Fig. 4, Extended Data Fig. 6 and Extended Data Tables 2–3, whose image-based tables are not present in plain-text extraction.

This page reports a model-mixture laboratory demonstration. It does not establish process-scale costs, lifecycle savings, performance on an untreated industrial waste stream, adjacent-lanthanide separation for every pair, or long-term cycling stability. Such broader claims require separate evidence. Connections to MOF recognition or future ligand design are labeled explainer analysis rather than experiments from this protein paper.
