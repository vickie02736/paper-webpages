容量、选择性与稳定性：三个问题Capacity, selectivity and stability: three questions

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

钪离子、结合基团与水在竞争什么？What competes among scandium, binding groups and water?

Sc(III) 表示带三个正电荷的钪离子；它在水中被水分子包围。AA 是丙烯酸，带有羧酸基团。作者把 AA 引入多孔 MIL-101，希望增加可参与结合的部位，但这些部位也受酸碱性和孔道可达性的影响。Sc(III) is scandium with three positive charges, surrounded by water in solution. AA is acrylic acid, which carries a carboxylic-acid group. The authors introduce AA into porous MIL-101 to add binding environments, whose use also depends on acidity and pore access.

羧酸与质子Carboxylic acid and protons

羧酸基团可失去 H⁺，变成带负电的羧酸根。改变 pH 会改变这种状态，也会改变金属离子在水中的化学形态。A carboxylic-acid group can lose H⁺ to form a negatively charged carboxylate. Changing pH affects this state and the metal ion’s solution chemistry.

测得容量与拟合极限Measured uptake and fitted limit

一定进料下的平衡量受可提供金属量限制；Langmuir qmax 则是按一系列浓度拟合的模型参数。不能把二者直接当作重复实验的差异。Equilibrium uptake at one feed is limited by available metal. Langmuir qmax is a parameter fitted across concentrations. Their difference is not simply disagreement between replicate tests.

Sel 是对数指标Sel is logarithmic

本文对两个分配比的比值取 log，压缩倍数差异。比值为 1 时 Sel 为 0；原文未明确底数，所以不把 Sel 反算成精确倍数。The paper applies log to a ratio of two distribution measures, compressing multiplicative differences. A ratio of 1 gives Sel = 0. The base is unspecified, so Sel is not converted back to an exact fold preference.

读完应能把材料名称中的两个投料数、三种容量，以及混合体系的 Sel 放回各自的实验设计中。You should place the two feed numbers in a material name, three uptake values and mixed-system Sel within their own experimental designs.

丙烯酸改性让 MIL-101 获得更多含氧结合基团，同时减少气体可及孔隙。真正的考验是：与另外 15 种 REE 共存时，它还会不会优先捕获 Sc？Acrylic acid functionalization adds oxygen-containing binding groups while reducing gas-accessible porosity. The key test is whether the material still favours Sc when 15 other REEs are present.

增加可结合的羧基Add carboxyl binding sites

在 Cr-MIL-101 合成中加入丙烯酸（AA），再进行 AA 聚合。表面滴定显示酸性基团增多，同时 N₂ BET 面积下降。Acrylic acid (AA) is added during Cr-MIL-101 synthesis and then polymerized. Surface titration reports more acidic groups, accompanied by a lower N₂ BET area.

用混合溶液检验偏好Test preference in mixtures

单离子中的最大容量，不等于混合溶液中对 Sc 的选择性。论文另外测试了 16 种 REE 共存体系。A single-ion capacity does not establish preference for Sc in mixtures. The paper separately tests a solution containing 16 REEs.

把酸洗纳入材料评价Include acidic regeneration

0.3 mol/L HCl 能解吸 Sc，但“可再生”仍需同时查看循环吸附率、残余质量和组分浸出。HCl at 0.3 mol/L desorbs Sc, while reuse still needs adsorption cycles, residual mass and component-leaching evidence.

核心结果：作者选择 0.075-AA-0.072@MIL-101，在测试的混合 REE 条件中优先吸附 Sc。其单离子 Langmuir 最大容量为 90.21 mg/g；这不是材料筛选实验中 62.45 mg/g 的同一测量。Core result: the authors select 0.075-AA-0.072@MIL-101, which favours Sc in the tested REE mixture. Its single-ion Langmuir maximum is 90.21 mg/g, a different quantity from the 62.45 mg/g material-screening result.

选择性会受基质与指标定义限制Matrix composition and metric definitions still matter

在 Cu、Zn、Mn、Co、Al 共存实验中，较高杂质浓度抑制 Sc 吸附；当共存离子对 Sc 的浓度比为 2:1 时，正文报告 Sc 吸附率降至约 62%。因此，16-REE 混合液的表现不能直接替代实际浸出液验证。Coexisting Cu, Zn, Mn, Co and Al suppress Sc uptake at higher impurity concentrations. At the reported coexisting-ion/Sc concentration ratio of 2:1, Sc adsorption falls to about 62%. A 16-REE solution therefore does not substitute for testing an actual leachate.
