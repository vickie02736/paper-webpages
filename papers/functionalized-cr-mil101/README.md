# Functionalized Cr-MIL-101 and REE adsorption

Bilingual interactive explainer for [Lee et al.2018, DOI10.1021/acsami.8b07130](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.8b07130).

The page compares five materials across five single-ion experiments, recalculates two published isotherm fits, and explains what the competition and regeneration results establish. It preserves source-table rankings that differ from the paper’s general prose summary. See [sources.md](sources.md) for the full article/SI ledger and identified inconsistencies.

Uses the shared Research Atlas reading system. Default Chinese; full English. Measured capacities and fitted model parameters are stored separately in `data.js`. Originals remain in the local research cache.

Catalogue integrated; first-publication date 2026-11-02. Full static, production bibliography, catalogue/taxonomy and all-43-page browser checks pass (3,649 interaction actions).
