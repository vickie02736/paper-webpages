给孔道加结合基团，可能让稀土吸附增加，即使气体能进入的表面积下降。这篇论文比较五种 Cr-MIL-101，关键是同时看接在哪里、吸的是谁，以及再生后还剩什么。Adding binding groups can increase rare-earth uptake even as gas-accessible area falls. This paper compares five Cr-MIL-101 materials, asking where groups attach, which ion is captured and what survives regeneration.

ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces 10, 23918–23927 (2018)

这篇论文比较了什么？What does the paper compare?

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

孔道提供空间，化学基团提供结合环境Pores provide space; chemical groups provide binding environments

Cr-MIL-101 是铬（Cr）节点与有机连接体构成的多孔框架。功能化是在框架上引入额外化学基团。它可能增加离子的结合位置，同时占用部分孔空间；因此“面积最大”不等于“吸附最多”。Cr-MIL-101 is a porous framework of chromium (Cr) nodes and organic linkers. Functionalization adds chemical groups. This can introduce ion-binding environments while occupying pore space, so the largest area need not give the most uptake.

PMIDAPMIDA

含羧酸、膦酸和氮的有机分子，本研究把它接到含氨基的连接体上。名称表示分子身份，不是证明每个部位都已结合 Gd。An organic molecule containing carboxylic-acid, phosphonic-acid and nitrogen groups, attached here to amino-bearing linkers. Its name identifies the molecule, not proof that every group binds Gd.

ED 与 DETAED and DETA

分别为乙二胺和二乙烯三胺，含可参与结合的氮。本研究的接入路线与 PMIDA 不同：利用脱水后的 Cr 位点。Ethylenediamine and diethylenetriamine contain nitrogen that can participate in binding. Their introduction here uses dehydrated Cr sites, a different route from PMIDA.

BET 与 qₑBET and qₑ

BET 是由气体吸附估计的每克可达表面积；qₑ 是平衡时每克吸附剂上的目标质量。前者用 m²/g，后者用 mg/g，测量对象不同。BET estimates gas-accessible area per gram; qₑ is target mass per gram of sorbent at equilibrium. Their units, m²/g and mg/g, describe different objects.

读完应能解释为什么增加结合能力与减少孔面积可同时发生，并用表 2 核对“最好”的材料是否随稀土改变。You should explain how stronger uptake and reduced pore area can coexist, and use Table 2 to check whether the best material changes with the REE.

把配位基团引入 MOF，可能减少气体可及孔隙，却提高稀土吸附。真正要比较的是哪些离子、哪一种容量，以及功能基团能否经受酸再生。Adding binding groups to a MOF can reduce gas-accessible porosity while improving rare-earth adsorption. The useful comparison depends on the ion, the capacity metric and the survival of the functional group during acid regeneration.

作者制备原始 Cr-MIL-101 及四种功能化材料，比较 La、Ce、Nd、Sm、Gd 的水相吸附，再以 Gd 为重点研究 pH、等温线、动力学、混合离子竞争和五次吸附–酸再生。The authors prepare pristine Cr-MIL-101 and four functionalized materials, compare aqueous adsorption of La, Ce, Nd, Sm and Gd, then study Gd in detail through pH tests, isotherms, kinetics, ion competition and five adsorption–acid-regeneration cycles.

表面积不是唯一变量Surface area is one variable

MIL-101 的 BET 为 3680 m²/g；PMIDA 材料降至 989 m²/g，但 100 mg/L Gd 实验中的容量由 16.5 增至 87.7 mg/g。BET area falls from 3680 m²/g for MIL-101 to 989 m²/g after PMIDA modification, while Gd uptake at 100 mg/L initial concentration rises from 16.5 to 87.7 mg/g.

87.7 与 90.0 的区别87.7 and 90.0 mean different things

87.7 mg/g 是表 2 在给定条件下的吸附量；90.0 mg/g 是表 3 的 Langmuir 拟合上限 qₘ。两者不可互换。87.7 mg/g is the uptake in Table 2 under stated conditions; 90.0 mg/g is the Langmuir fitted maximum qₘ in Table 3. They are not interchangeable.

“选择性”需要分母Selectivity needs a denominator

约 90% 指 Gd 在已吸附离子总量中的份额；竞争者是 Al、Fe、Ni、Zn、Co，不是其他稀土。About 90% is Gd’s share of the adsorbed ion amount. Competitors are Al, Fe, Ni, Zn and Co, rather than other rare-earth elements.

机制 · 这些步骤为什么不能省略Mechanism · Why these steps matter

两条接入路线，为何不能合画成同一种结构？Why must the attachment routes remain distinct?

  1. 先确定支撑框架Identify the support

    母体是 Cr 节点与 BDC 连接体；含氨基版本从氨基化连接体制备，为下一步提供接枝位置。The parent contains Cr nodes and BDC linkers. An amino-bearing version is prepared with aminated linkers to provide attachment positions for the next step.

  2. 在连接体上接 PMIDAAttach PMIDA to linkers

    DCC 是促成连接反应的试剂，帮助氨基与 PMIDA 的羧酸形成酰胺键。参与连接的部位与剩余可参与吸附的基团需要区分。DCC is a coupling reagent that helps an amino group react with a PMIDA carboxylic acid to form an amide bond. Distinguish the attachment group from remaining groups available for adsorption.

  3. 另一路在 Cr 位点引入胺Introduce amines through Cr sites by another route

    ED/DETA 用脱水 Cr 位点接入。比较酸再生后行为时，要把这种连接方式与 PMIDA 的路线一起考虑。ED/DETA are introduced through dehydrated Cr sites. Their different attachment route matters when comparing behavior after acid regeneration.

流程解释制备关系，不是新测得的原子结构。光谱与元素分析支持功能化，但不唯一确定全部吸附 Gd 的局部几何。The flow explains preparation relationships, not a newly resolved atomic structure. Spectra and elemental analysis support functionalization without uniquely locating all adsorbed Gd geometries.

有机连接体上的功能化Functionalization on the organic linker

MIL-101-NH₂ 使用氨基化 BDC 连接体直接合成。随后以 DCC 促进其氨基与 PMIDA 的羧酸反应，形成共价酰胺连接。剩余含氧基团及未反应氨基参与后续吸附。MIL-101-NH₂ is synthesized with aminated BDC linkers. DCC-assisted coupling between its amino groups and a carboxylic acid group of PMIDA creates a covalent amide linkage. Remaining oxygen-containing groups and unreacted amino groups can participate in adsorption.

金属位点上的配位接枝Coordination grafting at metal sites

ED 和 DETA 接枝到脱水后开放的 Cr 位点。作者将其酸再生后的性能下降与胺质子化、Cr–胺连接受扰动联系起来。这与 PMIDA 的共价连接路径不同。ED and DETA are grafted to Cr sites opened by dehydration. The authors associate poorer acid-regeneration behaviour with amine protonation and disruption of Cr–amine attachment. This differs from the covalent PMIDA route.

SI 给出了原始 MIL-101、NH₂、ED 和 DETA 的制备与表征细节；PMIDA 的 DCC 偶联步骤见正文。上面的路径是来源化学关系的文字概括，不是原子坐标或完整结构图。The SI provides preparation and characterization details for pristine MIL-101, NH₂, ED and DETA; the main text describes DCC coupling of PMIDA. The route descriptions summarize the reported chemical relationships rather than atomic coordinates or a complete structural model.

用质量平衡理解 qₑUnderstand qₑ through mass balance

qₑ = (Cᵢ − Cₑ) × V / m

Cᵢ、Cₑ 分别为初始与平衡浓度（mg/L），V 为液体体积（L），m 为吸附剂质量（g）。本实验 V/m = 1 L/g，因此 100 mg/L 的单离子进料给出 100 mg/g 的质量平衡上限；它不等于材料在任意条件下的饱和容量。Cᵢ and Cₑ are initial and equilibrium concentrations in mg/L, V is solution volume in L and m is adsorbent mass in g. Here V/m = 1 L/g, so a 100 mg/L single-ion feed gives a mass-balance ceiling of 100 mg/g, rather than a universal saturation capacity.

约 90% 到底是什么？What exactly does about 90% mean?

混合进料中 Gd、Al、Fe、Ni、Zn、Co 各为 50 mg/L；15 mg 材料接触 15 mL 溶液，pH 5.5，6 h。论文图 10 报告的“选择性”约 90%，其定义是 Gd 的吸附量除以所有已吸附离子的总量。The mixed feed contains 50 mg/L each of Gd, Al, Fe, Ni, Zn and Co; 15 mg solid contacts 15 mL solution at pH 5.5 for 6 h. Figure 10 reports about 90% “selectivity,” defined as the adsorbed Gd amount divided by the total adsorbed ion amount.

这不是进料 Gd 的回收率，也不是再生液的产品纯度。由于没有其他稀土共同竞争，这一实验没有证明 Gd 能与相邻镧系元素高效分离。This is neither feed-Gd recovery nor the purity of a regenerated product. Because other rare earths are absent from the competitive mixture, this experiment does not establish efficient separation of Gd from neighbouring lanthanides.

作者以硬酸–硬碱相互作用、多种供体的协同作用及金属物种差异解释结果。pH 同时改变吸附剂质子化与溶液金属物种；pH 5.5 的竞争实验不能不经测试就外推到强酸废水或真实复杂浸出液。The authors interpret the result through hard-acid/hard-base interactions, donor synergy and metal-speciation differences. pH affects both protonation and aqueous speciation; the pH 5.5 test cannot establish performance in strongly acidic wastewater or a complex real leachate.

仍缺少什么证明？What remains unproven?

循环容量和 FTIR 不能定量排除所有配体流失、节点金属浸出或低于检测能力的结构变化。这里没有把这些测量扩展成完整质量平衡或长期寿命验证。Capacity cycling and FTIR do not quantitatively exclude all ligand loss, node-metal leaching or structural changes below detection. They are not a complete mass balance or a long-term lifetime test.
