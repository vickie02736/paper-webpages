功能团相遇孔隙：
两种载体怎样回收 Lu / Y？Functional groups meet pores:
two supports for Lu/Y recovery

同一组吸附数据可以被两种模型很好地拟合，却得到相差很大的容量极限。本文同时比较两种载体，并提醒我们区分观测范围、模型假设和混合物选择性。Two models can fit the same uptake data well yet give very different capacity limits. This study compares two supports while requiring us to distinguish measured range, model assumptions and mixture selectivity.

先搭起理解这篇论文的基础Build the foundations for this paper

载体、基团和孔道一起变，不能只归因于孔径Support, groups and pores change together—not just pore size

SBA-15 是多孔二氧化硅，MIL-101(Cr) 是含铬的金属–有机框架。它们分别引入 1,4-苯二甲酰二酰胺（1,4-PA，酰胺类）基团和含膦酸、羧酸的 PMIDA 相关基团来结合 Lu（镥）或 Y（钇）。两条路线不仅改变孔尺寸，也改变结合化学。SBA-15 is porous silica and MIL-101(Cr) is a chromium metal–organic framework. They receive 1,4-phthaloyl diamide (1,4-PA, amide-type) groups and PMIDA-related groups bearing phosphonic and carboxylic acids, respectively, to bind Lu (lutetium) or Y (yttrium). The routes differ in binding chemistry as well as pore dimensions.

等温线Isotherm

固定温度下，比较平衡液体浓度与每克固体的吸附量。不同浓度点约束曲线，但并不保证观测到了饱和平台。At fixed temperature, compare equilibrium liquid concentration with uptake per gram. Concentration points constrain a curve without necessarily reaching a saturation plateau.

拟合参数 QₘFitted Qₘ

模型推算的渐近容量极限。不同函数在观测区内相似，离开观测区后却可能分开，因此 Qₘ 不是自动测得的最大值。The model’s asymptotic capacity limit. Functions can agree within the measured range and diverge outside it, so Qₘ is not automatically a measured maximum.

传质Mass transfer

离子从主体液体移到颗粒表面，再进入内部的过程。表面液膜和颗粒内部可能分别限制到达结合位置的速度。Movement of ions from bulk liquid to the particle surface and into its interior. An external liquid film and intraparticle transport can separately limit access to binding sites.

读完应能解释为何“R² 都是 0.99”仍不足以选择容量最大的模型，也能说明为什么单离子 Lu/Y 容量比不是竞争分离因子。You should explain why R² = 0.99 for both fits is insufficient to choose the largest capacity, and why a single-ion Lu/Y capacity ratio is not a competitive separation factor.

读清单离子容量、模型外推与循环保持，才能评价材料比较。Evaluate the comparison through single-ion capacity, model extrapolation and cyclic retention.

论文把 1,4-PA 改性 SBA-15 与 PMIDA 改性 Cr-MIL-101 放在相同批次吸附条件下比较。MIL-101-PMIDA 的 Langmuir 拟合容量更高，且在所测时间序列中更快接近平衡。The paper compares 1,4-PA-functionalized SBA-15 and PMIDA-functionalized Cr-MIL-101 under shared batch conditions. MIL-101-PMIDA has higher fitted Langmuir capacities and approaches equilibrium faster in the tested time series.

这些是分别测 Lu 和 Y 的单离子实验。它们支持吸附表现比较；没有给出 Lu / Y 同时存在时的竞争分离因子。These are separate single-ion Lu and Y experiments. They support an uptake comparison without providing a competitive separation factor for a Lu/Y mixture.

两种改性路线，不是只改变孔径The two routes change more than pore size

SBA-15 是由 TEOS 与 P123 模板制得的介孔二氧化硅。先制备 1,4-PA-APTES，再在甲苯回流条件下接枝到经活化的 SBA-15，得到 1,4-PA-SBA。Cr-MIL-101 先使用氨基对苯二甲酸配体，再在 DMF 中用 PMIDA / DCC 后修饰，得到 MIL-101-PMIDA。SBA-15 is mesoporous silica prepared from TEOS with a P123 template. Prepared 1,4-PA-APTES is grafted to activated SBA-15 under toluene reflux to give 1,4-PA-SBA. The Cr-MIL-101 route first uses aminoterephthalate, then PMIDA/DCC post-functionalization in DMF to give MIL-101-PMIDA.

作者将 MIL-101 的表现与更大的 BET 面积、孔体积及含磷酸基、羧基等功能团联系。这是多项差异共同存在的比较，不能由它独立分离“孔径贡献”或断言单一结合机理。The authors relate MIL-101 performance to greater BET area, pore volume and functional groups including phosphonic/carboxylic groups. Multiple properties change together, so the comparison does not isolate a pore-size contribution or establish one binding mechanism.

主要制备条件Main preparation conditions

SBA-15：35 °C 20 h 后 100 °C 一天，550 °C 煅烧 3 h；改性前 150 °C 过夜活化，甲苯回流 24 h。MIL-101-NH₂：150 °C 24 h；后修饰中材料 / PMIDA / DCC 摩尔比写为 1:1.5:2，80 mL DMF、150 °C、48 h，100 °C 干燥 12 h。SBA-15: 35 °C for 20 h, then 100 °C for one day and calcination at 550 °C for 3 h; activation overnight at 150 °C and grafting under toluene reflux for 24 h. MIL-101-NH₂: 150 °C for 24 h; modification reports material/PMIDA/DCC = 1:1.5:2 in 80 mL DMF at 150 °C for 48 h, then drying at 100 °C for 12 h.

表 1：表面积、体积与孔径不是一个量Table 1: area, volume and diameter differ

SBA-15 改性后，BET 面积、孔体积和平均孔径均下降；MIL-101-PMIDA 面积较大、平均孔径较小。氮吸附在 77 K 测量；BET 与 BJH 描述气体可及孔隙，不直接计数水中可用配位位点。After SBA-15 functionalization, BET area, pore volume and mean pore diameter decrease. MIL-101-PMIDA has greater area and a smaller mean diameter. Nitrogen sorption at 77 K, BET and BJH describe gas-accessible porosity without directly counting binding sites available in water.

Qₑ = V(Cᵢ − Cₑ)/m

本式中 V 用 L，浓度用 mg/L，m 用 g，得到 mg/g。对任何一个有限进料，吸附量还受到初始总金属质量约束；拟合的 Qm 是模型渐近参数，不能直接代入每一个有限浓度批次作为实测量。Here V is in L, concentrations in mg/L and m in g, yielding mg/g. In any finite feed, uptake is constrained by the initial metal inventory. A fitted Qm is an asymptotic model parameter, not measured uptake to insert into every finite-concentration batch.

Sips：同一对象为 330.4 mg/gSips: the same system gives 330.4 mg/g

Sips 增加描述非均一性的参数，也可在有限数据范围内拟合良好。大的远端极限不能凭 R² = 0.99 变成已观测容量。Sips adds a heterogeneity parameter and can fit a limited range well. A large distant limit does not become observed capacity because R² = 0.99.

R² 描述模型对观测数据变化的贴合程度，不是“结论有 99% 概率正确”。原始初始浓度范围为 2–40 mg/L；下方 Cₑ 超过 40 mg/L 时一定已超出这些实验，低于它也不表示每个滑块点都实测过。R² describes fit to variation in observed data, not a 99% probability that the conclusion is correct. Initial concentrations were 2–40 mg/L. A plotted Cₑ above 40 mg/L is necessarily beyond these tests; lower slider values are not all measured points either.

机制 · 这些步骤为什么不能省略Mechanism · Why these steps matter

传质模型把哪些步骤分开了？Which steps does the transport model separate?

  1. 从液体移到外表面Move from liquid to the outer surface

    颗粒外面存在浓度变化区，可用液膜传质系数描述。模型需要液体浓度与表面浓度的差作为驱动力。A concentration gradient near the particle is represented by an external-film transfer coefficient. The model uses the difference between bulk and surface concentrations as a driving force.

  2. 局部吸附与孔内移动Local adsorption and intraparticle movement

    表面扩散模型（SDM）假设表面吸附很快达到局部平衡，再描述已吸附离子沿颗粒内表面的移动。它使用等效球形颗粒等简化。The surface diffusion model (SDM) assumes rapid local adsorption equilibrium, then describes adsorbed ions moving along internal surfaces. It uses simplifications including an equivalent spherical particle.

  3. 用时间数据约束参数Constrain parameters with time data

    作者数值求解并与浓度随时间的实验比较，估计传质参数。拟合支持模型在这些条件下描述数据，不证明所有微观步骤已经唯一确定。The authors solve the model numerically and compare concentration-time data to estimate transfer parameters. Fit quality supports description under those conditions, not a unique identification of every microscopic step.
