同样回收 Lu / Y，比较两种功能化载体Compare two functionalized supports for recovering Lu and Y

论文把 1,4-PA 改性 SBA-15 与 PMIDA 改性 Cr-MIL-101 放在相同批次吸附条件下比较。MIL-101-PMIDA 的 Langmuir 拟合容量更高，且在所测时间序列中更快接近平衡。The paper compares 1,4-PA-functionalized SBA-15 and PMIDA-functionalized Cr-MIL-101 under shared batch conditions. MIL-101-PMIDA has higher fitted Langmuir capacities and approaches equilibrium faster in the tested time series.

这些是分别测 Lu 和 Y 的单离子实验。它们支持吸附表现比较；没有给出 Lu / Y 同时存在时的竞争分离因子。These are separate single-ion Lu and Y experiments. They support an uptake comparison without providing a competitive separation factor for a Lu/Y mixture.

两种改性路线，不是只改变孔径The two routes change more than pore size

SBA-15 是由 TEOS 与 P123 模板制得的介孔二氧化硅。先制备 1,4-PA-APTES，再在甲苯回流条件下接枝到经活化的 SBA-15，得到 1,4-PA-SBA。Cr-MIL-101 先使用氨基对苯二甲酸配体，再在 DMF 中用 PMIDA / DCC 后修饰，得到 MIL-101-PMIDA。SBA-15 is mesoporous silica prepared from TEOS with a P123 template. Prepared 1,4-PA-APTES is grafted to activated SBA-15 under toluene reflux to give 1,4-PA-SBA. The Cr-MIL-101 route first uses aminoterephthalate, then PMIDA/DCC post-functionalization in DMF to give MIL-101-PMIDA.

作者将 MIL-101 的表现与更大的 BET 面积、孔体积及含磷酸基、羧基等功能团联系。这是多项差异共同存在的比较，不能由它独立分离“孔径贡献”或断言单一结合机理。The authors relate MIL-101 performance to greater BET area, pore volume and functional groups including phosphonic/carboxylic groups. Multiple properties change together, so the comparison does not isolate a pore-size contribution or establish one binding mechanism.

主要制备条件Main preparation conditions

SBA-15：35 °C 20 h 后 100 °C 一天，550 °C 煅烧 3 h；改性前 150 °C 过夜活化，甲苯回流 24 h。MIL-101-NH₂：150 °C 24 h；后修饰中材料 / PMIDA / DCC 摩尔比写为 1:1.5:2，80 mL DMF、150 °C、48 h，100 °C 干燥 12 h。SBA-15: 35 °C for 20 h, then 100 °C for one day and calcination at 550 °C for 3 h; activation overnight at 150 °C and grafting under toluene reflux for 24 h. MIL-101-NH₂: 150 °C for 24 h; modification reports material/PMIDA/DCC = 1:1.5:2 in 80 mL DMF at 150 °C for 48 h, then drying at 100 °C for 12 h.

表 1：表面积、体积与孔径不是一个量Table 1: area, volume and diameter differ

SBA-15 改性后，BET 面积、孔体积和平均孔径均下降；MIL-101-PMIDA 面积较大、平均孔径较小。氮吸附在 77 K 测量；BET 与 BJH 描述气体可及孔隙，不直接计数水中可用配位位点。After SBA-15 functionalization, BET area, pore volume and mean pore diameter decrease. MIL-101-PMIDA has greater area and a smaller mean diameter. Nitrogen sorption at 77 K, BET and BJH describe gas-accessible porosity without directly counting binding sites available in water.

衍射与尺寸描述的边界Boundaries of diffraction and size interpretation

图 2 中 SBA-15 的低角度峰经改性明显减弱，作者联系孔阻塞及孔壁变化；正文同时把 d100 从 88.1 到 92.3 Å 的变化称为“减小”，与数值方向不同。MIL-101 的特征峰与 FTIR 支持制备后的结构/官能团特征，不能替代循环后组成与浸出测量。SBA-15 low-angle peaks weaken after modification in Figure 2, which the authors discuss through pore blocking and wall changes. The prose calls d100 changing from 88.1 to 92.3 Å a decrease, although the numbers increase. MIL-101 peaks and FTIR support as-prepared structural/group features without replacing post-cycle composition and leaching measurements.

把浓度、剂量、时间留在结论里Keep concentration, dose and time with the conclusion

平衡实验：0.01 g 吸附剂 / 50 mL，即 0.2 g/L；分别使用 LuCl₃ 或 YCl₃，初始浓度 2–40 mg/L；25 ± 1 °C、pH 5.0 ± 0.2、振荡 24 h。动力学使用初始 5 与 10 mg/L、120 rpm、最长 24 h。Equilibrium tests use 0.01 g in 50 mL, or 0.2 g/L, with separate LuCl₃ or YCl₃ solutions initially 2–40 mg/L, at 25 ± 1 °C and pH 5.0 ± 0.2 for 24 h. Kinetics use initial 5 and 10 mg/L, 120 rpm and up to 24 h.

Qₑ = V(Cᵢ − Cₑ)/m

本式中 V 用 L，浓度用 mg/L，m 用 g，得到 mg/g。对任何一个有限进料，吸附量还受到初始总金属质量约束；拟合的 Qm 是模型渐近参数，不能直接代入每一个有限浓度批次作为实测量。Here V is in L, concentrations in mg/L and m in g, yielding mg/g. In any finite feed, uptake is constrained by the initial metal inventory. A fitted Qm is an asymptotic model parameter, not measured uptake to insert into every finite-concentration batch.

同一数据，拟合的容量可以相差很大Similar data can yield very different fitted capacities

表 2 的原始参数，统一 0–350 mg/g；均是相应模型的 Qm。SBA-15 两个 Sips Qm 很高，即使其 R² 同为 0.99，也不能当作已经观测到的饱和平台。Original Table 2 parameters on one 0–350 mg/g scale, all Qm of their corresponding model. The large SBA-15 Sips values are not observed saturation plateaus merely because the fits have R² = 0.99.

正文主推 Langmuir：Lu 的 63.4 / 17.0 ≈ 3.73 倍，Y 的 25.3 / 17.9 ≈ 1.41 倍。选择不同模型会改变“最大容量”的含义，所以比较材料时需要先指定模型、实验范围与拟合不确定性。The prose emphasizes Langmuir: Lu 63.4 / 17.0 ≈ 3.73-fold and Y 25.3 / 17.9 ≈ 1.41-fold. A different model changes the inferred maximum capacity, so comparisons need the model, concentration range and fit uncertainty.

mg/g 的 Lu / Y 比，不能直接当选择性A Lu/Y mass-capacity ratio is not selectivity

教学换算仅对 MIL-101-PMIDA 的 Langmuir 参数，使用近似摩尔质量 Lu = 175、Y = 88.9 g/mol。质量比约 2.506，摩尔比约 1.273；两种比都不是混合物的分配系数比。This teaching conversion uses only MIL-101-PMIDA Langmuir parameters and approximate molar masses Lu = 175 and Y = 88.9 g/mol. The mass ratio is about 2.506 and molar ratio about 1.273; neither is a mixture distribution-coefficient ratio.

传质模型比“拟二级”多了什么？What does a transport model add beyond a pseudo-order fit?

图 7 同时拟合拟一级、拟二级与表面扩散模型（SDM）。作者明确指出，拟一级/拟二级的好拟合本身不解释吸附机制。SDM 把外部液膜传质与颗粒内表面扩散分开，并用 Crank–Nicolson 方法求解。Figure 7 compares pseudo-first-order, pseudo-second-order and surface-diffusion models (SDM). The authors explicitly note that a good pseudo-order fit alone does not explain mechanism. SDM separates external-film transfer from intraparticle surface diffusion and is solved using Crank–Nicolson.

表 4 参数来自本研究 5 / 10 mg/L 的拟合，不是对任意粒径、流速或工业水质均适用的常数。Ds 是有效表面扩散系数，不是金属离子在自由水中的扩散系数。Table 4 parameters fit the tested 5/10 mg/L cases. They are not universal constants for any particle size, flow or industrial water. Ds is an effective surface-diffusion coefficient, not ion diffusivity in free water.

读动力学时的两个数值边界Two numerical cautions for kinetics

方法及图 7 的时间用 h，而拟一级/拟二级速率常数标为 s⁻¹ 或含 s 的单位；本页不据此生成新的速率曲线。表 4 的 MIL-101–Y、10 mg/L 拟二级 qe = 34.1 mg/g，也高于表 2 的 Langmuir Qm = 25.3 mg/g；不同系列与拟合结果分列，不拼成同一条精确等温线。Methods and Figure 7 use h, while pseudo-order rate constants are labeled with s-based units. No new kinetic curve is generated from this unresolved convention. Table 4 gives MIL-101–Y at 10 mg/L a pseudo-second-order qe of 34.1 mg/g, above Table 2’s Langmuir Qm of 25.3 mg/g. The series and fits remain separate instead of being merged into one exact isotherm.

五轮保留，支持到哪一步？What does five-run retention support?

用 0.1 M HCl 解吸 6 h，过滤后以 0.1 M NaOH 处理以恢复适合吸附的 pH，再于 100 °C 干燥，进入下一轮；循环实验初始 Lu 或 Y 为 10 mg/L。Strip with 0.1 M HCl for 6 h, filter, treat with 0.1 M NaOH to restore a suitable adsorption pH and dry at 100 °C before the next run. Cycling uses initial Lu or Y of 10 mg/L.

图 8 是相对于初始吸附表现归一化的循环保持，正文报告五轮均保持约 90% 以上。未把柱图未标注的精确高度编成逐轮数据，也不将它称为每轮从进料移除超过 90% 的金属。Figure 8 normalizes cyclic performance to initial uptake; the prose reports roughly more than 90% retention over five runs. Unlabeled heights are not fabricated as exact per-run data, nor described as removing over 90% of feed metal every cycle.

这里没有同时报告循环后的 Cr / PMIDA 浸出量、BET、PXRD 或官能团定量。循环功能保持支持可再用性，但不足以覆盖所有化学稳定性维度。Post-cycle Cr/PMIDA release, BET, PXRD and quantitative group retention are not jointly reported. Functional retention supports reuse while leaving other dimensions of chemical stability untested.

从批次回收走向竞争分离From batch capture toward competitive separation

还需要同一工作液中的 Lu / Y 竞争实验、盐和其他稀土干扰、真实废水、酸碱消耗与金属质量守恒、动态床层和长期浸出测试。本文没有用这些单离子拟合参数完成工业过程验证。Next tests need Lu/Y competition in one feed, salts and other REEs, actual wastewater, acid/base consumption and metal balances, dynamic beds and long-term leaching. These single-ion fitted parameters do not complete an industrial-process validation.

比较研究的实用价值在于：功能化 MOF 在这组批次条件下优于所选功能化二氧化硅；同时，孔隙、功能团、拟合模型与再生程序必须一起保留，才能把这个结果迁移到新的工作液。The practical result is that the functionalized MOF outperforms the selected functionalized silica in these batch tests. Transferring that result to a new feed requires retaining the porosity, groups, model choices and regeneration conditions.
